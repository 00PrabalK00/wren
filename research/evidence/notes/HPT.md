# HPT — Scaling Proprioceptive-Visual Learning with Heterogeneous Pre-trained Transformers (2024, arXiv 2409.20537, NeurIPS 2024)
Setup: modular policy = per-embodiment "stem" (proprio MLP + cross-attention to 16 learnable tokens; vision = FROZEN pretrained encoder, default ResNet-18 7x7 features, cross-attended to 16 tokens) → shared transformer "trunk" → per-task head (e.g. MLP), Huber loss on normalized action trajectories (regression, no generative head). Trunk sizes: Small 3.1M (16L, w128), Base 12.6M (16L, w256), Large 50.5M, XL 226.8M, Huge 1.1B. Pretrain default: 27 OXE datasets, <=1000 traj each (16k traj), batch 256, 80k iters; scaled: 52 datasets / 200–270k traj incl. sim + human video, batch 2048. Transfer: new stem + head, trunk frozen (default) or fine-tuned. Sim transfer: Meta-World, RoboMimic, Fleet-Tools, 20–100 traj/task, 50 episodes × 5 seeds; SIMPLER with ~50 traj. Real: 2 unnamed embodiments, 4 tasks (Sweep Leftover, Fill Water, Scoop Food, Switch Insertion), ~100 demos/task, 10 Hz, 1 vs 2 cameras, relative vs absolute pose actions, 15 trials/task; FT 20k iters, batch 256, LR 5e-6. Inference: RTX 3070 — 47 Hz HPT-Base, 19 Hz HPT-XL.
Claim: pretraining a shared trunk on heterogeneous proprio+vision data scales with data/model/compute (validation loss) and improves fine-tuned success by >20% on unseen tasks in sim and real.
Evidence:
 - Real Sweep Leftover (Table 3, ~100 demos, mean±sd): From Scratch No Prop 26.7±3.3; From Scratch 43.3±3.8; R3M 50.0±3.0; Voltron 46.7±3.8; VC-1 53.3±2.6; No-Prop Finetuned (pretrained without proprio) 63.3±2.6; HPT-B Finetuned 70.0±3.0; HPT-XL Finetuned 76.7±3.3.
 - Real 4 tasks (Fig. 12): pretrained > No-Trunk and From-Scratch (incl. Diffusion Policy as scratch baseline on Fill Water) — figure only.
 - Sim (Fig. 10a): HPT-B→XL pretrained trunks improve success over scratch/no-trunk; SIMPLER (Fig. 10b) HPT-B compared to Octo/RT-1-X/RT-2-X — figure only.
 - Trainable params at transfer can be ~2% (stem + head) with frozen encoders.
Ablations:
 - Proprioception: removing proprio from scratch 43.3 → 26.7; pretraining with proprio vs without (both finetuned) 70.0 vs 63.3.
 - Pretraining vs vision-only pretrained encoders (R3M/Voltron/VC-1: 46.7–53.3) < full-policy pretraining (70.0).
 - Trunk scale: HPT-XL 76.7 vs HPT-B 70.0 on Sweep Leftover (15-trial-scale evaluation; sd ~3).
 - Data scaling (Fig. 5, val loss only): loss improves with #traj if model/batch scale with it; fixed small model plateaus ~1000 traj/dataset; more datasets (10→52) lowers val loss on a fixed subset.
 - Model scaling (Fig. 7, val loss): 1M→1B improves until plateau; depth vs width no significant difference.
 - Batch size / epochs: larger batches help heterogeneous pretraining (variance reduction).
 - Sim + human video added to pretraining: possible, "complementary" (val loss, figure only).
Failure/limitations: Authors: success still typically <90%; short-horizon tasks only; simple dataset balancing; data filtering underexplored; heterogeneous pretraining converges slowly. Critical read: scaling claims are measured with validation LOSS, not success; real-robot numbers are 15 trials per task (Table 3 sd values look like std-err across seeds, unclear); embodiments/cameras for real tasks not specified in main text; appendix (failure modes, A.5 details) truncated in our copy; head is plain regression — no multimodality handling; qualitative claims of lighting/camera robustness (Fig. 11) not quantified.
Conflicts: Proprio helps (vs Octo App. E "proprio seemed worse, causal confusion" and RDT dropping proprio history). Plausible reconciliation: HPT uses only the current proprio through a small stem with frozen vision features, on short tasks where arm state is informative; Octo mixed heterogeneous delta-EEF datasets. Frozen ResNet-18 features + small trunk working at 100 demos agrees with Octo's finding that ResNets beat big ViTs from scratch at small data, and with DP-style small-model success; disagrees with OpenVLA's "fine-tuning vision encoder is crucial" (7B VLA regime).
Relevance: Most 8 GB-friendly generalist in this batch: HPT-Base trunk is 12.6M params + frozen ResNet-18, runs 47 Hz on an RTX 3070 laptop-class GPU; official code + a LeRobot fork exist (github liruiw/HPT, liruiw/lerobot). Setup mirrors ours: ~100 demos, 10 Hz, 1–2 cameras, absolute pose actions allowed, proprio input. Expected gain from pretraining at 100 demos: ~+27 pts over scratch in one task (43→70), smaller over VC-1 (+17). But regression head (Huber) may hedge on multimodal human demos; consider swapping in a diffusion/flow head on top of the pretrained trunk.
Decision impact:
 - Q03 vision encoder: frozen ImageNet ResNet-18 features + learned attention pooling to 16 tokens is sufficient with a pretrained trunk; full-policy pretraining > vision-only pretraining (70 vs 46.7–53.3) — confidence L/M (one task, 15 trials).
 - Q07 history/proprio: supports including current proprio (43.3 vs 26.7 from scratch) — confidence M.
 - Q12 model size: small trunk (12.6M) already useful; 226.8M adds +6.7 pts — confidence L.
 - Q13 data: cross-embodiment pretraining helps 100-demo fine-tunes (+27 pts vs scratch) — confidence L/M.
 - Q10 latency: 12.6M trunk 47 Hz on RTX 3070 — confidence H (direct measurement).
