# MDT — Multimodal Diffusion Transformer: Learning Versatile Behavior from Multimodal Goals (2024, arXiv 2407.05996)
Setup: sim CALVIN (ABCD→D 24 h play, D→D 6 h; 1% language labels; static 224×224 + gripper 84×84 cams; delta EEF, velocity-based) and LIBERO (5 suites, 50 demos/task, 2% language labels = 1 labeled demo/task, static + wrist cam); real Franka toy kitchen: 4.5 h play, 20 tasks, 360 labels (~20%), two STATIC cams (no wrist), 6 Hz, joint-position actions, leader-follower teleop. Model: transformer encoder (per-view ResNet-18 + spatial softmax + GroupNorm, 1 token/view; goals via frozen CLIP ViT-B/32 text / B/16 image, 1 token) + diffusion transformer decoder with cross-attention and AdaLN noise conditioning, EDM/Karras continuous-time, DDIM 10 steps, chunk 10; MDT 75.1M trainable, MDT-V (frozen Voltron + Perceiver) 40.0M. Current observation only (no history). Aux losses: MGF (MAE-style ViT decoder reconstructs masked patches of image v=3 steps in the future from the encoder latent; mask ratio 0.75; α=0.1) and CLA (InfoNCE between language-goal- and image-goal-conditioned state embeddings; β=0.1). Real: 4 GPUs ~24 h. Eval: CALVIN 1000 chains × 3 seeds; LIBERO 20 rollouts/task × 3 seeds; real 5 rollouts/task × 20 tasks.
Claim: a diffusion transformer policy with two cheap self-supervised aux losses (future-frame masked reconstruction, cross-goal contrastive alignment) learns from sparsely-labeled play data and sets CALVIN SOTA without large pretraining.
Evidence:
 - CALVIN ABCD→D avg len (Table I): MDT-V 4.52, MDT 4.41, RoboFlamingo (3B) 4.09, Distill-D 3.16, HULC 3.06, MT-ACT 2.80. D→D: MDT-V 3.72, MDT 3.59, MT-ACT 2.98, Distill-D 2.97, LAD 2.88, HULC 2.68.
 - LIBERO (Table II, 2% labels, avg over 5 suites): MDT no aux 68.5; +CLA 73.1; +MGF 70.0; +both 74.3 (per-suite with both: Spatial 78.5, Object 87.5, Goal 73.5, Long 64.8, 90 67.2). Distill-D (U-Net DP, CLIP) avg 56.0. Transformer-BC with 100% labels: Spatial 71.8, Object 71.0, Goal 76.3, Long 24.2.
 - Real kitchen single-task (Table III, 100 rollouts each): MT-ACT 0.25, MDT 0.51, MDT+MGF+CLA 0.58. Long-horizon avg len (lang goals): 0.81 / 1.38 / 1.56; image goals: 0.13 / 0.38 / 0.56.
Ablations:
 - Aux losses on CALVIN ABCD (Table IX): MDT-V none 4.18, +CLA 4.32, +MGF 4.48, both 4.52. MDT (ResNet): none 3.34 → both 4.41 (+32%). With goal images via scratch ResNets instead of CLIP: 3.34 / 4.31.
 - MGF transfers to another policy (Table XI): MT-ACT 2.80 → 4.03 with MGF (+44%), no other change.
 - MGF mask ratio (Table VIII): CALVIN D 0.5: 3.70, 0.75: 3.72, 1.0 (full future-image reconstruction): 3.68; LIBERO-Spatial 67.8 / 67.5 / 63.7 → masked partial reconstruction ≥ full reconstruction.
 - Foresight distance (Table X): v=1 4.50 / 64.4; v=3 4.52 / 67.5; v=9 4.44 / 65.6 (CALVIN ABCD / LIBERO-Spatial) — short horizon (~3 steps) best, far horizon more variance.
 - Small-data (Fig 5, LIBERO-Long, 10% labels; values read from figure, mapping approximate): 5 demos: scratch ≈0.14, scratch+aux ≈0.18, pretrained (MGF+CLA on action-free LIBERO-90 video) ≈0.25, pretrained+aux ≈0.29; 20 demos: ≈0.52 / 0.53 / 0.64 / 0.65. Text: pretraining +100% at 5 demos, +25% at 20 demos; aux losses help from scratch too (small at 20 demos).
 - Architecture: no encoder (plain DP-style diffusion transformer): 1.41; noise-as-token instead of AdaLN: 3.58 vs 4.18.
 - MT-ACT with FiLM-ResNets performed poorly with image goals; pretrained Voltron encoders hurt MT-ACT/Distill-D (>20% drop).
Checkpoint selection (App. F): real robot MDT checkpoints chosen by lowest validation loss; MT-ACT used LAST epoch "since … performance improv[ed] even when the validation loss began to rise again." — implicit evidence that val loss is unreliable for ACT-style policies.
Failure/limitations (authors): aux losses do not help LIBERO-Long (single high-level instruction); impact varies by benchmark (CLA larger on LIBERO, MGF larger on CALVIN); diffusion is slower; real long-horizon lengths short; self-occlusion without wrist camera. My read: real results = 5 rollouts/task, no seeds; the aux-loss gain is largest when goals are multimodal/sparsely labeled — for single-task fully-labeled data the CLA term is irrelevant and MGF gain is untested. Model is 75M trainable plus MGF ViT decoder at train time.
Conflicts: agrees with Seer/VPP/FLARE that future-prediction signals help; unlike Seer/VPP it only needs a small masked-patch decoder (no video model) and adds zero inference cost. Disagrees with FLARE's claim that pixel reconstruction is unnecessary — MDT's masked partial reconstruction works; full reconstruction (mask 1.0) was worse, which is consistent with FLARE's argument that full pixel prediction wastes capacity.
Relevance: For SO-101 single-task pick-place with 50–100 demos, MGF is a cheap train-only add-on to an ACT/DP-style transformer (MT-ACT +44% on CALVIN); expected gain at our scale is uncertain (LIBERO-Long 20-demo from-scratch: ≈+1 pt). Current-obs-only + chunk 10 works. For a later 2-object language task: frozen CLIP text token as one encoder token is enough; CLA only useful if mixing image and language goals.
Decision impact:
 - Q09 aux objectives: supports masked future-frame reconstruction (MGF, v≈3, mask 0.75) as train-only aux loss — CALVIN MT-ACT 2.80→4.03, MDT 3.34→4.41, LIBERO +1.5 avg (MGF alone) — confidence M (big on play data; small at ≤20 demos from scratch).
 - Q09 aux objectives: weakens full-image future reconstruction vs masked (LIBERO-Spatial 63.7 vs 67.5) and long foresight (v=9) — confidence L/M.
 - Q08 language: supports frozen CLIP text embedding as a single conditioning token + contrastive alignment when goals are sparse/multimodal (LIBERO avg 68.5→73.1 with CLA) — confidence M.
 - Q07 history: current observation only is sufficient for SOTA on CALVIN/LIBERO with chunk 10 — confidence M.
 - Q13 data / pretraining: action-free video pretraining with MGF+CLA doubles 5-demo success (≈0.14→0.29) — confidence L/M (figure, 1 suite).
 - eval/checkpoint: ACT-style policy kept improving after val loss rose (authors' practice: last epoch) — confidence L.
