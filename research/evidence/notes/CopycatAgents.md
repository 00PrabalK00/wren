# CopycatAgents — Fighting Copycat Agents in Behavioral Cloning from Observation Histories (2020, arXiv 2010.14876)
Setup: sim only, 6 OpenAI-Gym MuJoCo locomotion/reach tasks (Ant, Hopper, Humanoid, Reacher, Walker2d, HalfCheetah) made partially observed by giving joint positions only (no velocities/forces); TRPO expert data (1k transitions for HalfCheetah/Reacher/Ant, 20k Hopper/Walker2d, 200k Humanoid); state-based MLPs (E 4-layer MLP, F and D 2-layer MLPs, 300 units); history H=2 for BC-OH; 300k iters, batch 64; 5 seeds, reward averaged over 5 late checkpoints. No images, no manipulation, no real robot.
Claim: with observation history, BC learns to "copy" the previous expert action (a nuisance correlate recoverable from o_{t-1},o_t); a target-conditioned adversary (TCA) + information bottleneck (IB) removes this shortcut and improves reward.
Evidence (Table 2, cumulative reward, mean±std over 5 seeds; same data, same MLP sizes):
 - BC-SO (H=1) vs BC-OH (H=2) vs Ours: Ant 1300 / 1750 / 2150; Hopper 275 / 293 / 1086; Humanoid 587 / 565 / 671; Reacher −79 / −64 / −54; Walker2d 363 / 592 / 1296; HalfCheetah −38 / 820 / 1250. Expert: 2348 / 1780 / 4963 / −10 / 2428 / 1336.
 - History helps over single frame in 5/6 envs here (these envs are genuinely partially observed: no velocity), but BC-OH still far below expert.
 - RNN history (BC-OH RNN): Ant −311, Walker2d 190 — worse than feedforward stacking in most envs.
 - Dropout on history frames (Dropout-BC): uniformly poor (Ant 830, Hopper 223, HalfCheetah 406).
 - DAGGER 100 queries ≈ Ours; DAGGER 1k queries better (Walker2d 2170, Reacher −15).
 - Smoking gun (Table 1): next action is MORE predictable from past actions for BC-OH rollouts than for expert rollouts in all 6 envs (e.g. Humanoid MSE 0.18 vs 6.93 ×10^-1); predictability inversely correlated with normalized reward (Fig 3, figure only).
 - Held-out loss vs reward (App. Table 7): BC-OH has LOWER test loss than BC-SO (e.g. Walker2d 0.0136 vs 0.0410; HalfCheetah 0.0309 vs 0.1225) yet sometimes lower reward (Humanoid); Ours has HIGHER test loss than BC-OH (Walker2d 0.0229, Hopper 0.0087 vs 0.0049) but much higher reward. Table 4 (Hopper): next-action test MSE BC-OH 0.89e-3 vs TCA 9.48e-3, yet TCA gets best reward.
Ablations: w/o adversary (IB only) already beats BC-OH (Walker2d 962, HalfCheetah 1260, Hopper 473); w/o TCA (unconditioned adversary) worst ablation in 4/6 (removes useful info); w/o IB (TCA only) close to full (Hopper 683 vs 1086; Walker2d 929 vs 1296). TCA makes a_{t-1} no easier to predict from features than from a_t alone (Table 3: 5.97e-3 vs 5.00e-3; BC-OH 0.38e-3).
Failure/limitations: authors state improvement in image-based experiments "is not significant yet". Low-dim state, locomotion, frame skip 1 (very high temporal correlation), single-step actions (no chunking). H=2 only. Not manipulation.
Conflicts: agrees with BAKU/robomimic-style reports that naive history often doesn't help, and gives the mechanism. Diffusion Policy/ACT sidestep copycat via action chunking (predicting a sequence of future actions rather than a single next action) and short (1–2 frame) obs history; the copycat shortcut is weaker when the target is a multi-step chunk and when proprio (which already gives state) is included. Here history HELPS over H=1 because velocities are removed — in our setting joint positions are given and quasi-static pick-place is nearly Markov from image+proprio.
Relevance: SO-101 at 30 fps would make consecutive actions even more correlated (copycat risk ↑); leader-follower teleop is smooth → strong action autocorrelation. Key transferable lesson: lower validation loss with history is NOT evidence it will work on the robot. Also: do not feed past actions as input.
Decision impact:
 - Q07 history: supports current obs only (or ≤2 frames + chunked targets) and NOT feeding past actions — confidence M (mechanism clear; evidence is sim state-based locomotion, not visual manipulation).
 - Q07 history: if history is needed, IB/bottleneck on history features helps; RNN and dropout-on-history don't — confidence L (MuJoCo only).
 - Q13/eval (checkpoint selection): weakens using held-out action loss to choose history/no-history or checkpoints — lower test loss coexisted with lower reward — confidence M.
