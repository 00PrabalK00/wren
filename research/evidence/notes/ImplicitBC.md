# ImplicitBC — Implicit Behavioral Cloning (2021, arXiv 2109.00137, CoRL)
Setup: Energy-based policy â = argmin_a E(o,a), trained with InfoNCE vs uniform counter-examples (256/sample) or Langevin MCMC + gradient penalty; inference by derivative-free optimisation (DFO: 16,384 samples × 3 iters sim; 1,024 samples × 3 iters real), autoregressive DFO for 3–12-D, Langevin for up to 30-D. Sim: D4RL human-expert (Kitchen, Adroit; 3 seeds × 100 evals), N-D particle (2,000 demos), xArm pushing (2,000 scripted), Planar Sweeping (50 HUMAN teleop demos, images), Bi-manual sweeping (1,000 scripted, 12-D). REAL: xArm6 + cylindrical pusher, 2-D planar delta-Cartesian actions, single RealSense D415 RGB 640x360 (downsized 90x120/…), policy 5 Hz, human teleop demos 95–502 per task, 4 tasks, 60 trials per method per task (3 seeds × 20). Explicit baseline = identical ConvMaxPool+MLP trunk with MSE (and MDN in sim). No action chunking; single-step.
Claim: implicit (EBM) BC beats otherwise-identical explicit MSE BC across sim and real, particularly for discontinuous, multi-valued, precise behaviour.
Evidence:
 - Real (Table 6, EBM vs MSE, success %): Push-Red-then-Green (95 demos) 85.0±5.0 vs 35.0±18.0; Push-Red/Green-Multimodal (410 demos) 88.3±7.6 vs 55.0±18.0; Insert-Blue 1 mm tolerance (223 demos) 83.3±3.8 vs 6.7±9.4; Sort-Blue-from-Yellow (502 demos) 48.3±4.6 vs 19.6±1.5. Note high seed variance of MSE (±18).
 - Sim pushing (Table 3; 2,000 scripted demos): single target states EBM 100 / MDN 100 / MSE 98.3; multi-target states 99.0 / 99.7 / 89.7; single target PIXELS 100 / MDN 10.0±4.3 / MSE 87.0.
 - Planar sweeping, 50 human demos, images (Table 4): best EBM (image+spatial softmax, 20 ResNet layers) 82.6 vs best MSE (image+pool) 75.6 (≈+7%); both ~28–29 from state.
 - Bi-manual sweeping 12-D (Table 5): EBM 78.2 vs MSE 63.9.
 - N-D particle: EBM trains to 95% success up to 16-D, MSE only up to 8-D (same 2,000 demos).
 - Toy coordinate regression: with 10 training images, ConvMLP-EBM gets 1–2 orders of magnitude lower test MSE than MSE-trained ConvMLP (figure; interpolation/extrapolation better at low data).
 - D4RL human: both their MSE-BC and EBM far above published BC; EBM better on average (per-cell numbers poorly extracted; not cited).
Ablations: EBM variant (App. B.4): plain DFO fails beyond ~5-D action; autoregressive DFO and Langevin reach 16-D (nonzero at 32-D). Encoder: EBM pairs best with spatial softmax, MSE with average pooling. Top-50%-return data filtering helps EBM more than MSE ("implicit BC values data quality more").
Compute: real-robot inference EBM 7.22 ms vs MSE 3.49 ms (RTX 2080 Ti, 1,024 samples × 3 iters; 2,048 samples → 9.25 ms); training 5.0 h vs 5.8 h on 8×V100. D4RL Langevin EBM 5.6× train+eval time of MSE.
Failure/limitations: authors: more compute in train/inference; DFO curse of dimensionality (>5-D); Langevin needs gradient penalties/spectral norm for stability. Critical read: real tasks are 2-D planar pushing — the action space is tiny, which is exactly where DFO works; not a 6-DoF arm + gripper with 1–2 s chunks (a chunk of 6-D × 10–30 steps is 60–180-D, out of reach for DFO; Langevin EBMs are notoriously unstable). Later papers (BeT: IBC overfits, needs early stopping, 14 h training; Diffusion Policy: IBC unstable and weak on real) failed to reproduce its strength. Explicit baseline is single-step MSE without chunking or history-heavy design — the modern regression baseline (chunked L1 transformer, ACT-style) is much stronger.
Conflicts: agrees with BeT/ACT/DP that plain single-step MSE is weak on human, multimodal/discontinuous data. Disagrees with BeT/DP about IBC's practical reliability (BeT: IBC 0.25 on CARLA, overfits; DP reports IBC well below DP). Why: IBC's authors tuned per task with extensive sweeps; low-D actions.
Relevance: For SO-101 (6 joints + gripper, chunked actions) an EBM head is impractical (dimensionality, training instability). The transferable lessons: (1) MSE heads blur discontinuities → failure at precise contact (insertion 6.7% vs 83%), relevant to the grasp/release moments of pick-place; (2) 95–500 human demos per task was their regime, with 5 Hz control; (3) spatial-softmax keypoint encoders pair well with multimodal heads.
Decision impact:
 - Q01 action head: single-step MSE regression — WEAKENED on real human demos (35/55/7/20% vs 85/88/83/48%) — confidence M/H (60 trials/task, real, but 2-D planar, no chunking).
 - Q01 action head: EBM/implicit — WEAKENED for our 6-DoF chunked setting (DFO fails >5-D; others report instability) — confidence M.
 - Q01 action head: MDN/GMM — WEAKENED with pixels (10% vs 87–100%) — confidence L/M (single sim task).
 - Q12/Q13 small data: implicit/multimodal heads interpolate/extrapolate better at 10–50 examples (toy + 50-demo sweeping +7%) — confidence L.
