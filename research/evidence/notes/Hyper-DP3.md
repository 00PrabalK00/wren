# Hyper-DP3 — Hyper-DP3: Frequency-Aware Right-Sizing of 3D Diffusion Policies for Visuomotor Control (2026, arXiv 2605.01581)
Setup: Sim: RoboTwin2.0 (50 dual-arm tasks, 50 demos, 100 eval scenes/task), Adroit + MetaWorld (10 tasks, 10 demos, 3 seeds, SR5 = mean of top-5 evals, 20 rollouts per eval). Real: AgileX Piper 6-DoF arm, ONE global Intel RealSense D455 (stereo depth), 50 leader-follower teleop demos/task, 6-DoF joint-position actions, 3 tasks, 15 trials/task, inference on RTX 4060. Model: DP3 point encoder (FPS downsample → MLP → max-pool, 64-dim output) + robot state added (C = z + Linear(s)); decoder = 6 "DiM" MLP-Mixer blocks (hidden 128) with FiLM + gated residual; x0-prediction; DDIM 100 train steps, 2 inference steps. 2.52 M params total. Horizon 8 (RoboTwin)/16 (Adroit, MetaWorld); execute 6/8; obs steps 3/2. Trained 3000 epochs on RTX 5880 Ada.
Claim: robot action chunks are extremely low-frequency (first 2 DCT modes = 98.5% of energy, k=1 alone 93.2%, RoboTwin2.0, T=32), so a tiny decoder and 2 denoising steps suffice; HDP3 matches/exceeds DP3 with <1% of the parameters and ~11× lower latency.
Evidence:
 - Params / NFE / latency (RTX 5880, batch 1): DP 85.6M/100/460 ms; DP3 255.8M/10/51.4 ms; FlowPolicy 255.8M/1/7.04 ms; MP1 255.8M/1/7.02 ms; HDP3 2.52M/2/4.50 ms.
 - RoboTwin2.0 avg (50 tasks): DP 28.0, DP3 55.2, FlowPolicy 41.1 (text says 40.9), MP1 55.2, HDP3 63.2.
 - Adroit+MetaWorld avg (10 tasks, 10 demos): BCRNN 8.8, IBC 2.0, DP 30.0, DP3 73.0, FlowPolicy 72.6, MP1 76.7, HDP3 78.4.
 - Real (Piper + D455, 50 demos, 15 trials): Place Object DP3 53.3 → HDP3 73.3; Adjust Bottle 33.3 → 46.7; Stack Blocks Two 6.7 → 20.
Ablations:
 - Decoder at equal ~2.5M params (Adroit Door/Hammer/Pen SR5): HDP3 54.3/100/48.7 (avg 67.7); vanilla MLP+FiLM 0/0/0 (did not train); shrunk U-Net (2.64M) 25.7/93.3/42.0 (avg 53.7) → residual + temporal mixing matter; a naively shrunk U-Net loses ~14 pts.
 - NFE (final checkpoint, 5000 episodes/task, Door/Hammer/Pen avg): HDP3 1 step 53.0, 2 steps 58.2, 5 steps 57.7, 10 steps 58.0. DP3 1 step 57.5, 2 steps 59.3, 5 steps 59.3, 10 steps 59.9.
 - Synthetic: low-frequency-dominant trajectories saturate in ~2 reverse steps; broadband need many (figure only, qualitative).
Failure/limitations: authors: only validated for 3D inputs; theory is approximate (Gaussian assumption). Critical read: (1) in the careful 5000-episode NFE test, the full DP3 is actually slightly BETTER than HDP3 at every NFE (59.3 vs 58.2 at 2 steps) — the "SOTA" claims rest on SR5 (top-5-of-many evals, optimistic) and on RoboTwin single-seed; the defensible result is "~same performance at 1/100 the size". (2) DP3 itself needs only 2 DDIM steps too (59.3 vs 59.9 at 10) — few-step sampling is a property of smooth action data, not of the new decoder. (3) Real: 15 trials × 3 tasks, no OOD tests, point-cloud preprocessing (crop/segmentation/calibration) not described in extracted text. (4) 2026 preprint, not peer reviewed as far as the text shows.
Conflicts: agrees with DP3 that a tiny MLP point encoder suffices; extends it to the decoder. Consistent with RoboVLMs finding that the action objective (flow vs MSE) matters little and with the general "few denoising steps suffice" observations (consistency/flow policies). Contrasts with 3DDiffuserActor's view that pooled 1D point embeddings are too weak — note HDP3 tasks are single-task, uncluttered benchmarks, where pooling is fine.
Relevance: Very close to our hardware: 6-DoF low-cost arm with leader-follower teleop, joint-position actions, 50 demos, single global D455 (stereo, like ours), consumer RTX 4060 GPU. Shows a DP3-style point-cloud policy works with STEREO depth on such a setup (absolute success modest: 20–73%). 2.5M params / 4.5 ms is trivially real-time on 8 GB → removes latency (Q10) as a blocker for a 3D branch and leaves budget for a 2D branch. Frequency argument also supports temporal smoothness at chunk boundaries if chunks are generated with few steps.
Decision impact:
 - Q04 3D input: point-cloud diffusion policy works on a Piper + single D455 with 50 demos (real 20–73%, beats DP3 by 13–20 pts) — M (15 trials, 3 tasks, no OOD).
 - Q04 noise/stereo: stereo D455 depth is usable for DP3-style policies — L/M (no explicit noise analysis).
 - Q12 model size: 2.52M-param policy ≈ 255.8M DP3 (58.2 vs 59.3, 5000 eps) and beats it on RoboTwin (63.2 vs 55.2) — M/H (strong evidence that big decoders are unnecessary in low data).
 - Q01 action head: diffusion with 2 DDIM steps is enough; 1 step drops ~5 pts — M/H (5000-episode eval).
 - Q10 latency: 4.5 ms inference for 2 steps at 2.5M params — H (measured).
 - Q06 horizon: 8–16 step prediction, execute 6–8 — L (not ablated).
