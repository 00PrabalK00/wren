# VC-1 — Where are we in the search for an Artificial Visual Cortex for Embodied Intelligence? (2023, arXiv 2303.18240)
Setup: CortexBench = 17 sim tasks / 7 benchmarks (Adroit 2, MetaWorld 5, DMControl 5, TriFinger 2, ObjectNav, ImageNav, MobilePick). The few-shot IL suites use a FROZEN encoder, CLS token (ViT) or pooled features (ResNet), frame stacking and a 3-layer MLP BC policy with 100 demos (Adroit, DMC, TriFinger) or 25 demos (MW); 100 epochs, best checkpoint. MAE ViT-B (86M) / ViT-L (307M) pretrained on Ego4D and combinations of manipulation (M), navigation (N) and ImageNet (I) data, up to 5.6M frames; VC-1 = ViT-L on Ego4D+MNI (>10,000 GPU-h in total). Hardware: TriFinger push-cube (30 demos, 12 trials); Franka, 4 tasks (reach 30 demos, bottle pickup 50, drawer 250, toaster 250; 10 trials each; 420×224 RGB + proprio, absolute joint targets, MLP).
Claim: no PVR is universally best; scaling data and model helps only on average; adaptation (E2E fine-tuning or continued MAE on in-domain frames) is needed to reach SOTA.
Evidence:
 - Frozen PVRs (Table 2, mean success over 7 benchmarks): MVP-L 67.5; MVP-B 62.4; R3M 58.0; CLIP-B 57.0; VIP 52.3; random ViT-B fine-tuned 47.4; random frozen ViT-B/L 20.4/22.1. By benchmark, R3M is best on Adroit 73.3, MW 96.0 and DMC 81.1 but worst-in-class on ObjectNav 22.7 / ImageNav 30.6.
 - Scaling (Table 4, mean): Ego4D ViT-B/L 62.2/63.5 → +M 62.8/64.4 → +N 63.8/67.1 → +MN 64.1/67.2 → +MNI 66.2/68.7 (VC-1). Diversity (adding navigation data or ImageNet) helps more than adding more manipulation frames; +800K frames with no new diversity gives ~0.
 - VC-1 frozen, by benchmark: Adroit 59.3, MW 88.8, DMC 66.9, TF 71.7 — WORSE than frozen R3M on all three MuJoCo manipulation suites despite being 5× larger and trained on more data.
 - Adaptation (Table 5), frozen → E2E fine-tuned → MAE-adapted: Adroit 59.3 → 15.9 → 72.0; MW 88.8 → 22.7 → 96.0; DMC 66.9 → 6.7 → 80.9; TF 71.7 → 70.9 → 80.6; ObjectNav 60.3 → 67.7 → 57.4; ImageNav 70.3 → 81.6 → 67.0; MobilePick 63.2 → 74.0 → 62.4.
 - Real Franka (Table 9, successes /10 for reach/bottle/drawer/toaster): VC-1 frozen 8/10/6/4 = 70%; VC-1 E2E fine-tuned (encoder LR 1e-5, policy 1e-3) 5/9/8/5 = 67.5%; VC-1 MAE-adapted 9/10/8/7 = 85%; in-domain MAE from scratch 3/1/5/5 = 35%; MVP 7/10/3/2 = 55%. TriFinger: frozen 45.8, E2E 52.7, MAE-adapted 43.5, MVP 31.9, in-domain MAE 22.9.
Ablations: ViT-B → ViT-L improves average performance and 15/17 tasks, but ViT-B beats ViT-L on MW and TF for Ego4D+MNI. MAE-adapt from random init (in-domain MAE) is surprisingly good on DMC/TF (77.6/80.4) but bad on real robots (35%). Attention maps (appendix): frozen MAE attends to contours/general features; E2E fine-tuning concentrates attention on task objects.
Failure/limitations: authors: sim proxies may not match real rankings; each benchmark needs its own adapted model. My read: the catastrophic E2E result is a 307M ViT-L + CLS token + MLP policy + ≤50K frames with no stated augmentation; it is not evidence against low-LR fine-tuning of small encoders (DP paper). Real tests are in-distribution, 10 trials/task (±15 pts noise); no lighting/texture/distractor evaluation anywhere. The CLS-token readout handicaps ViTs (Theia shows spatial tokens >> CLS).
Conflicts: DP paper: fine-tuned (10× lower LR) pretrained encoders are best → reconciled by model size and head: VC-1 fine-tunes ViT-L with an MLP on CLS and tiny data. On the real Franka with LR 1e-5, E2E ≈ frozen (67.5 vs 70), i.e. not a collapse. UnbiasedLookPVR: VC-1 is only marginally better than an Ego4D-only MAE on real robots (33 vs 28%) and ImageNet-MAE beats it (41%). WhatMakesPVRsRobust: MAE-family models (MVP) generalize worse OOD than DINO/MoCo. SPA: MAE strong frozen; VC-1 no better than ImageNet MAE.
Relevance: VC-1-L (307M) is too big to fine-tune on 8 GB at a useful batch size; VC-1-B is possible. The most transferable idea is MAE ADAPTATION: continue MAE pretraining on our own ~50–100 demos' frames (plus optionally unlabeled footage of the scene, pumpkins and lighting variants), then freeze → 85% vs 70% frozen on the real Franka. Cheap, and it can use unlabeled images from new lighting/objects, which suits our shift problem (untested OOD).
Decision impact:
 - Q03 vision: full E2E fine-tuning of a LARGE ViT on few demos can collapse (MW 88.8→22.7) — against fine-tuning large encoders, and in favour of a small encoder or a low LR — H (big consistent sim drops; real 1e-5 LR shows no collapse).
 - Q03 vision: self-supervised (MAE) adaptation on in-domain frames then freeze: real 70→85% — supports in-domain SSL adaptation — M (40 real trials).
 - Q03 vision: manipulation/egocentric pretraining (VC-1) is not better than R3M on manipulation and not universally best — weakens VC-1 as a pick — M.
 - Q12 model size: ViT-B ≥ ViT-L on some manipulation suites; larger helps only on average — ~ — L/M.
 - Q13 data: diversity of pretraining data > quantity (+ImageNet/+Nav > +800K frames) — supports diverse pretraining — M.
