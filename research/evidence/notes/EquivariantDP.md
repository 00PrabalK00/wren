# EquivariantDP — Equivariant Diffusion Policy (2024, arXiv 2407.01812)
Setup: Sim: 12 MimicGen tasks, 100/200/1000 demos, 3 seeds, metric = MAX success over 50 evaluations during training (optimistic). RGB version: agent-view + eye-in-hand 84×84 (2 cams); voxel version: 4×64³ grid (occupancy + RGB) fused from 4 calibrated cameras + eye-in-hand image. Real: Franka + fin-ray fingers, 3 RealSense D455 (stereo) fused into a 64³ voxel grid covering (0.4 m)³ (requires extrinsics), gripper state; 5 Hz; 6 tasks, 20–60 demos, 20 trials/task; random voxel crop 58³ (+ rotation aug in 2 tasks); DDIM 16 steps. Architecture: escnn SO(2)/C_u-equivariant encoders (equivariant ResNet for images, 8-layer equivariant 3D conv for voxels) → per-group-element 1D temporal U-Net ([256,512,1024]) → equivariant decoder; actions = SE(3) end-effector pose (6D rot + xyz + gripper) in world frame, absolute or relative; obs history 2 (RGB) / 1 (voxel), predict 16, execute 8.
Claim: building SO(2) (rotation about gravity axis) equivariance into the diffusion denoiser gives much better sample efficiency for full 6-DoF control.
Evidence:
 - Avg over 12 tasks, absolute control (100 / 200 / 1000 demos): EquiDiff-Voxel 63.9 / 72.6 / 77.9; EquiDiff-RGB 53.7 / 68.5 / 79.7; DiffPo-C (U-Net DP) 42.0 / 57.8 / 71.4; DiffPo-T 29.0 / 43.0 / 64.9; DP3 23.9 / 35.1 / 56.8; ACT 21.3 / 38.2 / 63.3.
 - Relative control: EquiDiff-Voxel 48.8 / 58.0 / 70.2; EquiDiff-RGB 35.4 / 50.4 / 74.0; DiffPo-C 33.3 / 47.3 / 63.2; BC-RNN 22.9 / 41.2 / 70.3.
 - EquiDiff-Voxel with 200 demos (72.6) > every baseline with 1000 demos (best 71.4).
 - Real (EquiDiff-Voxel vs DiffPo-C-Voxel, same voxel input, similar params, same aug; 20 trials): Oven Opening (20 demos) 95 vs 60; Banana in Bowl (40) 95 vs 30; Letter Alignment (40) 95 vs 0; Trash Sweeping (40) 90 vs 5; Hammer to Drawer (60) 85 vs 5; Bagel Baking (58) 80 vs 10.
 - Bagel: zero-shot generalization to 8 unseen oven rotations except where joint limits intervene (qualitative).
Ablations:
 - Remove equivariance vs remove voxels (avg 12 tasks, abs, 100/200/1000): full 63.9/72.6/77.9; no voxel (RGB equi) 53.7/68.5/79.7; no equi (voxel DP) 46.3/62.5/75.6; neither 42.0/57.8/71.4 → equivariance is the bigger factor; voxel 3D input alone gives only +4.3/+4.7/+4.2 over RGB DP.
 - Equivariance via rotation augmentation on a plain CNN (100 demos): voxel Equi-Net 63.9 / CNN+Aug 53.3 / CNN 46.3; RGB 53.7 / 46.2 / 42.0 → augmentation recovers roughly half the gain; CNN+Aug matches equi on easy tasks (Stack), fails on hard ones.
 - SE(2) instead of SE(3) actions (200 demos): Stack Three 77.3 vs 75.3 (≈), Threading 40.0 vs 12.7, Coffee Prep 85.3 vs 0.0.
 - Relative-pose control benefits less than absolute (RGB equi only +2–4 pts).
Failure/limitations: authors: symmetry broken by arm in voxel grid and camera noise; "incorrect equivariance" can hurt when demos are not symmetric; not shown for non-diffusion policies. Critical read: max-over-50-evals metric inflates all numbers; DP3 baseline here uses points above table from 4 cams and is weak (23.9 at 100 demos) — contrasts with DP3 paper; real baseline is a voxel DP (not image DP, not ACT); real relies on 3 calibrated D455 stereo cameras fused — heavy calibration; benefits concentrate where object poses vary widely (high-equivariance tasks).
Conflicts: Weakens the "3D input alone is the big win" story: here voxel-vs-RGB for the same DP is only ~+4 pts; the structural prior (equivariance) matters more. DP3's weak showing conflicts with DP3/iDP3/Hyper-DP3 — plausibly because MimicGen tasks require 6-DoF precision and multi-object reasoning where a pooled color-free embedding struggles (same pattern as 3DDiffuserActor's CALVIN result). Agrees with GreenScreenAug/RoboEngine-style evidence that augmentation helps but structural invariance/architecture helps more for its targeted shift.
Relevance: Our pumpkin/tray positions and orientations vary on a table → rotation-about-gravity symmetry is real, but SO-101 is a 5-DoF arm (+gripper) with a base yaw joint: joint-space actions are NOT equivariant in the escnn sense; would need EE-pose actions in a gravity-aligned robot-base frame (IK on a backlash-prone arm) and a calibrated scene camera. Cheaper transfer: SO(2) rotation augmentation of point clouds/voxels + EE actions (recovers ~half the gain). Equivariance does not address lighting/appearance shift directly (voxels carry RGB). 3× D455 fusion is beyond our single-RealSense setup.
Decision impact:
 - Q04 3D vs RGB: with the same DP, voxel input adds only ~+4 pts over RGB (100 demos 46.3 vs 42.0) — M (sim, max-over-evals metric).
 - Q04 structure/equivariance: SO(2)-equivariant denoiser +21.9 pts at 100 demos; real 80–95% vs 0–60% with 20–60 demos — M/H (large consistent gains) but needs EE actions + calibration.
 - Q05 augmentation: rotation augmentation recovers ~half of equivariance gain (53.3 vs 63.9 vs 46.3) — M.
 - Q02 action space: absolute EE pose > relative EE pose for both DP and EquiDiff (DP 42.0 vs 33.3 at 100 demos) — M (MimicGen).
 - Q13 data: equivariant 200 demos > baselines with 1000 — M.
