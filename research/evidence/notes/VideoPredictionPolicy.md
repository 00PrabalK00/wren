# VideoPredictionPolicy — Video Prediction Policy: A Generalist Robot Policy with Predictive Visual Representations (VPP) (2024, arXiv 2412.14803)
Setup: Stage 1: fine-tune Stable Video Diffusion (1.5B params) into a text-guided video prediction (TVP) model on ~375k trajectories (Something-Something-v2 human video 30%, OXE subsets, CALVIN, MetaWorld, own robot data); 8×A100, 2–3 days. Stage 2: single forward pass (one denoising step, timestep 20) of TVP on current frame(s) → multi-layer up-sampling features → "Video Former" (spatial-temporal attention to fixed tokens) → diffusion-transformer action head (MDT-style, 10 sampling steps), action chunk 10; 4×A100 6–12 h. Inputs 256×256, third-view + wrist (predicted per view). Latency ≈140 ms per TVP pass on RTX 4090 → 7–10 Hz with chunking. Sim: CALVIN ABC→D (language-annotated ABC only), MetaWorld 50 tasks × 50 demos. Real: Franka Panda 2,000 traj over 30+ tasks (~65/task avg), XArm + 12-DoF Xhand 4,000 traj over 100+ tasks; 200+ / 500+ real rollouts; "unseen" = new object or new background.
Claim: representations inside a manipulation-fine-tuned video diffusion model encode predicted future dynamics; conditioning a policy on them (implicit inverse dynamics) beats static encoders and explicit future-generation methods.
Evidence:
 - CALVIN ABC→D avg len (Table 1): RT-1 0.90, Diffusion Policy 0.56, Uni-Pi 0.92, MDT (official impl., ABC only) 1.55, RoboFlamingo 2.47, Susie 2.69, GR-1 3.06, Vidman 3.42, RoboUniview 3.65, VPP 4.33. With 10% of annotated data: GR-1 1.41 vs VPP 3.25.
 - MetaWorld 50 tasks (Table 2, avg success): RT-1 0.346, DP 0.279, Susie 0.410, GR-1 0.574, VPP 0.682 (hard tasks 0.015/0.095/0.255/0.451/0.526).
 - Real (Table 5): Panda seen/unseen: DP 0.42/0.25, Susie 0.56/0.46, GR-1 0.52/0.38, VPP 0.85/0.73. Dexterous hand seen/unseen/tool-use: DP 0.28/0.11/0.05, Susie 0.45/0.28/0.23, GR-1 0.32/0.15/0.15, VPP 0.75/0.60/0.68.
Ablations (CALVIN ABC→D avg len):
 - Encoder swap, everything else fixed (Table 3): VDM predictive features 4.33; Stable-VAE 2.58; VC-1 (MAE, fine-tuned on same videos) 1.23; Voltron (MAE + language, fine-tuned) 1.54.
 - Pretraining (Table 4): w/o internet manipulation video 3.97; w/o CALVIN video in TVP fine-tuning 3.31; w/o internet data AND w/o SVD pretraining (video model from scratch on CALVIN) 1.63.
 - Architecture: w/o Video Former 3.86 and latency ~450 ms vs ~140 ms; final-layer features instead of multi-layer aggregation 3.60; single layer 3/6/9/12: 3.72/3.88/4.29/4.05; diffusion timestep 10/20/30: 4.21/4.33/4.25; 2 denoising steps instead of 1: 4.19 (≈2× latency); no temporal attention in Video Former: 4.18; single static view (no wrist): 3.58.
Failure/limitations: authors give few; my read: needs a 1.5B video model fine-tuned for days on 8 A100 and ~140 ms/frame on a 4090 — far beyond an 8 GB laptop GPU for training and likely inference (SVD UNet in fp16 is ~3 GB weights plus activations for 16-frame latents at 256²; plausibly fits for inference only with care, but training the TVP stage is out of reach). Baselines weak in places (DP 0.56, MDT 1.55 on CALVIN ABC→D are much lower than those methods' own reports on other splits). Real multi-task with ~65 traj/task avg; "unseen" includes new backgrounds, where VPP holds up (0.73) better than DP (0.25).
Conflicts: agrees with Seer/FLARE/MDT that future-predictive representations help; the encoder ablation (VC-1 1.23 vs VDM 4.33) contrasts with works where MAE/VC-1-style encoders are competitive — here VC-1 was paired with a resampler into a DiT head on the hard ABC→D generalization split, which heavily rewards pretraining diversity. FLARE argues the same benefit can be had without a pixel/video generator.
Relevance: Not directly deployable on SO-101 + 8 GB GPU; relevant as evidence that (a) predictive/dynamics-aware features improve robustness to new backgrounds/objects (Panda unseen 0.73 vs DP 0.25), and (b) wrist view matters (single view 3.58 vs 4.33). Cheap analogs for us: FLARE-style latent future alignment or MDT-style masked foresight as train-only losses. Real DP baseline with ~65 demos/task in multitask: 0.42 seen / 0.25 unseen — a sober reference.
Decision impact:
 - Q09 aux objectives / representation: supports future-predictive representations (VDM features 4.33 vs VC-1 1.23 / Voltron 1.54 / VAE 2.58) — confidence M (sim, strong effect, but heavy model).
 - Q09: supports video-model pretraining with diverse (incl. human) video — w/o SVD+internet 1.63 vs 4.33 — confidence M; impractical at our compute — H.
 - Q11 cameras: supports wrist + static (4.33) over static only (3.58) — confidence M.
 - Q14 robustness: unseen objects/backgrounds real: VPP 0.73 vs DP 0.25 (Panda) — confidence M.
 - Q10 latency: one-step VDM encoding ~140 ms on 4090; 2-step halves control rate for no gain — confidence M.
