# Dita — Dita: Scaling Diffusion Transformer for Generalist Vision-Language-Action Policy (2025, arXiv 2503.19757)
Setup: Same architecture family as DiffusionTransformerPolicy (same group; many identical tables): frozen CLIP text + DINOv2 (fine-tuned) + Q-Former/FiLM → Llama-style causal transformer that denoises the 7-D EE action chunk in-context; 334M params (221M trainable). OXE pretraining: DDPM T=1000, 2 obs frames, 16-step chunks, 100k steps, batch 8,192 on 32 A100s; DDIM 20 steps for zero-shot eval. Real: Franka + Robotiq 2F-85, single third-person RealSense D435i (RGB only) ~1.5 m away, cluttered background; 10 demos per task ("10-shot"), multi-task LoRA fine-tune 20k steps with ColorJitter (brightness .3, contrast/saturation .7–1.3, hue .07), DDPM 100 steps, 1 obs / 1-step prediction (to match OpenVLA), control at 3 Hz with the model on an A100 server.
Claim: in-context DiT denoiser scales across embodiments and adapts with 10 real demos to long-horizon, rotation-heavy tasks with robustness to background/clutter/lighting.
Evidence:
 - SimplerEnv zero-shot (Table 1; coke can match/variant, move near m/v, drawer m/v): Dita 83.7/85.5, 76.0/73.0, 46.3/37.5 vs OpenVLA-7B 16.3/54.5, 46.2/47.7, 35.6/17.7 vs Octo-Base 17.0/0.6, 4.2/3.1, 22.7/1.1 vs RT-1-X 56.7/49.0, 31.7/32.3, 59.7/29.4.
 - LIBERO avg (Table 2): DP-scratch 72.4, Octo 75.1, OpenVLA 76.5, Dita 82.4, Dita + wrist camera 92.3 (LONG 63.8 → 83.6 with wrist cam).
 - CALVIN ABC→D avg len (Table 3): MLP diffusion head w/o pretrain 1.68 / with pretrain 3.16; Dita w/o pretrain 2.38 / with 3.61; +image aug 3.78.
 - ManiSkill2 novel views (Table 4): discretized 256-bin head 30.2, MLP diffusion head 58.6, Dita 65.8 (identical to DiffusionTransformerPolicy Table 4).
 - Real 10-shot: figure only (Fig. 5), qualitative: Dita > Diffusion-Head policy > Octo/OpenVLA on all tasks; text: "Dita achieves 63.8% success rate on two-step tasks". Robustness: LoRA fine-tune "fails to complete tasks under extreme variances"; full fine-tune reaches 20% under those variances (App.).
Ablations:
 - DDIM inference steps (Table 12, SimplerEnv, trained with 1000 DDPM steps; Pick-Coke variant / match / Move-Near variant / match): 100 steps 76.4/79.7/52.1/49.1; 50 → 79.1/83.3/66.0/72.0; 20 → 85.5/83.7/73.0/76.0; 10 → 85.3/82.0/69.5/74.0; 5 → 82.7/82.x/63.5/72.0; 2 → 70.4/73.3/51.6/65.0. Best at 10–20 steps; MORE steps (100) is worse; 2 steps loses ~15 pts. Authors attribute to less overfitting with fewer steps.
 - Head designs w/o pretraining on CALVIN (Table 9): UNet1D head 1.80, Transformer head 1.72, 8-layer MLP head 1.66, 3-layer MLP 1.68, single-token action chunk 0.84, Dita in-context 2.38.
 - Obs/trajectory length and execution steps: identical to DiffusionTransformerPolicy (traj 2→32: 40.8→65.8; 3 obs 35.4; exec 1 vs 16: 61.6 vs 58.0).
 - Image augmentation on CALVIN: 3.61 → 3.78. LR decay 3.43 → 3.61. Shuffle buffer 128k→256k: coke 71.2/73.6 → 83.7/85.5 (data-loader detail with 12-pt effect!).
 - LoRA (~11M trainable) vs full fine-tune under environment variance: LoRA fails, full FT 20% — LoRA capacity too small to absorb augmentation.
Failure/limitations: authors: baselines fail on grasp position; LoRA insufficient for robustness. Critical read: real numbers are figure-only, few trials, 3 Hz on an A100 — no latency on consumer hardware; heavy overlap with DiffusionTransformerPolicy (same ManiSkill2/ablation tables), so treat as ONE piece of evidence; robustness claim "surprisingly resilient" is qualitative, with 20% under extreme variance. The 12-pt swing from shuffle-buffer size shows how fragile these benchmark comparisons are.
Conflicts: Denoising-step result (10–20 DDIM ≥ 100) conflicts with ConsistencyPolicy (DDIM-15 collapses on ToolHang .14 vs DDPM .79) — different tasks: coarse pick/move vs high-precision hanging; big pretrained model vs small UNet from scratch. Wrist camera +10 pts avg on LIBERO (82.4 → 92.3) agrees with wrist-camera evidence elsewhere (Q11).
Relevance: (1) Denoising-step curve supports running a diffusion/flow head at ~10 steps with little loss and 2 steps with a notable loss — relevant for latency budgeting on the RTX 5060. (2) Even a 334M "lightweight" VLA runs at 3 Hz on A100 here — the same trap as our SmolVLA 2 s latency; don't expect this class to be real-time on 8 GB. (3) Full fine-tune + color jitter needed for lighting/background robustness; LoRA-only adaptation brittle → matters if we fine-tune any pretrained policy. (4) Wrist camera large gain.
Decision impact:
 - Q01 action head: continuous diffusion (in-context) > discrete 256-bin tokens (65.8 vs 30.2) and > small MLP diffusion head (2.38 vs 1.68 CALVIN scratch; UNet1D head 1.80) — confidence M (sim; same-group).
 - Q10 steps vs quality: DDIM 10–20 steps best (Pick-Coke var 85.3–85.5), 100 steps worse (76.4), 2 steps 70.4 — supports ~10 steps; 1–2 steps need distillation/consistency — confidence M.
 - Q10 latency: 334M model at 3 Hz on A100 — weakens mid-size VLAs for our laptop — confidence L/M (no timing breakdown).
 - Q11 cameras: +wrist camera LIBERO 82.4 → 92.3 (LONG 63.8 → 83.6) — confidence M.
 - Q14/Q05 robustness: LoRA fails under background/clutter/lighting variance, full FT + ColorJitter 20% — supports full fine-tune with augmentation — confidence L.
