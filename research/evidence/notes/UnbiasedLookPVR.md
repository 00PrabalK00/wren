# UnbiasedLookPVR — An Unbiased Look at Datasets for Visuo-Motor Pre-Training (2023, arXiv 2310.09289)
Setup: MAE ViT-B (768-d) pretrained with identical recipe on 1M frames from each of ImageNet, Ego4D, Kinetics-700, 100 Days of Hands (DoH), RoboNet, plus mixtures (Soup-1M = 200K each; Soup-2M; Soup-1M + 1M of X) — 15 representations. Downstream: FULL FINE-TUNING (encoder + 2-layer policy) with BC, GMM head, 50K Adam iterations, data augmentation on images, dropout p=0.2 in the policy. Sim: 13 tasks (RoboSuite, MetaWorld, Franka Kitchen). Real: Franka, third-person camera + proprio, Cartesian velocity actions, 3 tasks with 50 teleop demos each: block stacking (new configurations), pouring (NOVEL cup and bowl at test), toasting (NOVEL object, randomized toaster pose); standard errors reported (~25 trials/task implied by SE). Baselines: VC-1, MVP (same MAE ViT-B family, ≥2.5M frames mostly Ego4D), and Scratch (same ViT-B, random init).
Claim: the pretraining image distribution (curated, diverse) matters more than size or apparent domain relevance; ImageNet/Kinetics/DoH beat Ego4D/RoboNet on real robots; sim rankings do not predict real rankings; dropout matters on hardware.
Evidence:
 - Real average (Table 1; stack / pour / toast): ImageNet 68/44/10 → 41%; Kinetics 72/50/10 → 44%; DoH 55/19/40 → 38%; Ego4D 64/19/0 → 28%; RoboNet 76/13/0 → 30%; VC-1 60/25/10 → 33%; MVP 52/13/10 → 25%; Scratch 0/0/0 → 0%.
 - Sim average (same table): ImageNet 72, Ego4D 74, Kinetics 69, DoH 67, RoboNet 65, VC-1 65, MVP 65, Scratch 38 → sim ranks Ego4D first, real ranks it near last.
 - Mixtures (Table 2, real avg): Soup-1M 41; Soup-2M 41 (no gain from doubling); Soup+ImageNet 35; +Ego4D 36; +Kinetics 43; +DoH 60 (stack 92, pour 38, toast 50); +RoboNet 36. Best model is 60% vs strongest baseline (VC-1) 33%.
 - Sim-vs-real correlation across all models: R² = 0.32 (cherry-picked closest task pair R² = 0.34).
Ablations:
 - Dropout (p=0.2) in the policy: consistently improves real block stacking across the five 1M models and makes motion smoother, but often HURTS in sim (figure only). Block-Stacking-Robust (human knocks the cube away mid-grasp): average success 10% → 24% with dropout.
 - Appendix D (not in our text): the ImageNet/Kinetics/DoH > Ego4D/RoboNet ordering also holds with SimCLR pretraining (stated in the main text).
Failure/limitations: authors: no cheap predictor of real performance; MAE/BC only. My read: a single real scene per task; the OOD content is novel OBJECTS (pour, toast) but not lighting/background/camera; the toasting numbers are tiny (0–50%) with SE up to ±17; ViT-B from scratch at 0% shows 50 demos cannot train a ViT; all models are fine-tuned, so these are rankings of INITIALIZATIONS, not of frozen features.
Conflicts: contradicts R3M/VC-1/MVP's premise that egocentric/manipulation data is best (with real evidence); consistent with Burns (ImageNet-trained ViTs generalize better than manipulation-designed PVRs) and with SPA (human-interaction data no advantage over ImageNet MAE). Strong caution against trusting sim PVR benchmarks, which SPA/VC-1/Theia/Burns rely on. Complements DP: with fine-tuning + aug + dropout, a pretrained ViT-B trains fine on 50 demos, whereas scratch ViT-B = 0% (DP also found scratch ViT-B 0.22 vs ResNet 0.94).
Relevance: directly our regime (single third-person camera, 50 demos, Franka pick/place-style tasks, novel objects at test). Takeaways: (1) any ImageNet-style/curated-diverse init beats Ego4D/robot-data init; (2) do NOT train a ViT from scratch on 50 demos; (3) add dropout in the policy MLP/head (smoother, more robust real motion — relevant to our jerkiness); (4) do not pick the encoder from sim benchmarks.
Decision impact:
 - Q03 vision: curated diverse (ImageNet/Kinetics/DoH) pretraining > Ego4D/RoboNet/VC-1/MVP when fine-tuned on 50 real demos (41–44% vs 25–33%) — against robot-specific PVRs, for an ImageNet-family init — M/H (3 real tasks, SEs ±7 on averages).
 - Q03 vision: ViT-B from scratch fails at 50 demos (0%) — pretrained init mandatory for ViTs — H.
 - Q12/Q14 regularization: policy dropout 0.2 improves real success and recovery (10→24% on the perturbed task) — supports dropout — M.
 - Q14 robustness: novel test objects are handled better by ImageNet/Kinetics/DoH inits (pour 44–50% vs Ego4D 19%) — ~ supports diverse-image init — M.
 - Evaluation: sim PVR rankings do not transfer (R²=0.32) — discount sim-only encoder rankings — M/H.
