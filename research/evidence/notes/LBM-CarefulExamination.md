# LBM-CarefulExamination — A Careful Examination of Large Behavior Models for Multitask Dexterous Manipulation (2025, arXiv 2507.05331)
Setup: TRI bimanual Franka FR3 stations (9 stations), 2 scene cams (FRAMOS D415e, RGB used) + 2 wrist cams per arm (FLIR), up to 6 RGB images; policy at 10 Hz. Model fixed throughout: CLIP ViT-B/16 image encoder (FINETUNED, shared across cams, LR ×0.1), frozen CLIP text encoder + trained projection, 8-layer DiT (768-d) with adaLN, DDIM; obs history 2, predicts 16 steps of 20-D actions (relative EE SE(3) trajectory, 6D rot, gripper width), executes 8. Aug: resize 256×342 → random crop 224, color jitter (brightness 0.8–1.2, contrast 0.6–1.4, sat 0.8–1.2, hue ±0.05). Pretraining "Ramen" ≈1695 h (TRI 545 h: real 468 h/362 tasks/46k demos, sim 45 h, UMI 32 h; OXE subset 1150 h), 48k steps, batch 2560. Finetune 30k steps (real) / 10k (sim), batch 320, LR 2e-5. Evaluation: 1,800 real rollouts (50 per task×policy×condition, blind, randomized A/B bundles, image-overlay initial conditions), >47k sim rollouts (200 per task×policy×condition), Bayesian posteriors + sequential hypothesis tests with CLD letters.
Claim: finetuning a multitask-pretrained diffusion policy beats training single-task from scratch, needs 3–5× less task data, and the advantage grows under distribution shift; gains scale smoothly with pretraining data — but effects are small enough that most robotics papers are statistically underpowered.
Evidence (most results are violin plots; figure only unless noted):
 - "Seen" tasks: finetuned LBM statistically ≥ single-task on 3/3 real and 15/16 sim tasks; statistically BETTER on 2/3 real and 3/16 sim (nominal) and 10/16 sim under distribution shift (lighting, env map, camera extrinsics/intrinsics, textures, distractors) → the gap widens under shift.
 - Pretrained LBM without finetuning: ≈ single-task in sim, WORSE than single-task in real nominal (3/8 real tasks worse, 5/8 same).
 - Table S6 (success rates; single-task / pretrained / finetuned): PutKiwiInCenterOfTable real 0.44 / 0.20 / 0.82, sim 0.135 / 0.02 / 0.09; TurnMugRightsideUp real 0.84 / 0.56 / 0.88, sim 0.375 / 0.485 / 0.465.
 - Unseen long-horizon real tasks: finetuned > single-task on task completion in 4/5 real tasks; SetBreakfastTable single-task empirical SR = 0, finetuned LBM with only 15% of the data statistically beats single-task with 100%.
 - Sim unseen tasks: finetuned LBM needs <30% of the task data to match single-task; overall claim 3–5× less data.
 - Pretraining scaling (sim, task completion): full > TRI-only > TRI-50% > TRI-25% > single-task, all separable at 15% finetune data; no discontinuities.
 - Data quantity in single-task (sim Breakfast scenario): 49 demos → SR 19.5% and 13.5%; 196 demos → 50%; 490 demos → 68.5%.
Ablations:
 - Data normalization bug (wrong per-source normalizers in pretraining): small effect nominal, correct normalization better under distribution shift (Fig. S21, figure only). Authors: "decisions like data normalization ... often dominate architectural or algorithmic changes".
 - Low-motion-start filtering (remove frames until gripper moved >5 cm or 15°): unfiltered data → policies fail to start moving (e.g., finetuned LBM idle in 89/200 TurnCupUpsideDown rollouts); filtering helped single-task in sim but hurt pretrained LBM (committed to wrong tasks).
 - Pretraining data mix: adding diverse OXE data still helps when finetune data is scarce; with lots of task data, less pretraining suffices.
 - Language: non-finetuned LBM often performs the WRONG task in visually ambiguous scenes (small CLIP text encoder → weak language steering).
Failure/limitations: authors: CIs don't include training-seed variance; 50 real rollouts may miss small effects; small CLIP language encoder; normalization bug; sim vs real timeouts differ (Kiwi real 0.82 drops to 0.70 with sim timeout; single-task 0.44 → 0.20). Critical read: the backbone, action head, history, and chunking were NOT varied — this is a data/pretraining study, not an architecture study; bimanual Franka with ~hundreds of demos/task is a different regime from 50–100 demos on one low-cost arm; no 3D input.
Conflicts: agrees with RoboVLMs (in-domain/same-robot data > cross-embodiment; pretraining helps few-shot) and with DP-family results that CLIP/ViT finetuned + diffusion + 2-step history + 16/8 chunking is a solid default. Disagrees with the common claim that generalist pretrained policies work zero-shot — here non-finetuned is ≤ single-task in real. Its statistics warning undercuts many small-trial papers in this batch (PointVLA 3–11 trials, 3DDA 10, Hyper-DP3 15).
Relevance: (1) For a single pumpkin task with 50–100 demos, the big lever is starting from a pretrained policy and FINETUNING on our task (their 15%-of-data result), not using a generalist zero-shot. For us the available pretraining is SO-100/101 community data (in-domain embodiment → per RoboVLMs better than cross-embodiment). (2) Idle-at-start failure: trim low-motion prefixes of our teleop episodes (5 cm / 15° threshold) — cheap and directly relevant to SO-101 demos. (3) Normalization per timestep with 2–98 percentile clipping to [−1.5,1.5]. (4) Evaluation: our lighting/pumpkin/camera shift tests need ~50 trials per condition to separate policies; ≤20 trials only detects huge effects. (5) Their default recipe (finetuned CLIP ViT-B/16, 2-frame history, DiT diffusion, predict 16/execute 8 at 10 Hz, crop + color jitter) is a reasonable, 8 GB-feasible baseline architecture.
Decision impact:
 - Q13 data: pretrain-then-finetune reaches single-task performance with 3–5× less task data (real: 15% of data beats 100% single-task on SetBreakfastTable) — H (blind, 50 rollouts, statistical tests).
 - Q13 data quantity: single-task SR 13.5–19.5% at 49 demos vs 50% at 196 and 68.5% at 490 (sim) — M (different tasks, confounded).
 - Q13 data quality: trimming idle starts fixes "robot never moves" failures for single-task — M.
 - Q14 robustness: pretraining advantage grows under lighting/camera/texture shift (sim 3/16 → 10/16 tasks significantly better) — H (sim, 200 rollouts/condition).
 - Q03 vision encoder: finetuned CLIP ViT-B/16 (LR ×0.1) + crop/jitter works at scale — L (not ablated).
 - Q07 history: 2 obs steps default — L (not ablated).
 - Q06 chunking: predict 16, execute 8 at 10 Hz — L (not ablated).
 - Q08 language: small frozen CLIP text encoder → wrong-task execution in ambiguous scenes; needs finetuning — M.
 - Evaluation: 50 real trials/condition + paired, blind, randomized bundles; small-trial comparisons are likely noise — H.
