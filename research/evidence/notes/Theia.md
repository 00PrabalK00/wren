# Theia — Theia: Distilling Diverse Vision Foundation Models for Robot Learning (2024, arXiv 2407.20179)
Setup: DeiT-Tiny/Small/Base (~5M/22M/86M) student distilled on ImageNet-1k (50 epochs, ~150 H100-hours) to match the SPATIAL tokens of CLIP-L, DINOv2-L and ViT-H (final teacher set "CDiV") through shallow-CNN feature translators; loss 0.9·cos + 0.1·smooth-L1; teacher features normalized per dimension. Eval: CortexBench MuJoCo subset (Adroit, MW, DMC) + TriFinger reach + ImageNav, FROZEN encoders, spatial tokens → "compression layer" CNN → MLP (VC-1 protocol). Real: WidowX 250s with 3 tasks (door opening 48 demos, 50 trials; pick-place 63 demos, 20 trials; toy-microwave 101 demos over 10 objects, 50 trials incl. 10 on 5 unseen objects), CNN+MLP BC head, SmoothL1, lr 1e-4, 400 epochs; Spot drawer opening (50 scripted demos, 20 trials), Diffusion Policy head.
Claim: a small encoder distilled from several VFMs beats its teachers and robot-specific PVRs at a fraction of the compute; spatial tokens >> CLS; entropy of the feature-norm distribution predicts downstream performance.
Evidence:
 - MuJoCo subset vs inference MACs (figure only, qualitative): Theia-S/B are the only models scoring >80; Theia-T beats most much larger models; naive concatenation of CLIP+DINOv2+ViT (CDV) is much worse than each alone.
 - CortexBench 14-task mean (Table 1, frozen): Theia-B 79.79; MVP-L-sp 77.42; R3M 76.51; E-RADIO 73.50; VIP 71.18; VC-1-L-sp 69.56.
 - Real (Table 2). Door opening, FROZEN (open / fully open): Theia-B 92/66; E-RADIO 72/46; MVP-L 32/2; VC-1-L 12/4; R3M 48/36; DINOv2-L 46/12; ViT-H 18/4. Pick-place, FROZEN (pick / place): Theia 85/75; E-RADIO 75/55; MVP 55/35; VC-1 55/45; R3M 35/10; DINOv2-L 10/0; ViT-H 15/0. Toy-microwave, FINE-TUNED (pick / place / close): Theia 58/52/40; E-RADIO 58/54/42; MVP 40/26/18; VC-1 14/4/4; R3M 36/26/18; DINOv2-L 20/10/2; ViT-H 52/44/40. With FROZEN encoders every model scored 0% on the microwave task (appendix). Spot drawer, frozen / fine-tuned: Theia 85/100; E-RADIO 15/80; MVP 30/65; VC-1 0/45; R3M 0/55; DINOv2-L 35/20; ViT-H 45/85.
 - Feature-norm entropy vs MuJoCo score: R=0.943 (regular models), R=0.638 (distilled models); VC-1 shows high-norm outlier tokens on task-irrelevant patches.
Ablations (Theia-T unless noted, MuJoCo score):
 - Using the CLS token vs spatial tokens: 50.0 vs 78.9; distilling CLS+spatial vs spatial only: 77.3 vs 78.9.
 - Loss: MSE 78.2 vs cos+L1 78.9. Register tokens (Theia-B): 0 → 79.2, 1 → 80.3, 4 → 79.4, 8 → 79.6.
 - Teachers: single-teacher ViT or CLIP best; SAM and Depth-Anything slightly neutral or negative; CDiV best; RADIO's set (CDiS) is worse (figure only).
Failure/limitations: authors: several CortexBench tasks not reproducible (Move Cube, ObjectNav, MobilePick excluded). My read: real tests vary robot/object POSITION (and 5 unseen objects in only 10 microwave trials) but not lighting, background or camera; no OOD split reported per object; 20–50 trials per task, one seed. Frozen evaluation dominates the sim results. Fine-tuning improved nearly every encoder on the Spot drawer (VC-1 0→45, R3M 0→55, E-RADIO 15→80) EXCEPT DINOv2-L (35→20) — the authors cannot explain it.
Conflicts: supports DP-paper "fine-tune > frozen" in the real world (drawer; microwave frozen = 0%), except for DINOv2-L. Supports SPA/Burns in that DINOv2 is mediocre as a frozen robot feature (pick-place frozen DINOv2-L 10/0). Agrees with VC-1 that CLS readout is a weak interface (50 vs 79). Shows VC-1-L frozen is poor on real WidowX tasks (12/4 door), consistent with UnbiasedLook's finding that VC-1 is weak on real robots.
Relevance: Theia-T/S (5–22M) fits the 8 GB GPU easily and runs fast at inference; trained on ImageNet only; public weights. Use SPATIAL tokens (patch grid → small conv/attention pooling or FiLM), not CLS. Fine-tune it at low LR on our 50–100 demos; the drawer result suggests fine-tuning a distilled small encoder is safe (85→100). No lighting/camera-shift evidence though.
Decision impact:
 - Q03 vision: Theia-S/T (multi-teacher distilled, small) as init — supports it — M (sim + 4 real tasks, same authors, no OOD split).
 - Q03 vision: use spatial tokens, not CLS/global pooling (50.0 vs 78.9) — supports spatial features — H (large, consistent across models).
 - Q03 vision: fine-tuning > frozen in real multi-stage tasks (microwave frozen 0%; drawer 0–35 → 45–100); DINOv2-L the exception — supports fine-tuning — M.
 - Q12 model size: 22–86M distilled encoder beats 300–600M VFMs — supports small encoders — M.
