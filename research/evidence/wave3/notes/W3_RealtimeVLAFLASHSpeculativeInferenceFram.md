# W3_RealtimeVLAFLASHSpeculativeInferenceFram — Realtime-VLA FLASH: Speculative Inference Framework for Diffusion-based VLAs (2026, arXiv 2605.13778)
Setup: pi0 (2.7B VLM + action expert, flow matching, 10 denoise steps), chunk H=50, replan every 12 actions, RTX 4090D. FLASH: 110M draft model (1 Gemma block + learned action queries, L-regression) proposes a chunk from fresh image features but REUSES the last VLM KV cache; main action expert verifies at 2 flow timesteps in parallel; accept longest consistent prefix (δ=0.15); fall back to full path on rejection, on gripper switch, and periodically (PF=2). LIBERO 4 suites x 10 tasks x 50 trials. Real: UR5 + 2 RealSense D435i, conveyor-belt sorting (toy dog, hairbrush), 200 demos per object, LoRA 20k steps, 10 trials per condition, SYNCHRONOUS inference (no async/RTC).
Claim: most replanning rounds can be served by a cheap draft verified by the main model, cutting latency ~3x with little success loss; lower latency directly widens the feasible speed of reactive tasks.
Evidence:
 - Profiling pi0 on 4090D: image encoder 11.3 ms, VLM prefill 26.7 ms, action denoise 20.0 ms (memory-bound).
 - LIBERO (Table 1) SR / task-level latency: Torch-pi0 94.1 / 58.0 ms; Triton-pi0 94.2 / 39.7; FLASH 93.4 / 34.9; FLASH+Triton 93.8 / 19.1 (3.04x). Flash round 7.8 ms vs 58 ms full.
 - Real conveyor (Table 4, 10 trials, demos at 6 m/min): 10 m/min toy dog/hairbrush: JAX-pi0 20/50, Triton 80/40, FLASH+Triton 80/90; 13 m/min: 0/0, 30/10, 50/30; 15 m/min: 0/0, 0/0, 20/10. JAX-pi0 80% on toy dog at 8 m/min.
Ablations (LIBERO-10, Table 3): draft-only (flash path always) 58.4% at 13.3 ms vs full 85.2%; + periodic refresh PF=2 80.6 / 21.0 ms, PF=3 82.0, PF=4 75.4; + gripper-phase fallback only 66.8; FB + PF=2 84.6 / 24.1 ms. Accepted prefix covers ~70% of the replan window; ~67% of rounds served by flash path.
Failure/limitations: heuristic thresholds; draft model trained per suite; real test only 10 trials per cell and a synthetic speed stress; draft-only regression model loses badly on long-horizon (58.4 vs 85.2) — errors accumulate especially in fine-adjustment phases (grasp/release).
Conflicts: Complements RTC/PAINT (they hide latency; FLASH reduces it). The draft-only result echoes ACT/other findings that a small plain-regression head is weaker than a generative head on multimodal/long tasks — but here it also lacks fresh VLM context.
Relevance: Moderate. Key practical points for our 2 s SmolVLA latency: (1) a 2.7B pi0 runs a full round in 58 ms on a 4090D, so 2 s for 450M SmolVLA on an RTX 5060 laptop indicates a pipeline problem (CPU preprocessing, no fp16/bf16, no KV cache, cold compilation, or network) rather than an inherent limit; (2) kernel-level optimization (Triton) cut 32% latency with no success loss; (3) latency matters most around grasp/release and moving targets; (4) staleness failures ("gripper arrives behind the object") are the signature of too-long open-loop execution.
Decision impact:
 - Q10 latency: supports reducing per-call latency (kernel/compile optimizations 58 -> 40 ms at equal SR; speculative drafting to 19 ms) and shows success collapses with stale chunks on moving targets (15 m/min: 0% vs 20%) — confidence M (real but 10 trials).
 - Q06 chunking: long open-loop execution (12 of 50 actions per replan) with synchronous inference fails in reactive tasks; draft-only fast regression without refresh loses 27 pts on long horizon — confidence L-M.
 - Q12 model size: 110M regression draft alone 58.4% vs 2.7B pi0 85.2% on LIBERO-10 (draft reuses stale VLM cache) — confidence L.
