# W3_LearningLatentPlansfromPlay — Learning Latent Plans from Play (Play-LMP) (2019, arXiv 1903.01973, CoRL 2019)
Setup: simulated (MuJoCo) 8-DoF-ish tabletop playroom (drawer, sliding door, buttons, blocks), VR-teleoperated; 18 goal-image-specified test tasks; baseline expert data 100 demos/task (1800 demos, ~1.5 h); play data up to ~7 h unlabeled. Models: Play-GCBC (goal-conditioned BC) and Play-LMP (seq2seq CVAE: bidirectional plan-recognition encoder, learned conditional prior p(z|s_c,s_g), stochastic RNN policy); conv encoder trained from scratch on pixels, or ground-truth state. Eval: 3 seeds (pixels), 20 rollouts per task/CI.
Claim: goal-conditioned policies self-supervised on unlabeled play data (random windows relabeled with their end state as goal) beat per-task expert BC; a latent-plan CVAE helps handle multimodality vs plain GCBC; play-trained models are more robust to initial-state perturbation.
Evidence (18-task avg success): pixels — BC (18 per-task, labeled) 66.5% +-12.1; Play-GCBC 58.7% +-11.6; Play-LMP 69.4% +-10.8. States — BC 70.3%; Multitask BC 66.2%; Play-GCBC 77.9%; Play-LMP 85.5%. Play-LMP with 30 min of play (states) 71.8% > 18 BC policies on 90 min of expert data (70.3%). Play covers 4.2x more of the interaction space than expert demos for the same collection time (14.4x more than random).
Ablations: latent plan (LMP) vs none (GCBC): +10.7 pts pixels, +7.6 pts states, up to +50 pts on individual tasks (figure). Robustness to initial-position perturbation 0-0.4 m (Fig 6b, figure only, qualitative): play-trained models degrade much more slowly than expert BC. Emergent retrying (qualitative).
Failure/limitations: authors: same environment for play and test; no novel objects/environments. Critical read: sim only; pixel-level differences between BC 66.5 and LMP 69.4 are within the +-11 CIs; goal-image conditioning not our interface.
Conflicts: supports the view (later: Diffusion Policy, ACT CVAE, MimicPlay) that multimodal action/plan modeling matters with human data; supports "coverage/recovery data beats narrow optimal demos" (also DART, Decomposing-gap, Behavior Retrieval robustness).
Relevance: moderate-low. Transferable ideas for SO-101: (1) collect some "play"/varied-start data around the pumpkin and tray, not only clean resets, to get coverage and retry behavior; (2) CVAE latent (as in ACT) is a validated way to absorb multimodality. Not directly about vision robustness or latency.
Decision impact:
 - Q13 data diversity: supports broad coverage (play) over narrow expert demos: 30 min play 71.8% vs 90 min expert 70.3%; play-trained more robust to perturbation — M (sim, consistent).
 - Q01 action head: latent-variable (CVAE) plan policy > deterministic-ish GCBC by 7.6-10.7 pts — L/M (sim; pixel CIs overlap).
