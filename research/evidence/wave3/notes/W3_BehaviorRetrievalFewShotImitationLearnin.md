# W3_BehaviorRetrievalFewShotImitationLearnin — Behavior Retrieval: Few-Shot Imitation Learning by Querying Unlabeled Datasets (2023, arXiv 2304.08742)
Setup: Sim: RoboSuite CanPick, NutAssembly (Franka, 7-DoF EE), PyBullet WidowX Office; real: WidowX 200, 3-DoF delta EE control at 3 Hz, one Logitech C920 third-person cam (sim also wrist cam), 84x84 images. Target data Dt = 10 expert demos/task; prior Dprior = 160-1200 unlabeled/mixed-quality demos (real Pickle/Soap: 285/485 Bridge trajectories from visually similar scenes). Policy: ResNet-18 (R3M init for Bridge tasks) + spatial softmax, 2-layer LSTM (history 10, hidden 1000), 5-mode GMM head; random crop (sim) + color jitter (real). Retrieval: beta-VAE (s,a) embedding trained on Dprior, keep prior transitions whose normalized max similarity to any Dt transition > delta (0.6-0.8). Eval: sim 100 trials x 3 seeds; real 40 trials/method/task; robustness 10 trials/cell.
Claim: using the few target demos to retrieve relevant (s,a) pairs from a large unlabeled prior dataset and co-training beats Dt-only, naive mixing, and GC pretrain+finetune by ~20-30%.
Evidence:
 - Sim (Table I, 10 demos) avg success: GCBC on Dprior 35%, GCBC+FT 36%, Behavior Retrieval 72%. Per task (Ours): CanPick img 74/state 73, NutAssembly img 58/state 75, Office img 64/state 85.
 - vs retrieval strategies (Fig 9, figure only, qualitative): Ours ~ ground-truth-label oracle; >20 pts over Dt-only and Dprior+Dt with 10 demos, ~10 pts with online interventions; Dt-only is high variance and collapses on some tasks.
 - Real (Fig 10, figure only): >20 pts over all baselines on NutAssembly/Pickle/Soap, exceeds ground-truth task filtering because it also retrieves useful sub-segments (e.g. grasps) of irrelevant tasks; Dt-only fails completely on real NutAssembly.
 - Full visually diverse Bridge as prior (20 trials, cup pick-place): Ours 55%, Dt 30%, Dprior+Dt 35% (delta=0.9).
 - Robustness, real Pickle (Table II, 10 trials): Dt-only vs Ours - different cup color 1/10 vs 6/10; real pickle 0/10 vs 4/10; 4 visual distractors 0/10 vs 4/10; physical perturbation 3/10 vs 4/10.
Ablations: VAE vs contrastive-RL embedding for retrieval, sim avg 72% vs 38% (contrastive diverged on Office image). delta: best ~0.75 sim and real; performance degrades gracefully as delta decreases (more irrelevant data), sharply as delta -> 1 (discard all prior). With LMP: LMP(Dprior) 55%, +FT on Dt 56%, +FT with retrieval 67%. Failure modes (real, 40 trials x 3 tasks): Ours failures 54% grasp / 2% intermediate / 44% place, similar to oracle; naive all-data mixing -> more intermediate failures (31%) from adversarial prior data.
Failure/limitations: authors: retrieval uses visual-similarity embedding, may fail when prior is visually very different; needs prior data at train time. Critical read: 3 Hz, 3-DoF control, 84x84 images - very low-fidelity regime; real results mostly figure-only; robustness cells only 10 trials; testing location had constant lighting.
Conflicts: agrees with later retrieval/co-training work (e.g. STRAP, DataMIL) that naive co-training with off-task data can hurt, filtered co-training helps. Differs from pretrain-then-finetune generalist practice (Octo/OpenVLA fine-tune) — here GC pretrain+FT underperforms, but these are tiny from-scratch models, not large pretrained VLAs.
Relevance: moderate. Our 50-100 demos are more than 10, but for new objects/lighting we could retrieve from public LeRobot SO-100/SO-101 community datasets (same embodiment) rather than naive mixing. The Dt-only collapse under cup color change / distractors (1/10, 0/10) mirrors our pumpkin/lighting failure: more varied (retrieved) data gave the robustness, not the architecture.
Decision impact:
 - Q13 data: supports co-training with filtered same-embodiment prior data over Dt-only or naive mixing (sim avg 72% vs 35-36%; real +20 pts) — M (consistent sim+real, but 10-demo regime, 3 Hz).
 - Q14 robustness: supports prior-data diversity for appearance shift (cup color 1/10 -> 6/10, distractors 0/10 -> 4/10) — L (10 trials).
 - Q01 action head: GMM (5 modes) + LSTM works in this low-data setting; no head comparison — L.
