# W3_HybridConsistencyPolicyDecouplingMultiMo — Hybrid Consistency Policy: Decoupling Multi-Modal Diversity and Real-Time Efficiency in Robotic Manipulation (2025/26, arXiv id not in text)
Setup: Diffusion Policy teacher (DDPM, 80 steps) distilled into a student that runs a K-step stochastic SDE prefix then one consistency jump (HCP, 25+1 steps). Obs: 2 frames; output 8-step EE pose chunk. Sim: Push-T (200 demos), Avoiding (96 demos), 100 trials. Real: Flexiv Rizon 7-DoF, wrist RealSense D415 + third-person D435, 224x224 crops, VR teleop, 60 demos/task, teleop at 30 Hz logged at 10 Hz, policy executes at 10 Hz, LeRobot, RTX 4080; 6 tasks (3 multimodal, 3 single-mode), 20 trials/task.
Claim: A stochastic prefix to the "mode bifurcation" time followed by a one-step consistency jump keeps DDPM-level success and mode coverage at ~3x lower latency.
Evidence:
 - Sim (Acc / entropy / #modes): Push-T DP-ddpm 69%/0.99/2; DP-ddim 74%/0/1; HCP-5step 59%/0.47/2; HCP-25 64%/0.92/2. Avoiding: ddpm 89%/2.52/6; ddim 100%/0/1; HCP-5 86%/0/1; HCP 87%/2.08/5. Overall acc ddpm 79, ddim 87, HCP-5 72.5, HCP 75.5.
 - Real multimodal (time per chunk on RTX 4080; Push-T / Avoiding / Pick&Place acc; overall): ddpm 0.54 s, 70/90/75, 78.3%; ddim 0.34 s, 70/100/60, 76.7% (entropy 0.39); HCP-5 0.04 s, 5/70/40, 38.3%; HCP-25 0.17 s, 55/90/75, 73.3%.
 - Real single-mode (acc, episode time): Nail Hammering ddpm 50% 22.4 s vs HCP 35% 19.8 s; Belt Removal 80%/19.6 vs 80%/14.4; Screwing 90%/12.6 vs 95%/9.1; overall 73.3% vs 70%, 18.6 s vs 14.4 s.
Ablations: switch time: 5-step prefix vs 25: real overall 38.3 -> 73.3%, latency 0.04 -> 0.17 s; Push-T-Real 5% -> 55%. Premature jump collapses modes AND success.
Failure/limitations: student still below teacher on dynamic tasks (Push-T-Real 55 vs 70, Hammering 35 vs 50); switch time hand-tuned; requires a trained 80-step teacher + distillation; 20 trials/task.
Conflicts / critical read: Deterministic DDIM (50 steps) had EQUAL OR HIGHER success than stochastic DDPM in sim (87 vs 79) and near-equal real (76.7 vs 78.3) despite collapsing to a single mode — mode diversity is not required for task success on these tasks; it matters for "coverage" metrics, not success. This weakens the argument that one must preserve multimodality at inference for performance; what matters is not averaging modes in training (which a generative head does regardless of sampler). The 5-step consistency student's collapse (38%) shows naive aggressive distillation hurts. Contrast: flow-matching papers (e.g., LAFM) report 1-step flow with no loss — flow/rectified paths distill/shortcut more gracefully than DDPM consistency jumps.
Relevance: Directly relevant to our latency problem and our 10 Hz data regime (same logging rate). Diffusion chunk generation at 0.34-0.54 s on RTX 4080 is too slow-ish for smooth 10 Hz control with a laptop 5060; prefer a flow-matching head with few Euler steps, or a deterministic sampler; no need to preserve stochasticity for success on pick-place.
Decision impact:
 - Q10 latency: DDPM 0.54 s -> DDIM 0.34 s -> HCP 0.17 s per chunk (4080) with 78.3/76.7/73.3% real success; 5-step consistency 0.04 s but 38.3% — supports few-step deterministic sampling, warns against naive 1-step distillation of DDPM — confidence M
 - Q01 action head: deterministic DDIM success 87% sim / 76.7% real ~= stochastic DDPM 79% / 78.3% despite mode collapse — sampling stochasticity not needed for success; generative training still assumed — confidence M
