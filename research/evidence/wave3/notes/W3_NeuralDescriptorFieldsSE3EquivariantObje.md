# W3_NeuralDescriptorFieldsSE3EquivariantObje — Neural Descriptor Fields: SE(3)-Equivariant Object Representations for Manipulation (2021, arXiv 2112.05124)
Low relevance. NDF transfers grasp and place poses from 5–10 demos by descriptor matching on segmented point clouds (4 calibrated depth cameras). It is open-loop pose optimization and uses no closed-loop policy. In simulation it beats 2D Dense Object Nets, mainly when objects are in arbitrary 6-DoF poses (the extracted tables are too garbled to quote reliably; the text says NDF is on par with DON for upright grasps and much better for arbitrary poses, with >85% overall success from 10 demos; ablations show all-layer occupancy-network features >> first/last layer or random features, and performance drops sharply with 1 demo). Real Franka results are qualitative. Takeaway for us is conceptual only: SE(3)-equivariant 3D object representations generalize over object pose with very few demos, but they require segmented multi-view depth and a pose-level (not trajectory) task formulation. Our pumpkin pick-place could be framed this way, but that abandons the end-to-end policy route.
Decision impact:
 - Q04 3D: ~ 3D equivariant object-centric representations give pose generalization from 5–10 demos, but only for open-loop pick-place with good multi-view depth — confidence L (sim, open-loop)
