# W3_OutputFeedbackTubeMPCGuidedDataAugmentat — Output Feedback Tube MPC-Guided Data Augmentation for Robust, Efficient Sensorimotor Policy Learning (2022, arXiv 2210.10127)
Low relevance: simulated quadrotor trajectory tracking; the expert is a model-based robust tube MPC whose tube + ancillary feedback controller give corrective action labels for perturbed states, and synthetic images for those states are rendered from a photogrammetry mesh (145 real images). Result (10 seeds x 20 rollouts): 1 demo + tube augmentation -> 100% success without wind, >=94.5% with wind; BC/DAgger baselines need 40-50 demos (no wind) and DAgger+DR needs 120 demos for 97.3% (wind). Adding an auxiliary state-prediction output "improved performance" (qualitative).
Why not more: requires a dynamics model + MPC expert, no human teleop, no manipulation. Conceptual link only: synthesizing off-trajectory views WITH corrective action labels (cf. SPARTN/NeRF augmentation for grasping) is far more data-efficient than plain demos — for SO-101 this would need a scene reconstruction and a corrective-label rule for the arm, not practical for us now.
Decision impact: Q05 augmentation: novel-view synthesis + corrective labels cut demos ~50-100x (sim quadrotor) — L. Q13: covariate-shift coverage matters more than demo count — L.
