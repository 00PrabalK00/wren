# W3_PerceiverActorAMultiTaskTransformerforRo — Perceiver-Actor: A Multi-Task Transformer for Robotic Manipulation (PerAct) (2022, arXiv 2209.05451)
Setup: Sim: RLBench, Franka, 18 tasks / 249 variations, 4 noiseless RGB-D cams (front, L/R shoulder, wrist) 128x128, 10 or 100 demos/task, 25 eval episodes/task, one multi-task agent. Real: Franka, ONE front RGB-D camera, 7 tasks / 18 variations, 53 demos total (5-10 per task), 5-10 test trials per task. Model: RGB-D fused into 100^3 voxel grid (1 m^3), Perceiver transformer (2048 latents x 512, 6 self-attn layers), CLIP language, predicts NEXT KEYFRAME as discretized classification (translation voxel, 5° rotation bins, gripper, collision flag) executed by a sampling-based motion planner. Training 16 days on 8 V100 (600K iters, batch 16).
Claim: voxelized 3D observation+action space with a Perceiver transformer is far more data-efficient for multi-task 6-DoF language-conditioned BC than image-to-action agents.
Evidence: Abstract: outperforms Image-BC (CNN/ViT, RGB-D input, FiLM-CLIP) by 34x and C2FARM-BC (3D ConvNet coarse-to-fine) by 2.8x; vs C2FARM-BC average 1.33x at 10 demos, 2.83x at 100 demos, wins 25/36 task-evaluations. Image-BC near zero on most tasks. Near-zero for all agents on high-precision insert peg / stack cups / place cups. Real (Table 2, % success): press hand-sanitizer 90 (5 train/10 test), put marker 70, place food in drawer 60, hit ball 40, stack blocks 60, sweep beans 40, (7th task) 20 — exact row alignment partly garbled in extraction; >65% only on simple short-horizon tasks.
Ablations (open drawer, figure only): no language -> chance; Perceiver global receptive field crucial (C2FARM variants ~33% chance on top/middle/bottom drawer vs PerAct >70%); keyframe extraction essential — random or fixed-interval keyframes -> 0%; skip connection only speeds training. Sensitivity (appendix, not in extracted text): more latents help multi-task capacity; 32^3 enough for some tasks, 100^3 needed for precise ones; rotation augmentation helps.
Failure/limitations: needs motion planner and keyframe heuristic (joint-velocity ~0 + gripper unchanged) — not reactive, no dynamic tasks; real failures mostly wrong gripper-open predictions leading to OOD states; exploits dataset biases; training cost huge (8xV100, 16 days) for multi-task; train and test objects identical (no novel-object test). Critical read: Image-BC baseline is a single-step BC-Z style agent without chunking — a weak image baseline by 2024 standards (no ACT/DP comparison).
Conflicts: Later work (Act3D, RVT, 3D Diffuser Actor) improved on PerAct; DP/ACT-style dense closed-loop image policies succeed at low data on real hardware, showing the "image BC ≈ 0" result is baseline-specific (single-step, from-scratch, multi-task with few demos).
Relevance: Low-moderate. Keyframe + motion planner formulation does not fit SO-101 teleop joint-space closed-loop control (and SO-101 has 5-DoF arm + gripper, limited IK). Take-aways: (a) 3D fused representation from a single RealSense can support few-demo learning; (b) keyframe-based pick-place is a viable alternative paradigm for simple pick-and-place (pumpkin -> tray is essentially 2-3 keyframes), sidestepping chunk-boundary jerk and latency; (c) training cost is prohibitive for 8 GB (though single-task/smaller grids are lighter).
Decision impact:
 - Q04 3D: supports 3D voxel input for few-demo multi-task 6-DoF (2.8x over 3D ConvNet, 34x over image-BC) — M (sim, weak image baseline)
 - Q02 action space: keyframe next-best-pose + planner is data-efficient for quasi-static pick-place; bad keyframes (fixed interval) -> 0% — L for us (planner-dependent)
 - Q08 language: CLIP-conditioned single multi-task agent works; without language -> chance on multi-variation tasks — L
 - Q12 model size / compute: Perceiver 100^3 voxel training 16 days on 8 V100 — infeasible on 8 GB laptop — M
