# W3_SimulationDistillationPretrainingWorldMo — Simulation Distillation: Pretraining World Models in Simulation for Rapid Real-World Adaptation (2026, arXiv id not in text)
Setup: UR5e, 2 precise assembly tasks (peg insertion 16 mm, table leg), Narrow (2x2 cm) and Wide (35x35 cm) init; wrist + overhead + side RGB 224x224, ResNet18 (ImageNet) -> 64-D latent; relative EE pose + binary gripper, 5 Hz, history H=5 and horizon T=5; world model pretrained on 100k sim trajectories from a privileged RL expert + perturbations; real adaptation with MPPI planning, 20 teleop demos + 15-30 min real rollouts; 20 real trials per point. Also Unitree Go2 locomotion. Baselines: RLPD, IQL, SGFT-SAC, Diffusion Policy and pi0.5 trained on 100 real demos (and real+sim).
Claim: pretrain encoder/reward/value/dynamics in sim; in real only fine-tune latent dynamics -> monotonic improvement with little real data, ~2x baselines.
Evidence: real curves are figure-only; qualitative: SimDist reaches ~2x the success of any baseline; DP with 100 real demos fails over much of the Wide init region (Fig. 7 scatter); gap widens Narrow -> Wide. RL fine-tuning baselines collapse.
Ablations (sim, Table I, success Peg / TableLeg): full 0.90/0.85; 50% sim data 0.72/0.61; 10% data 0.06/0.02; expert-only data (same volume) 0.10/0.05; per-step MLP reward/value 0.82/0.60; + pixel reconstruction loss 0.32/0.21 (quadruped reward: recon 23.34 vs 22.78, slight help). Unfreezing encoder or value during real adaptation hurts (figure only).
Failure/limitations: requires an accurate simulator + privileged RL expert per task; frozen value can cap performance. Real results are figures with 20 trials. Not an imitation-learning method.
Conflicts: pixel reconstruction as aux loss harms manipulation (0.90 -> 0.32) — agrees with KAI (future-depth aux weak) and against reconstruction-aux advocates; mechanism: reconstruction forces encoder to spend capacity on task-irrelevant pixels. Expert-only vs perturbed data (0.10 vs 0.90) is a strong datapoint that coverage of failures/recoveries matters — but for world-model planning, not BC.
Relevance: Low for our SO-101 BC pipeline (no sim of our scene). Transferable lessons: (1) do not add pixel-reconstruction aux loss; (2) diverse/perturbed data including recoveries is key for model generalization; (3) DP on 100 real demos is brittle over a wide init region.
Decision impact:
 - Q09 aux objectives: weakens pixel-reconstruction auxiliary loss for manipulation (0.90 -> 0.32 sim) — M (sim, world-model setting not BC)
 - Q13 data diversity: supports perturbed/recovery-rich data over expert-only (0.10 vs 0.90 at equal volume) — L for BC (different learning paradigm)
