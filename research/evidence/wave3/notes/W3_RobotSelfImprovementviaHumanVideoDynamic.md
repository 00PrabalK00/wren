# W3_RobotSelfImprovementviaHumanVideoDynamic — Robot Self-Improvement via Human-Video Dynamics Models (DGAC) (2026, arXiv 2606.21406)
Setup: Hello Robot Stretch 3 (5 tasks: socks, kitchen, microwave, table, basketball) + Franka (box, sweep); 15 trials/task. Policy/dynamics/value models pretrained on human videos (actions = absolute 6-DoF wrist pose + hand closure; state = DINOv3 tokens + 3D point flows), flow-matching policy with gripper-view input, small teleop set per task (count not stated in text I found), then autonomous rollouts; success/fail labels by humans. Compute not given.
Claim: failed rollouts can be converted into corrective supervision (retrieve successful reference, compose flow velocity fields, rank candidates with learned dynamics+value) → 40%→81–85% without human interventions.
Evidence (Stretch avg, 15 trials × 5 tasks): Expert BC 41.3; SWIM 49.3; LPB 60.0; AWR 60.0; RECAP (no human corrections) 61.3; RISE 76.0; Ours 85.3. π0.5+SFT 62.7 → +RECAP 68.0 → +DGAC 88.0. Franka: BC 36.7 → 70.0.
Ablations: w/o DGAC (just iterate on rollouts) 62.7; copy retrieved success trajectory 58.7; random candidate selection 52.0; median-value 57.3; min-value 48.0; VLM selector 64.0; full 85.3. Human-video pretraining scale 0% / 50% / 100% → 52.0 / 58.7 / 85.3. Removing robot adaptation reduces performance (figure only).
Failure/limitations: human-labeled success/failure; DGAC local, short-horizon only; heavy pipeline (human-video pretraining, point tracking, dynamics+value models); 15 trials/task → ±12 pt per task noise.
Conflicts: consistent with RECAP/AWR-style literature that iterating on own filtered rollouts gives +~20 pts over BC; here explicit failure repair adds another ~20.
Relevance: Low direct relevance for our first build (too heavy); conceptual takeaway for Q13: after initial 50–100 demos, a cheap loop of deploy → keep successes (+ fine-tune) already lifts 41→~61% (AWR/RECAP) on real robots — a practical data-flywheel with human success labels.
Decision impact:
 - Q13 data: supports iterative self-collected rollout fine-tuning (success-filtered) after BC — +20 pts (41.3→61.3) — confidence L/M (15 trials, different robot, not our architecture).
