# W3_SIDSlidingintoDistributionforRobustFewDe — SID: Sliding into Distribution for Robust Few-Demonstration Manipulation (2026, arXiv 2605.13428)
Setup: Real UR5e + Robotiq 2F-85, ONE wrist/end-effector-mounted RealSense L515 RGB-D (egocentric; calibrated to EE frame). 6 real tasks (Open Drawer, Pour Water, Hang Cup, Hang Tape, PnP-Box, Multi-PnP-Box), 2 raw demos/task expanded by egocentric point-cloud reprojection augmentation to 50–100 samples. Pipeline: SAM2/SAM3 target segmentation + pose → object-centric learned "motion field" that moves the EE into the demonstrated manifold; then PointNet-style egocentric point-cloud policy with conditional flow matching over action chunks; SID-closed adds OOD detection to re-invoke the motion field. Baselines: ACT, DP3, pi0.5 (100 demos), MT3, Ret-BC (10 demos). 50 trials/task (main), 25 (appendix).
Claim: Explicitly steering the robot back into the training distribution (object-centric motion field + egocentric view) plus kinematically consistent reprojection augmentation gives ~90% OOD success from 2 demos, robust to disturbances and clutter.
Evidence:
 - Table I, ID success (ACT / DP3 / pi0.5, 100 demos): Drawer 82/86/92, Pour 68/74/82, Tape 62/56/84, Cup 52/46/78, PnP-Box 56/72/76, Multi-PnP 18/24/64.
 - Table I, OOD object poses (ACT / DP3 / pi0.5 / Ret-BC / MT3 / SID-O / SID-C): Drawer 0/10/14/56/76/90/92; Pour 0/6/4/54/78/86/90; Tape 0/2/6/36/46/82/88; Cup 0/4/6/62/74/86/90; PnP-Box 0/12/10/44/58/84/92; Multi 0/0/0/36/52/82/86. → End-to-end ACT/DP3/pi0.5 collapse to ≈0–14% outside the demo placement region.
 - Table II dynamic disturbances (ACT / DP3 / pi0.5 / Ret-BC / MT3 / SID-O / SID-C): Tape 6/14/80/12/4/44/84; Cup 2/8/68/4/0/36/88; PnP-Box 2/10/74/8/2/52/86; Pour 4/10/78/6/2/40/82. pi0.5 is the only strong end-to-end baseline under disturbances.
 - Table III clutter (2 tasks, parse partial): Ret-BC 58/40, MT3 72/54, SID-O 86/84, SID-C 88/90 (segmentation filters distractors).
 - Cross-object (Table VI, /25, SID-O / SID-C): train obj A 20/22; B 14/17; C 9/11; D 4/6 — degrades with visual/geometric dissimilarity.
Ablations:
 - Egocentric reprojection augmentation (2 demos → 50): avg 89.7 vs 3.7 without (Drawer 92 vs 18, others 86–92 vs 0).
 - Egocentric (wrist) RGB-D vs fixed external RGB-D camera, same 50 demos, no aug, same architecture: 80.3 vs 26.0 avg (+54.3); per task ego 88/80/82/76/82/74 vs fixed 46/24/28/16/30/12. Caveat: motion field still uses egocentric obs in both, so the fixed-camera policy is handicapped in their pipeline.
 - Raw demos 2 / 10 / 50 (all augmented to 100): Tape 19/20/22, Cup 21/20/23, PnP 20/22/21 (/25) — diminishing returns after augmentation.
 - Motion field vs explicit pose alignment (open/closed): Tape 15/19/20? (Align-O / Align-C / MF: Tape 15,19,20; Cup 16,22,22; PnP 19,22,21 — ordering from flattened table, moderately confident).
Failure/limitations: heavy dependence on SAM segmentation + pose estimation and EE-calibrated depth camera; tasks structured as reach-then-local-skill; baselines were trained on narrow ID placement regions and then tested OOD by construction; lighting/background not tested; no latency numbers; UR5e is precise, unlike SO-101 backlash.
Conflicts: Strong wrist-view advantage agrees with wrist-camera papers (e.g. Hsu et al. "Vision-based manipulators need to also see from their hands") while contrasting with results where scene cam is needed for global localization — here the global localization is outsourced to the motion field. ACT/DP3 at 0% OOD pose agrees with the broad finding that end-to-end BC does not extrapolate spatially beyond demo coverage.
Relevance: We have a wrist cam; pumpkin placement variation beyond demo coverage will make ACT/SmolVLA fail (≈0%) — we must cover the placement region in demos or add spatial augmentation. The reprojection augmentation needs a depth camera on the wrist (we have RGB wrist only), so not directly usable; the lesson "segment target + object-centric view" is a plausible robustness route against lighting/distractor changes but adds SAM latency.
Decision impact:
 - Q11 cameras: egocentric/wrist view policy 80.3% vs fixed cam 26.0% with same data — supports relying heavily on wrist cam — confidence M (confounded by pipeline).
 - Q14 robustness: end-to-end ACT/DP3/pi0.5 fall to 0–14% on object placements outside demo region; segmentation-based object-centric pipeline keeps 82–92% and survives clutter — supports explicit object-centric/segmentation for spatial & distractor robustness — confidence M.
 - Q05 augmentation: kinematically consistent point-cloud reprojection augmentation 3.7% → 89.7% at 2 demos — supports geometric viewpoint augmentation (needs depth) — confidence M.
 - Q13 data: after augmentation 2 vs 50 raw demos differs by ≤3/25 — augmentation can substitute count — confidence L.
 - Q04 3D: point-cloud egocentric policy works with 2 demos; DP3 baseline no better than ACT OOD — mixed — confidence L.
