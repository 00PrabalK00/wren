# W3_SECANTSelfExpertCloningforZeroShotGenera — SECANT: Self-Expert Cloning for Zero-Shot Generalization of Visual Policies (2021, arXiv 2106.09678, ICML 2021)
Setup: SIM only, RL. Stage 1: SAC expert with weak aug (random crop). Stage 2: student distilled by DAgger-style imitation of the expert under STRONG augmentations (cutout-color, random conv, mixup, cutmix, Gaussian/impulse noise, random color), up to 30K steps, no reward. Domains: DMControl (color + video backgrounds), Robosuite (4 tasks; easy/hard/extreme test sets with unseen table/floor/object textures), CARLA weathers, iGibson rooms.
Claim: decoupling policy learning (weak aug) from robust representation learning (strong aug, supervised imitation) gives much better zero-shot visual generalization than training with strong augmentation directly.
Evidence: Robosuite (Table 3, reward): easy — door 782 vs best prior ~438, nut 419 vs 242, two-arm lift 610 vs 62, peg 837 vs 390; extreme — 309/138/377/520 vs prior ≤ 164. Reported +337.8% avg over prior SOTA (DR, NetRand, SAC+crop, PAD). DMControl +26.5%.
Ablations: student augmentation (Table 2, DMControl): mixtures (Combo1 = cutout-color + random conv + mixup + crop) generalize better than any single operator; Mixup and Cutmix best single ops for video backgrounds. Augmentation schedule (Fig 3, figure only): W→S (weak expert, strong student) > S-only (single-stage strong aug) and > W-only; DAgger rollouts for distillation > expert-only or student-only data (Table 4).
Failure/limitations: RL in sim with synthetic texture shifts; reward-based; no real robot; test shifts are texture/color, not camera pose.
Conflicts: consistent with Robust-visual-sim2real (texture diversity is what matters) and with the observation that very strong augmentation in a single stage makes the policy objective harder to fit (Diffusion Policy/ACT use only mild crop/color jitter). The two-stage idea maps onto BC as: train a teacher on clean images (or with privileged/segmented inputs) then distill into a student with strong augmentation — a lever for low-data IL not commonly used.
Relevance: For 50–100 demos, suggests (a) use a MIX of strong augmentations (mixup/cutmix with random backgrounds, random conv, cutout-color) rather than only color jitter, and (b) if strong aug hurts fitting, apply it in a distillation stage from a weakly-augmented teacher. Evidence is sim/RL, so low confidence for our setting.
Decision impact:
 - Q05 augmentation: mixture of strong augmentations (mixup/cutmix/random-conv/cutout-color) > single ops for unseen textures/backgrounds; weak→strong two-stage > single-stage strong aug (figure) — confidence L-M (sim RL only)
 - Q14 robustness: zero-shot texture/background robustness large gains in Robosuite (e.g., two-arm lift 610 vs 62) — confidence L (sim)
