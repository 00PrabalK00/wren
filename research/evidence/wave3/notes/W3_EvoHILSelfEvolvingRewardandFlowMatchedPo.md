# W3_EvoHILSelfEvolvingRewardandFlowMatchedPo — EvoHIL: Self-Evolving Reward and Flow-Matched Policy Optimization for Robust Human-in-the-Loop RL (2026, arXiv n/a in text)
Setup: real-robot human-in-the-loop RL (HIL-SERL/RLPD family) with pretrained ResNet-10 encoder, 128×128 cropped RGB, 10 Hz. Franka FR3 (4 tasks, 2 wrist cams [+side], 6-D EE delta twist) and SO-101 (candy push: top+side cams, fixed gripper, 30 demos; medicine-box stow: top+side+wrist cams, xyz + thresholded gripper, 30 demos). Components: SER (online-updated success classifier), AFS (flow-matching action chunks, H/E = 24/4 on SO-101, 8/2 or 16/4 on FR3, 5 Euler steps, BC + smoothness terms), retention-aware offline fine-tuning on generatively RELIT copies of recorded interaction (Uni-Relight / Cosmos-Transfer1) with α=0.75 original-light share + anchors. Eval: 60 trials per method per scene at 60% of a 10-level illumination shift index (brightness, color temp, shadow, reflection); baselines HIL-SERL, HG-DAgger, BC, IBRL, ACT with matched demos/interaction/sensors but NOT relit data.
Claim: adapting reward, action generator and visual domain (via relit replay) gives robust RL policies under lighting shift on two embodiments incl. SO-101.
Evidence:
 - Table IV (60% illumination shift, SR / duration s): SO-101 candy push — HIL-SERL 0.20/13.3, HG-DAgger 0.27, BC 0.07, IBRL 0.27, ACT 0.32/13.27, EvoHIL 0.93/7.80; medicine-box stow — HIL-SERL 0.15, HG-DAgger 0.03, BC 0.02, IBRL 0.30, ACT 0.45/36.01, EvoHIL 0.73/34.12. FR3 USB/RAM/wipe/breaker EvoHIL 1.00/1.00/0.87/0.83 vs HIL-SERL 0.50/0.43/0.33/0.13; ACT 0.38/0.30/0.23/0.40.
 - Sweep (Fig 12, figure-level numbers stated in text): candy push HIL-SERL 0.97 at 0% shift → 0.37 at just 10% shift → 0.10 at 100%; EvoHIL > 0.83 throughout. Stow: HIL-SERL 0.00 from 80%; EvoHIL 0.60–0.80. USB: HIL-SERL 1.00 → 0.00; EvoHIL 0.97 at 100%.
 - USB (single-step SAC backbone): best online policy 1.00 original / 0.50 shifted; + relit replay offline fine-tune → 1.00/1.00 without new robot interaction.
Ablations:
 - Relit replay × anchors (Table II, USB): mixture w/o anchors 0.77/0.67 (og/re); anchors w/o relit data (α=0) 0.50/0.67; both 1.00/1.00. α=1.0 (no relit data) shifted 0.88. Unanchored: shifted SR 0.00–0.07 over much of α range.
 - Anchors (Table I): none 0.77/0.67; feature only 0.57/0.97; action-mean only 1.00/0.80; both 1.00/1.00.
 - Flow chunk actor vs Gaussian per-step actor (SO-101): first-difference L2 −44.5% (0.387 vs 0.697 push; 0.483 vs 0.870 stow), high-frequency power −58.3% (push), −47.9% (stow). w/o AFS vs full: push duration 11.45 → 7.80 s. SER matters more for success: w/o AFS 0.88/0.69, w/o SER 0.70/0.47 (push/stow at 60% shift).
 - AFS objective (Table III, push, original light, 30 rollouts): no AFS 0.87 / 6.57 s; w/o FPO 0.93/7.24; w/o BC 0.87/5.23; w/o smoothness 0.97/6.02; full 0.97/5.44.
Failure/limitations: stated: single training run per config, checkpoint selection on eval protocol (optimistic), no held-out illumination (sweep = targeted adaptation, not zero-shot generalization), relighter must preserve geometry; relighting cost is hours per 8k-transition stream and ~40–49 GB GPU memory (Table VI; column alignment in extraction ambiguous). Inference latency not recorded. My read: baselines were NOT given relit data, so the lighting-robustness gap largely measures "trained on relit images vs not"; RL with human interventions is a heavy pipeline.
Conflicts: consistent with augmentation/generative relighting literature (e.g., GreenAug, RoboAgent, ROSIE-style) that visual coverage beats architecture for lighting robustness; ACT without augmentation collapsing under lighting is consistent with SmolVLA fragility we observe.
Relevance: HIGH (same SO-101 hardware, lighting shift, 30 demos, top/side/wrist cameras, 10 Hz). Direct evidence that (1) a plain ACT on SO-101 falls to 0.32–0.45 under moderate lighting change; (2) offline relit copies of our own recorded episodes (same actions) with ~25% share fix most of it; (3) flow-matched chunks halve high-frequency command jitter vs per-step Gaussian on SO-101. We can apply (2) offline without RL (relight dataset; cheaper photometric/illumination augmentation as fallback since generative relighting is too heavy for 8 GB).
Decision impact:
 - Q14 robustness (lighting): on SO-101 at 60% lighting shift ACT 0.32/0.45 vs relit-trained 0.93/0.73; HIL-SERL 0.97 → 0.37 at only 10% shift — supports explicit lighting augmentation/relit data; unaugmented policies are very brittle — confidence M-H (60 trials, same robot; single run).
 - Q05 augmentation: relit replay with ~25% relit share + anchoring to original-domain behavior: USB 0.50 → 1.00 shifted with original kept at 1.00; without relit data (α=1.0) 0.88 — supports mixing relit/augmented copies at a moderate ratio, keep originals — confidence M.
 - Q01 action head: flow-matched chunks vs Gaussian per-step: −44.5% action first-difference, −48–58% high-frequency power on SO-101 — supports flow/chunk head for smoothness — confidence M.
 - Q10 smoothness: smoothness loss term: w/o smoothness same SR 0.97, duration +0.58 s — weak support for adding smoothness regularization — confidence L.
 - Q11 cameras: SO-101 stow used top+side+wrist; no camera ablation — no evidence.
