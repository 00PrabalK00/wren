# W3_GloVLALetGeometryMoveandLocalVLAInteract — GloVLA: Let Geometry Move and Local VLA Interact for Robust Object-Centric Manipulation in Unstructured Environments (2026, arXiv id not in text)
Setup: Hybrid: object/basket centers (sim state in LIBERO; SAM 3 segmentation + calibrated depth on real UR10e) -> proportional geometric transport controller moves EE to a handoff region near the target (fixed offsets) -> local VLA (pi0, pi0.5, GR00T N1.6/N1.7) trained only on grasp/place clips cut from the same demos executes the contact phase. Obs: external + wrist RGB + proprio; 7D OSC pose actions; chunks native H (50/40/10), 8 executed. Sim: LIBERO-Object, LIBERO-Plus Object, new LIBERO-Challenge (50 eval-only scenes: clutter, similar distractors, illumination, camera/appearance shift, obstruction; 50 eps/scene). Trained on 8xA100. Real: UR10e tabletop, 10-20 trials per setting (90 total); real demo count not stated in extracted text.
Claim: Letting a geometric controller do long-range transport and restricting the learned policy to short local interaction dramatically improves robustness to visual/geometric shift and data efficiency.
Evidence:
 - LIBERO-Object clean avg (FullVLA -> GloVLA): pi0 87.4 -> 98.6; pi0.5 97.8 -> 99.6; GR00T N1.6 88.8 -> 98.4; N1.7 96.4 -> 99.6.
 - LIBERO-Challenge (GR00T N1.6): FullVLA 20.9 -> GloVLA 88.5 avg; medium 86.5, hard 74.9 for GloVLA.
 - LIBERO-Plus Object: GloVLA 88.2 avg (best on 6/7 axes).
 - Real UR10e (FullVLA / GloVLA): clean 90% / 95% (20 trials); easy clutter 30/90, obstruction 40/100, illumination 0/80, visual shift 40/100, distraction 20/100(?), layout ~?/80 (10 trials each; column alignment partly garbled); medium 10/80; hard 0/70; overall 35.6% -> 90.0% (90 trials). Inference time per episode 60.1±19.6 s -> 28.2±2.0 s.
 - Data efficiency (LIBERO-Challenge task 1): GloVLA 100% clean with 10 demos; 99.9% avg under perturbations with 50 demos vs FullVLA 48.5%.
Ablations: handoff parameters: robust to extraction radius (>80% across sweep); horizontal grasp offset tightest (collapses to 0-50% at -8 lambda); other offsets fine within ±8 lambda. As handoff moves farther, GloVLA degenerates to FullVLA.
Failure/limitations: needs reliable object localization (wrong center -> policy invoked outside trained region); fixed offsets; sim uses GT object state (so LIBERO robustness gains partly reflect perfect localization, which is immune to lighting/distractor shift!); real relies on SAM3 (open-vocab) — its robustness is doing much of the work. 10 trials per real condition. Only pick-and-place-like object-centric tasks.
Conflicts: Consistent with other "local/object-centric policy" results (e.g., cropping/wrist-centric observation improving robustness). Contrasts with pure end-to-end scaling claims: large VLAs (GR00T) collapse under illumination (0% real) and camera/appearance shift when trained on narrow demos.
Relevance: HIGH conceptually for our pumpkin->tray task: a RealSense depth + open-vocab segmentation (pumpkin/tray) could drive a scripted approach, and a small learned policy (wrist-cam dominant) handles grasp and place from demo clips. Directly targets our failures (lighting, moved camera, different pumpkin) and latency (fewer policy calls). Costs: SAM3-class segmentation on 8 GB GPU (feasible with smaller segmenters), calibration of scene cam (camera move breaks 3D localization unless recalibrated — but only coarse accuracy needed given broad handoff band).
Decision impact:
 - Q14 robustness: factorized transport + local policy real overall 35.6 -> 90.0; illumination 0 -> 80; LIBERO-Challenge 20.9 -> 88.5 — supports hybrid geometric approach + local learned policy for lighting/distractor/camera shift — confidence M (10 trials/condition; localization via GT/SAM3 carries much of the gain)
 - Q13 data: local-only policy 100% with 10 demos clean, 99.9 vs 48.5 perturbed at 50 demos — supports segmenting demos into local interaction clips — confidence L (sim, one task)
 - Q10 latency: per-episode inference time 60.1 s -> 28.2 s (fewer VLA calls) — L
 - Q11 cameras: local phase relies on wrist + external views near object; supports wrist-centric local control — L
