# W3_TacSushiTactileGroundedWorldActionModeli — TacSushi: Tactile-Grounded World–Action Modeling for Dexterous Sushi Manipulation (2026, arXiv 2609.19613)
Setup: Shadow Dexterous Hand Lite (13 actuators, 68 fingertip taxels), bare-hand MediaPipe teleop at ≤10 Hz (targets low-pass filtered, step-limited), logging 30 Hz; front + top workspace RGB. Backbone Cosmos3-Nano (pretrained world model, mostly frozen + adapters); tactile encoder fused via feature-wise sigmoid gate (hf = LN(hv + g ⊙ Wτ hτ)); action head predicts 16-step chunks of absolute actuator targets, execute 12 then re-observe. Training-only consequence decoder conditioned on the demonstrated action chunk predicts future RGB (33 frames), task progress, contact risk, tactile summary; removed at deployment. Data: 340 successful + 50 failed trials (3 tasks); failed trials supervise consequences only (action loss masked). Eval: 6 methods x 5 conditions (3 ID tasks, 2 held-out ingredients) x 20 trials = 600 rollouts; success = human(5)+VLM(3) composite visual score ≥3. Single training run per method.
Claim: training-only future-consequence supervision and gated (not concatenated) tactile fusion each substantially improve a deployed world–action policy, including on held-out ingredients.
Evidence (success ID avg / OOD avg): M1 GR00T (no touch) pooled 15%; M2 GR00T+tactile pooled 35% (ID 48.3 / OOD 15.0 implied by stated gaps); M3 Cosmos3 concat, no aux 15.0 / 5.0; M4 concat + aux 25.0 / 17.5; M5 gate, no aux 36.7 / 10.0; M6 TacSushi (gate + aux) 68.3 / 37.5 (rice ball 75, seaweed 60, surf clam 70; black rice 35, salmon 40). Pooled five-task: 15/35/11/22/26/56%.
Ablations:
 - Training-only consequence supervision (same deployed network): gate: 36.7 → 68.3 ID, 10.0 → 37.5 OOD; concat: 15.0 → 25.0 ID, 5.0 → 17.5 OOD.
 - Gated vs concatenated tactile fusion: no aux 15.0 → 36.7 ID; with aux 25.0 → 68.3 ID.
 - Future-vision reconstruction improves with consequence supervision (M5 → M6: MAE 5.6% → 3.8%, LPIPS 0.17 → 0.13) — diagnostic only.
 - Failed trials used for consequence targets with masked imitation (not separately ablated).
Failure/limitations: authors: one training run/checkpoint per method (no seed variance); ablations remove all consequence targets jointly (can't attribute to future-vision vs progress vs risk vs failure data); success defined by visual ratings, not task geometry. My read: 20 trials per cell with Wilson intervals of ~±20 pts; dexterous hand + tactile far from our setup; large pretrained Cosmos backbone.
Conflicts: consistent with GAM, and world-model-as-training-signal literature (future prediction helps as an auxiliary loss; no test-time imagination needed); consistent with Selective Perception finding that naive concatenation of extra sensors can hurt vs gated fusion.
Relevance: moderate for Q09/Q13 ideas, low for architecture. Transferable: (1) a training-only future-prediction/progress auxiliary head costs nothing at inference and gave the largest single gain here; (2) failed teleop trials need not be discarded — use them for auxiliary (progress/outcome) targets with the imitation loss masked (contrast TOTO: including failures in BC loss hurt); (3) gated fusion for any extra modality (e.g., depth) rather than raw concatenation.
Decision impact:
 - Q09 auxiliary objectives: supports training-only future-consequence (future RGB + progress + contact) supervision: ID 36.7 → 68.3, OOD 10.0 → 37.5 at identical deployed compute — confidence M (real, 100 rollouts per variant, single seed).
 - Q13 data quality: supports keeping failed demos for auxiliary targets while masking their actions — confidence L (not isolated).
 - Q14 robustness: held-out ingredient transfer improved with aux supervision (OOD 10.0 → 37.5) — confidence L-M.
 - Q12/fusion design: gated fusion of extra modality > concatenation (15.0 → 36.7 ID) — confidence L-M.
