# W3_RLDGRoboticGeneralistPolicyDistillationv — RLDG: Robotic Generalist Policy Distillation via Reinforcement Learning (2024/25, arXiv id not in text)
Setup: Franka with 1 kHz impedance controller; actions = 6-D EE delta pose (+gripper); single 128x128 wrist camera only; RL experts via HIL-SERL (real-world RL with human interventions) at 10 Hz; generalists OpenVLA (4 Hz) and Octo (10 Hz) fine-tuned on either RL rollouts or human teleop demos with SAME number of successful episodes; tasks: connector insertion (multi-connector), FMB insertion (+-1.5 mm), pick-and-place, FMB single-object assembly; 20 trials per condition (80 for 4 unseen connectors).
Claim: fine-tuning generalists on RL-generated data beats human demos by ~30-50% success and needs 6-10x less data; gain mainly from action quality.
Evidence (text, rest figure-only): OpenVLA +33% (FMB insertion), +23% (connector); Octo +10%/+37%. Pick-place OpenVLA 16/20 -> 19/20, Octo 1/20 -> 4/20; unseen pick-place Octo 0/20 -> 4/20. FMB assembly (RL data only for insertion phase) 20/20 vs 12/20 human. VGA connector: 100% with 45 RL episodes vs 300 human; human data plateaus at 90% on unseen Type-C even with 900 demos; RLDG 150 episodes -> 100% on unseen Type-C. Unseen connectors: OpenVLA-RLDG 73/80, Octo-RLDG 50/80, best RL specialist 49/80. RL specialist collapses 20/20 -> 1/20 on unseen pick-place; distilled OpenVLA 10/20.
Ablations: "human states + RL-relabeled actions" recovers most of the gap (>50% improvement at 25/50/75 trajectories on FMB insertion vs pure human; still below pure RL) -> action quality/consistency is the main factor; state coverage secondary. Human action distribution concentrated near zero ("hesitant"), RL actions point toward goal.
Failure/limitations: requires reward functions and a real-world RL loop; RL speed optimization caused early drops in pick-place; OpenVLA's 4 Hz slowed execution vs RL's 10 Hz (latency affects dynamics). 20 trials.
Conflicts: Strong evidence that demo CONSISTENCY/quality dominates quantity for precise tasks — human data plateaus even at 900 demos. Consistent with X-Diffusion (bad demos hurt) and SeedPolicy note (pauses in demos -> freezing). Octo's diffusion head underfit and grasped poorly "likely due to lack of depth perception" from a single wrist cam.
Relevance: For SO-101 teleop: human demos are noisy/hesitant; improving action consistency (smooth, decisive, consistent strategy; trimming pauses; possibly relabeling actions) is likely worth more than collecting many more demos. RL infrastructure itself is out of scope.
Decision impact:
 - Q13 data quality: supports consistent, decisive, near-optimal actions over more demos (45 RL eps = 300 human; human plateaus 90% at 900) — M (real, 20 trials, precise insertion tasks)
 - Q10 latency: lower control rate (4 Hz OpenVLA) slows cycle time vs 10 Hz — L
 - Q11 cameras: wrist-only policies work for insertion but Octo wrist-only failed grasps attributed to depth ambiguity — L
