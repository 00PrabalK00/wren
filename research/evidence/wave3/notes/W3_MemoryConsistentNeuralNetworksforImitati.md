# W3_MemoryConsistentNeuralNetworksforImitati — Memory-Consistent Neural Networks for Imitation Learning (MCNN) (2023/24, arXiv 2310.06171, ICLR 2024)
Setup/claim: SIM only (D4RL Adroit pen/hammer/door/relocate human+expert demos, Franka Kitchen 566 demos, CARLA 400 demos; mostly proprio/state inputs, visual variants via frozen encoders). Outputs of an MLP/BeT/Diffusion BC policy are hard-constrained to stay near the action of the nearest "memory" prototype (10–20% of data), bounding compounding error; reported consistent gains, e.g. Franka Kitchen p5 (all 5 objects) where baselines ~0 and MCNN+Diff gets a 4x improvement over the nearest baseline (exact p5 value lost in extraction), p1–p4 = 1.0 like Diff-BC.
Low relevance: no real robot, no image-policy chunking, no robustness to visual shift; the nearest-neighbour anchoring presumes in-distribution observations, which is exactly what breaks under our lighting/object/camera shifts. Only weak signal for Q01 (a constrained/regularized head reduces off-distribution errors in low-data regimes, e.g. pen-human MCNN+MLP > Diffusion, figure only). Ledger: one L entry.
Decision impact:
 - Q01 action head: memory-anchored MLP can match/beat diffusion on low-dim sim BC (figure only) — confidence L (sim, low-dim)
