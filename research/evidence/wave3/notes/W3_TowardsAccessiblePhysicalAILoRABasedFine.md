# W3_TowardsAccessiblePhysicalAILoRABasedFine — Towards Accessible Physical AI: LoRA-Based Fine-Tuning of VLA Models for Real-World Robot Control (2025, arXiv 2512.11921)
Setup: SO-101 (same arm as ours), overhead RealSense D455 (1280x720, 60 cm high, 40° tilt) + wrist USB cam 320x240, leader-follower teleop at 30 Hz, LeRobot v3 format. One task: press a controller button. 20 / 50 / 200 episodes (100 reported in results but not in dataset list). Model described as "SmolVLA … Phi-2 2.7B + SigLIP-SO400M, 3.1B, pretrained on OXE" with LoRA r=8 + 4-bit NF4, MSE loss, chunk 50, bs 1 x grad-accum 8, 5k (frozen vision) / 10k (unfrozen) steps on RTX 4060 8 GB (10–20 GPU-h). Deployment 20 Hz, 45 ms total latency, 6.8 GB peak VRAM. Trial counts for success rates NOT reported.
Claim: LoRA + 4-bit lets a ~3B VLA be fine-tuned and run on an 8 GB GPU with SO-101; data quantity is the main driver; frozen vs unfrozen vision differ little at 200 episodes.
Evidence (text, no trial counts): success 20 eps 18%; 50 eps 45–52%; 100 eps 68–72%; 200 eps 74% (frozen vision) / 76% (unfrozen vision). "Vision influence" (L2 change of actions when images zeroed): 20 eps 0.8 (frozen) / 1.2 (unfrozen); 50 eps 1.5/2.3; 100 eps 3.2/4.8; 200 eps 4.5/6.2. 200-ep failures: oscillation near target 40% of failures, wrong approach 30%, vision tracking 20%.
Ablations: frozen vs LoRA-unfrozen vision encoder: 74 vs 76% at 200 eps; 4-bit quantization "<2% degradation" (no table).
Failure/limitations: CREDIBILITY CONCERNS — the model description is factually wrong for SmolVLA (SmolVLA is ~450M with a SmolVLM2 backbone and a flow-matching action expert, pretrained on community LeRobot data, not Phi-2/OXE/MSE last-token head), success rates are given as ranges with no trial counts, and the 100-episode condition appears only in results. Single easy task, no baselines, no robustness tests. Treat numbers as anecdotal.
Conflicts: 20-episode failures (oscillation, policy ignores vision and follows proprioception) mirror copycat/proprio-shortcut findings elsewhere (VBT, PredVLA). Their 45 ms inference on an RTX 4060 conflicts with our ~2 s SmolVLA latency on an RTX 5060 — suggests our latency is a configuration/pipeline issue (e.g., CPU inference, fp32, network round-trip, or too many flow steps), not a hardware limit.
Relevance: High on setup match (SO-101, D455 overhead, wrist cam, 8 GB GPU) but low evidential weight. Practical takeaways: (1) with ~50 demos a VLA fine-tune may still barely use vision — measure a "zero-the-image" sensitivity diagnostic on our policy; (2) plan for 100–200 demos if success must exceed ~70%; (3) visual-token dropout 0.1 across views was used to avoid over-reliance on one camera (not ablated).
Decision impact:
 - Q13 data quantity: success rises 18% (20 eps) -> ~50% (50) -> ~70% (100) -> 75% (200) on SO-101 VLA fine-tune — confidence L (no trial counts, questionable reporting).
 - Q03 vision encoder: frozen vs LoRA-tuned vision 74 vs 76% at 200 eps; unfrozen uses vision more — confidence L.
 - Q10 latency: 45 ms/chunk on RTX 4060 8 GB with 4-bit LoRA VLA at 20 Hz — our 2 s latency is likely fixable — confidence L-M.
 - Q07 history/copycat: low-data policies rely on proprioception (image-zeroing changes actions little) — confidence L.
