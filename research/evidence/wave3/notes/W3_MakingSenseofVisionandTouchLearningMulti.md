# W3_MakingSenseofVisionandTouchLearningMulti — Making Sense of Vision and Touch: Learning Multimodal Representations for Contact-Rich Tasks (2019/2020, arXiv 1907.13098, T-RO extension of ICRA'19)
Setup: peg insertion (~2 mm clearance, 4 peg shapes); sim Kuka (SAI2/CHAI3D), real Franka + Kinect v2 RGB-D (128x128) + OptoForce F/T; 20 Hz; representation pre-trained self-supervised on 150k real / 600k sim random+heuristic states, then FROZEN; policy = small net trained with TRPO (RL, staged reward), NOT imitation; eval 50 rollouts per policy.
Claim: a variational multimodal (RGB+depth+force+proprio) representation trained with action-conditional self-supervised objectives (optical flow, next-step contact, EE pose, time-alignment pairing) makes real-robot RL on insertion sample-efficient and transfers to unseen peg shapes.
Evidence (sim square peg, completed-insertion rate, Fig 5 — numbers partly figure-only): Full model 78%; Deterministic encoder 24% (was 76% for 3-DoF actions in prior work; adding roll control broke it); No-pairing loss 22%; Reconstruction (VAE image recon) 36%; latent d=16 18%; d=256 drops "by more than a third" vs d=128. Modality ablation: removing depth is worst, removing RGB least harmful; none reaches full model (exact bars not reliably readable). Real: similar-to-sim success on triangular/semicircular pegs (figure); policy trained on triangular peg transfers to unseen hexagonal/square pegs at >70% (50 episodes each). Deterministic model overfits: test/train loss ratio 312x (contact) and 2514x (pairing) vs 13.8x / 5.0x for variational.
Ablations: see above — action-conditional predictive objectives > reconstruction (78 vs 36%); variational > deterministic (78 vs 24%); latent size has a sweet spot (128).
Failure/limitations: RL with shaped reward, fixed camera, contact-rich insertion — different regime from our teleop BC pick-place; sim ablations only; real results figure-only; ~7 h of real RL per policy.
Conflicts: consistent with later findings that predictive/dynamics aux objectives beat pixel reconstruction for control (e.g. state-prediction aux in "Capturing visual environment structure"); consistent with iDP3/DP3 that depth helps geometry-heavy tasks.
Relevance: low-moderate. We have no F/T sensor (Feetech servo current could be a proxy). Transferable: if we add an aux loss, predict action-conditional/predictive targets (EE pose, contact/gripper state, future flow) rather than reconstruct pixels; depth is informative for geometric alignment.
Decision impact:
 - Q09 aux objectives: supports action-conditional predictive aux (flow, contact, EE pose, alignment) over reconstruction (78% vs 36%) and variational bottleneck vs deterministic (78% vs 24%) — L/M (sim, RL, single task, 50 rollouts).
 - Q04 depth: removing depth hurt most in sim insertion (qualitative from figure) — L.
