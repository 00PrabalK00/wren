# W3_BayesianDisturbanceInjectionRobustImitat — Bayesian Disturbance Injection: Robust Imitation Learning of Flexible Policies (2021, ICRA 2021; arXiv id not in text)
Setup: V-REP sim table-sweep (2 and 3 boxes) + real UR3 table-sweep of 2 office objects; state = low-dim object/arm coordinates from AR markers tracked by a RealSense D435 (NO image policy); action = EEF x/y velocity; policy = infinite overlapping mixture of Gaussian processes (max M=5); demonstrations collected iteratively in K rounds of 2 demos (real: K=5 → 10 trajectories) with learned Gaussian noise injected into the expert's actions (DART-style); 100 test trials per method.
Claim: a multimodal (mixture) policy + optimized noise injection during demonstration gives both flexibility (multiple valid orders) and robustness to covariate shift.
Evidence: Sim 2-box (% of expert performance): MGP-BDI 92±10.7 vs MGP-BC 58±34.7; 3-box 90±14.3 vs 71±30.9; unimodal UGP-BC/UGP-BDI ≈0 (mean-averaged action reaches neither box). Real UR3 success (100 trials): UGP-BC 7±6.4%, UGP-BDI 18±19.9%, MGP-BC 51±18.7%, MGP-BDI 91±7%.
Ablations (2x2 factorial): unimodal→multimodal policy: 7→51% (BC), 18→91% (BDI). No noise→noise injection: 51→91% (multimodal), 7→18% (unimodal). Unimodal + noise learns excessively large (unsafe) noise because model error is misread as needed perturbation (figure only).
Failure/limitations: 10 demos, low-dim marker states, 2-D velocity actions, GP policy — no vision; multimodality is engineered (sweep order). Noise injection requires perturbing the teleop'd robot during collection (feasible on SO-101 leader/follower in principle, but safety/jitter issues). Very small scale; old (2021).
Conflicts: agrees with ACT (CVAE needed on human data) and Diffusion Policy (unimodal regression averages modes) that multimodal heads matter when demos contain discrete choices; agrees with DART that recovery data (perturbation) reduces compounding error. For a single pick-place with a consistent strategy, the multimodality benefit is smaller than for a sweep-order task.
Relevance: Two transferable lessons for 50–100 SO-101 demos: (1) if operators take different routes/grasp sides, an averaging head (MSE/L1 without latent) can fail between modes; (2) injecting small perturbations during teleop collection (follower offset from leader) to capture corrections is a cheap robustness lever.
Decision impact:
 - Q01 action head: supports multimodal head over unimodal regression when demos contain discrete choices (real 7→51%, 18→91%) — confidence M (real robot, 100 trials, but GP on low-dim state).
 - Q13 data quality: supports noise-injected/recovery demonstrations (real 51→91%) — confidence M (real robot, but tiny low-dim setting).
