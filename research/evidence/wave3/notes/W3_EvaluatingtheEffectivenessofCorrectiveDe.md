# W3_EvaluatingtheEffectivenessofCorrectiveDe — Evaluating the Effectiveness of Corrective Demonstrations and a Low-Cost Sensor for Dexterous Manipulation (2022, arXiv n/a in text)
Setup: SIM ONLY, Adroit 24-DoF hand relocate-ball task, DAPG (BC pre-train + natural policy gradient RL with demo term), state-based; 30 demos total per policy; train in 0.5 m "full" space, originals from 0.3 m "restrictive" space; corrective demos recorded at the failure states of a policy trained on originals; 1,000 eval initial states. Also LeapMotion ($90) vs CyberGlove ($13k) demos (25 each).
Claim: corrective demos (targeted at policy failures outside the training distribution) beat randomly sampled additional demos when they make up the majority of the data; no difference when they are the minority. Cheap noisy hand tracker demos reach equal final success but slower.
Evidence (Table III, success % at 200 / 400 / 600 RL iterations): 30-O (restrictive only) 1.1 / 21.2 / 72.6; 10-O+20-R 7.0 / 36.0 / 75.6; 10-O+20-C 66.6 / 92.7 / 97.9; 20-O+10-R 3.2 / 59.4 / 96.0; 20-O+10-C 2.7 / 55.8 / 98.4. All combined policies >90% by 800 iterations; 10-O+20-C +5.4 pts final vs 10-O+20-R. LeapMotion: comparable final success, ~600 vs ~200 iterations to converge (figure only). Pilot: policy trained on restrictive-space demos: >95% in-distribution, 69% in full space.
Ablations: proportion of corrective vs original demos (above).
Failure/limitations: sim, RL-in-the-loop (not pure BC), single task, single seed apparently; corrective-demo gains mostly in sample efficiency of RL, not pure imitation.
Conflicts: consistent with DAgger/HG-DAgger-style findings that targeted recovery data helps more than more of the same; contrasts with papers finding no gain from few corrections — here the benefit appears only when corrections are ≥2/3 of the data.
Relevance: low-moderate: supports spending our extra demos on (a) the workspace regions/positions where the current policy fails and (b) broad initial-state coverage, rather than repeating nominal demos. Pure BC transfer uncertain.
Decision impact:
 - Q13 data: coverage outside the training region matters (restrictive-only 69% in full space); corrective demos at failure states ≫ random extra demos early (66.6 vs 7.0% at 200 iters) when they are the majority, no difference when minority — supports targeted/corrective demo collection over random extra demos — confidence L (sim, DAPG RL).
