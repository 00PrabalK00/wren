# W3_VIMAGeneralRobotManipulationwithMultimod — VIMA: General Robot Manipulation with Multimodal Prompts (2023, arXiv 2210.03094)
Setup: VIMA-Bench (Ravens/PyBullet UR5 sim, suction/spatula), 17 task templates, 600K+ scripted-oracle trajectories, high-level SE(2) pick-and-place/push primitives (not continuous control); object-centric tokens from Mask R-CNN crops, T5-Base prompt encoder, cross-attention decoder, discretized pose tokens; 2M–200M params. Sim only; no real robot.
Claim: multimodal (text+image) prompts + object tokens + cross-attention give a scalable, data-efficient generalist; up to 2.9x over alternatives at L4 zero-shot, 2.7x with 10x less data.
Evidence: all headline numbers are in figures (Fig. 4–7); extracted text has no result tables and the appendix is truncated. Figure only, qualitative: VIMA > VIMA-Gato (patches), VIMA-Flamingo (Perceiver), VIMA-GPT (single image token) at every model size 2M–200M; with 1% data VIMA ~ baselines at 10% on L1/L2; at L4 VIMA with 1% data surpasses others with full data.
Ablations (qualitative, figure only): object tokens > image patches / Image Perceiver / single-image-token; downsampling objects to fixed tokens (Object Perceiver) much worse; Mask R-CNN detections ~ oracle boxes thanks to false-positive detection augmentation; cross-attention prompt conditioning > GPT-style concatenation mainly at small model sizes and for novel tasks; T5 small/base/large prompt encoders no significant difference; "minimal degradation" with more distractors (appendix, not available).
Failure/limitations: scripted oracle data, primitive action space, sim-only procedurally generated objects; no continuous control, chunking, or real-world shift. Numbers not recoverable from text.
Conflicts: supports the object-centric/mid-level-representation argument (Sax et al.) that pixels overfit at low data; agrees with other notes that language-encoder size barely matters for simple instruction sets.
Relevance: low for continuous SO-101 control. Two weak takeaways: (i) for adding a 2nd object with language, cross-attention/FiLM-style conditioning to a small frozen text encoder is enough (T5 size irrelevant); (ii) object-centric inputs reduce overfitting in small models.
Decision impact:
 - Q08 language conditioning: cross-attention conditioning better than token concatenation for small models; text encoder size (30M–368M) irrelevant — confidence L (sim, figure only, primitive actions).
 - Q12 model size: object-centric small models (2M–200M) retain advantage at low capacity — confidence L.
