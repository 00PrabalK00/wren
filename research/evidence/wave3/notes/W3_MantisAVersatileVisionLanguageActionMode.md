# W3_MantisAVersatileVisionLanguageActionMode — Mantis: A Versatile Vision-Language-Action Model with Disentangled Visual Foresight (2025, arXiv 2511.16175)
Setup: 5.8B VLA (Qwen2.5-VL 3.7B backbone, 1.4B Sana DiT foresight head, 0.3B DiT action head, 0.3B VAE); pretrain SSV2 220K human videos → DROID 76K episodes → + 38 image-text datasets; LIBERO fine-tune 30 epochs (50 trials x 10 tasks per suite); real Agilex platform, 3 scenarios x (4 ID + 4 OOD instructions) x 10 executions, compared with pi0.5 (figure only). Action head 10 diffusion steps; temporal ensembling at inference.
Claim: predicting future frames through a separate DiT head fed with the current frame via residual (so queries learn "latent actions") improves action learning and convergence; mixing language supervision preserves instruction following.
Evidence: LIBERO avg 96.7% (claimed best vs pi0, UniVLA, etc.; per-baseline table split in extraction). Convergence (Fig 5, figure): Mantis converges about as fast as OpenVLA/UniVLA; UnifiedVLA (entangled pixel foresight) stays at 0% for first 10 epochs. Real (Fig 6, figure only): Mantis > pi0.5 on ID and OOD instructions; pi0.5 near-zero on OOD instructions.
Ablations (Table 2, LIBERO Spatial/Object/Goal/Long/Avg, trained from scratch except last): no-DVF 93.4/93.0/90.4/88.2/91.3; DVF without residual 96.8/97.0/93.8/89.8/94.4; DVF 98.2/98.8/93.6/92.2/95.7; video-pretrained DVF 98.4/99.0/94.0/93.2/96.2. Adaptive temporal ensemble (only ensemble when instruction-relevant patches overlap moving patches): ~50% fewer inference calls with comparable SR (Fig 8).
Failure/limitations: critical read: model far exceeds 8 GB; LIBERO is saturated (differences of 1–5 points); real results figure-only, few trials; the foresight benefit is shown only in sim.
Conflicts: agrees with other future-prediction auxiliary-loss papers (e.g. GR-1, UniPi-style, "dense visual supervision complements sparse actions") that foresight is a useful auxiliary target; shows that entangling pixel prediction into the backbone slows convergence — the auxiliary head should be separate and dropped at inference.
Relevance: low for direct use (size). Transferable ideas: (1) auxiliary future-frame (or future-feature) prediction head that is removed at inference, predicting the residual from current frame; (2) activate temporal ensembling only during fine phases (grasp) to save compute.
Decision impact:
 - Q09 auxiliary objectives: future-frame prediction head (disentangled, residual) +4.4 avg LIBERO (91.3→95.7), +0.5 more with video pretraining — confidence L-M (sim, saturated benchmark, huge model).
 - Q10 latency: adaptive temporal ensembling halves inference calls at equal SR — confidence L.
 - Q12 model size: 5.8B; not applicable to 8 GB — confidence L.
