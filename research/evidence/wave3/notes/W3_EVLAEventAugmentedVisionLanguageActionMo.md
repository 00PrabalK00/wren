# W3_EVLAEventAugmentedVisionLanguageActionMo — E-VLA: Event-augmented Vision-Language-Action Model for Dark and Blurred Scenes (2026, arXiv 2604.04834)
Setup: Real LeRobot SO100 leader/follower (same family as SO-101); SINGLE wrist camera = DAVIS346 (RGB + events, 346x260, 30 FPS, wide-angle 2.2 mm lens, >100° FOV); SmolVLA (~0.5B, SigLIP) backbone with VLM FROZEN, only action expert + projections (+13M-param event adapter) trained, 20k iters on one A800; inference on Jetson AGX Orin, synchronous, chunk 40 @ 30 Hz (1.33 s). Data: 724 episodes, 3 tasks (Pick-Place, Sorting, Stacking), multi-task multi-illumination (305 eps at 200 lux + 419 at 100/75/40 lux) → roughly ~240 eps/task. Eval: 10 fixed test configs per condition, partial credit (pick/place).
Claim: adding event data (even parameter-free overlay on RGB) keeps a SmolVLA-style policy working in low light and motion blur where RGB-only collapses.
Evidence:
 - Pick-Place, trained on all illuminations (Tab 1; 75/40/35/30/25/20 lux, avg): Image-only 100/80/70/35/0/0 (47.5); +RetinexNet 100/100/85/80/25/10 (66.7); +Retinexformer 100/80/80/75/20/10 (60.8); +EvLight 100/95/95/75/45/10 (70.0); +E2VID recon 80/60/55/10/5/5 (35.8); overlay 100/100/85/75/65/60 (80.8); event adapter 100/100/95/90/90/90 (94.2).
 - OOD lighting — trained ONLY on 200 lux (Tab 2, OOD at 100/75/40/30/20 lux): Image-only 100/65/30/0/0 (avg 39.0); Retinexformer 100/70/50/10/0 (46.0); overlay 100/95/55/10/10 (54.0); adapter 100/85/75/70/45 (75.0).
 - Motion blur (exposure 100→1000 ms, P&P): image 100/85/40/30/15/0 (avg 45.0); overlay 71.7 avg; adapter 74.2 avg. Sorting image avg 43.8 vs ~70.
Ablations:
 - Event window: time-based 5/20/40 ms avg 60.8/82.5/75.0; event-count 500/2000/4000 avg 67.5/94.2/85.8 → count-based windows robust to robot pauses/speed changes.
 - Training recipe at 30 lux: Action→Joint 75, Event→Joint 50, Action→Event→Joint 80, +patch-embed weight sharing 90.
 - Random dropout of the image branch used to prevent RGB shortcut (not ablated numerically).
Failure/limitations: events lose absolute color → Sorting placement errors; Stacking fails from wrist-camera occlusion by the grasped object (no event fix); needs a DAVIS event camera (not our hardware); 10 trials per cell; "normal" 200 lux images are already dark (gray ~60/255) because of the DAVIS's low gain — the RGB baseline is handicapped relative to a RealSense.
Conflicts: consistent with our observed SmolVLA lighting fragility: a frozen SigLIP VLM + action-expert fine-tune still degrades 100→65→30% as light drops from 100 to 75 to 40 lux when trained at 200 lux only. Image enhancement (Retinex) helps only moderately — supports the view that robustness must come from training distribution (multi-illumination data/photometric augmentation) or sensing, not preprocessing. Stacking occlusion failure agrees with work arguing a wrist-only setup needs a scene view.
Relevance: Same arm family and same base policy (SmolVLA) as ours. Most useful lessons: (1) including low-light demos in training moves image-only from 30%→80% at 40 lux (Tab 2 ID vs OOD) — i.e., collecting demos under several lighting levels is the cheap fix; (2) wide-angle wrist lens deemed "critical" (qualitative); (3) chunk 40 @ 30 Hz synchronous works on an Orin. Event cameras themselves are out of scope for us.
Decision impact:
 - Q14 robustness (lighting): shows frozen-VLM SmolVLA RGB policy on SO100 is lighting-fragile when trained at one lux level (100→30% at 40 lux, 0% at 30 lux); training on multiple illumination levels raises 40 lux 30→80% — confidence M (real SO100, 10 trials)
 - Q13 data diversity: supports collecting demos under varied lighting (ID vs OOD: 75 lux 100 vs 65, 40 lux 80 vs 30) — confidence M
 - Q05 augmentation: weakens image-enhancement preprocessing as a lighting fix (Retinexformer OOD avg 46 vs 39 image-only) — confidence L-M
 - Q11 cameras: wrist-only setup fails on stacking due to occlusion by grasped object; wide FOV wrist lens critical (qualitative) — confidence L
