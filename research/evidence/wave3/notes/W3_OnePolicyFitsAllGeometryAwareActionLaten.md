# W3_OnePolicyFitsAllGeometryAwareActionLaten — One-Policy-Fits-All: Geometry-Aware Action Latents for Cross-Embodiment Manipulation (2026, arXiv 2603.14522)
Low relevance: cross-embodiment co-training across 11 end-effectors (mostly dexterous hands) via a geometry-aware latent action space on top of DP3; real setup UR5e + Azure Kinect, 24 demos/embodiment/task, 10 trials. Gains (e.g. +19.8% avg over naive co-train in 9-embodiment spray picking, 8-demo few-shot on new hand) only matter when you have multiple embodiments' data — we have one SO-101 with a parallel gripper.
Only tangential takeaway: naive co-training with per-embodiment decoders can underperform single-source training (action conflicts); no evidence on our 14 decisions for a single-arm, single-gripper setup. No ledger lines.
