# W3_BeyondViewpointGeneralizationWhatMultiVi — Beyond Viewpoint Generalization: What Multi-View Demonstrations Offer and How to Synthesize Them for Robot Manipulation? (2026, arXiv 2603.26757)
Setup: RoboTwin 2.0 sim, 12 tasks, cameras on a hemisphere (1.70 m, 45 deg elevation, azimuth every 10 deg within +-60), 50 demos per view by default, eval always from the canonical 0 deg camera, 50 rollouts. Policies: Diffusion Policy (ResNet-18, 3-frame obs) on Franka; pi0 LoRA 5k steps multi-task on Aloha. Clean (white bg) and Random (11k textures) modes. RoboNVS: Wan2.1-14B LoRA novel-view video synthesis from monocular video + aligned depth (DepthCrafter + DA3) -> DW-Mesh render -> diffusion inpainting; ~50 s per 49-frame clip on a 48 GB RTX 4090; trained 1 week on 8 H200. Real: Franka + top-down RealSense D435, 3 tasks, 30 demos each from 0 deg, DP, 20 trials.
Claim: extra training viewpoints improve manipulation even when testing from the training camera (not just viewpoint robustness); moderate offsets (+-20 deg) best; synthesized views from monocular data give the same benefit.
Evidence:
 - Offset study (DP, 4 tasks AB/BBH/CA/CB, mean SR): 0 deg only 12.5; +-10: 29.0; +-20: 58.0; +-30: 42.5; +-40: 40.5; +-50: 26.0; +-60: 19.5.
 - Strict budget-matched (same trajectories, same count, each trajectory shown from exactly one view): 0 deg 12.5; +-10: 26.0; +-20: 33.5; +-30: 26.0; +-40: 23.0; +-50: 21.0; +-60: 18.0 -> the viewpoint distribution itself helps (~2.7x at +-20).
 - pi0 multi-task: seen 32.4 -> 40.7, unseen (fine-tuned transfer) 38.7 -> 46.0, overall 34.0 -> 42.0.
 - Frozen DINOv2 + policy head test MSE lower with multi-view in all 4 task/mode cells (e.g. 0.0128 -> 0.0070 range; exact cell mapping garbled).
 - 2D augmentation, budget-matched (25 orig + 25 augmented), 4 tasks at 0 deg: baseline 8/14/24/4 (mean 12.5); +-20 view aug 34/20/30/18 (25.5); perspective, rotation, crop, blur, mixed all ~4–10 mean (below baseline) — alignment of these columns reconstructed from garbled text, treat as approximate.
 - Real Franka, 30 demos, 20 trials (Bell / Fruit P&P / Lego P&P): monocular 25/10/5%; EX-4D synthetic views 40/30/20; EX-4D + depth alignment 50/40/35; RoboNVS 70/60/55.
Ablations: viewpoint offset (above); demos per added view 10 -> 50 steadily improves (figure only); scaling 10 -> 640 demos: single-view plateaus while multi-view keeps improving (figure only, qualitative); Bi-directional masking improves FVD 35.90 -> 31.40.
Mechanism: Grad-CAM — multi-view policies attend to gripper/objects, single-view to background; linear probe R2 for EE/object pose lower but nonlinear probe higher with multi-view; smoother training curves.
Failure/limitations: sim baselines are very weak (12.5% avg DP at 50 demos) so relative gains are inflated; 2D-aug comparison halves real demos which penalizes aug (and "crop hurts" contradicts DP practice); real experiments only 20 trials, only 0-deg eval (no camera-shift test in real), only top-down cam; RoboNVS generator needs 48 GB and ~50 s/clip; no wrist camera anywhere.
Conflicts: contradicts common view that multi-view data is only for viewpoint invariance. Conflicts with DP/ACT practice where random-crop helps — here budget-matched 2D augs hurt, likely because augmented samples replaced real ones rather than being added online. Supports the camera-shift fragility we see: single-view policies latch on to background.
Relevance: HIGH. We have a RealSense with depth => depth-based reprojection of our demos to +-10–20 deg virtual views is feasible offline (even the crude EX-4D + depth alignment gave 5 -> 35% on the hardest real task), directly targeting our "slightly moved camera" and background/lighting brittleness. Heavy generator won't run on 8 GB but synthesis is offline (rent a GPU) or use simple point-cloud reprojection + inpainting. Also suggests if we can mount a 2nd scene camera during data collection only, it may help even if deployment uses one camera.
Decision impact:
 - Q05 augmentation: supports novel-view synthetic augmentation of demos (+-20 deg) over 2D image augs; real 30 demos: 5–25% -> 55–70% — confidence M (3 real tasks x 20 trials + large sim study).
 - Q11 cameras: supports training with multiple (moderate +-20 deg) viewpoints even for single-camera deployment; too-large offsets (+-60) help less — confidence M (sim, budget-matched).
 - Q14 robustness: multi-view training reduces background reliance (Grad-CAM) and gains are largest in randomized-texture scenes — confidence L-M (qualitative + sim).
 - Q13 data: extra views of the same trajectories beat more single-view demos once single-view saturates (figure only) — confidence L.
