# W3_GeometricActionModelforRobotPolicyLearni — Geometric Action Model for Robot Policy Learning (GAM) (2026, arXiv 2606.17046)
Setup: backbone = Depth-Anything-3 Giant (geometric foundation model, 1.14B ViT-G) split at layer 12; 12-layer causal future predictor (210M) inserted there predicts next-frame latent geometry tokens + an action token from language (frozen T5), proprio, previous action; predicted tokens pass through remaining DA3 blocks → DPT future-depth head + small action head (8M, L1 regression, chunk C=8, 7-D EE deltas, executed open-loop). 1.4B total, 983M trainable (blocks 0–12 + DPT frozen). Losses: action L1 (λ3) + future-feature L1 (λ1) + future-depth (λ3; pseudo-depth from the GFM on real). Pretrain 784K trajectories (OXE 72%, MimicGen, RoboCasa365) on 64 GH200 for ~96 h. Inputs RGB only (1 external + 1 wrist, 224x224); augment 90% crop, ±5° rotation, colour jitter. Eval: LIBERO (500/suite), LIBERO-Plus zero-shot perturbations, RoboCasa-Kitchen; real: 4 tasks, 169–284 teleop demos each, wrist ZED + external RealSense, 20 trials/task (10 nominal, 10 with external camera moved 85 cm and rotated 45°); baselines π0.5, Spatial Forcing.
Claim: repurposing a pretrained 3D geometry model as the policy backbone (with latent future prediction) gives accuracy comparable to VLAs, much better camera-viewpoint robustness, and far lower latency.
Evidence:
 - LIBERO avg 97.6 (π0.5 96.9, OFT 97.1, Cosmos-Policy 98.5). LIBERO-Plus total 85.5 (↓12.1) vs π0.5 84.6 (↓12.3), Cosmos 82.4, OFT 69.6, π0 69.3, π0.5+Spatial Forcing 25.7, π0.5+ROCKET 47.5. Camera perturbation: GAM 83.1 vs best baseline 73.4 (+9.7 pts), OFT 56.4.
 - RoboCasa-Kitchen 24 tasks: 69.4 vs Cosmos-Policy 67.1, GR00T-N1.5 64.1, π0 62.5.
 - Latency (Table 4/8, GH200, bf16): GAM 6.9 ms with CUDA graphs (17.5 ms torch.compile only), π0.5 29.2 ms, OFT 70.1–77.8 ms, Cosmos-Policy 382.4 ms.
 - Real: figure only, qualitative: GAM substantially above π0.5 and Spatial Forcing in-domain and especially under the 85 cm/45° external-camera move.
Ablations (Table 2, LIBERO-Object orig / Plus):
 - Full (pretrain + depth + feat, H=1): 99.6 / 89.7. No pretrain, no aux losses: 93.6 / 50.0; no pretrain + both aux losses: 98.4 / 73.4; no pretrain + feat only 95.2 / 66.5; no pretrain + depth only 96.4 / 80.0. With pretraining, removing depth and/or feat losses ≈ no change (98.4–99.6 / 89.0–89.7).
 - Observation history H=1: 89.7 Plus; H=2: 84.4; H=4: 85.1 → longer history hurts robustness (spurious correlations).
 - Split layer (no depth loss): Ls=12 99.6/70.1; 19 95.6/63.4; 0 5.4/1.8; 27–39 ≈0 (collapse).
 - Action token through deep GFM blocks vs direct supervision: Plus 89.7 vs 84.1.
Failure/limitations: authors: language limited by frozen T5. My read: needs large pretraining (64 GH200-days class); real eval 10 trials per condition; depth used only as supervision target (pseudo-labels), inputs are RGB; action head is plain L1 regression chunk (C=8) — works here with large pretraining + filtered data.
Conflicts: supports "geometry priors → camera robustness" (like PolarNet/3D policies) but achieved WITHOUT depth input at test time — geometric pretrained encoder + depth/future auxiliary loss suffices. Auxiliary future-geometry loss helps mostly when there is no pretraining (50.0 → 73.4 on Plus) — consistent with world-model-as-training-signal papers (benefit is training-time supervision). History H=1 best agrees with copycat/causal-confusion literature. L1 regression head success contradicts ACT's CVAE necessity — difference: pretrained backbone + large data.
Relevance: high for Q03/Q09/Q14 even though the model is too big to train from scratch for us: a DA3/VGGT-style geometric encoder is a candidate vision backbone (DA3-Small/Base variants would fit 8 GB); future-depth auxiliary loss is cheap to add using our RealSense depth as target (we have real depth, no pseudo-labels needed). Latency: single-pass regression head avoids multi-step denoising.
Decision impact:
 - Q03 vision encoder: supports geometric-foundation-model encoder (DA3) over VLM/video backbones for camera robustness (LIBERO-Plus camera 83.1 vs 73.4 best, OFT 56.4) — confidence M (sim zero-shot; real small-n).
 - Q09 auxiliary objectives: supports future depth/latent prediction loss, especially without large pretraining (Plus 50.0 → 73.4; depth-only 80.0) — confidence M (sim, single suite).
 - Q07 history: weakens multi-frame history (H=1 89.7 vs H=2 84.4, H=4 85.1 on Plus) — confidence M.
 - Q14 robustness (camera shift): supports geometry priors + crop/rotation aug; real 85 cm/45° camera move handled better than π0.5 — confidence L-M (figure-only real).
 - Q04 3D/depth: depth as auxiliary target rather than input can confer viewpoint robustness — confidence L-M.
 - Q10 latency: single-pass L1 chunk head: 6.9–17.5 ms at 1.4B vs π0.5 29.2 ms, Cosmos 382 ms — confidence M.
