# W3_RobustVisualSimtoRealTransferforRoboticM — Robust visual sim-to-real transfer for robotic manipulation (2023, arXiv 2307.15320, IROS 2023)
Setup: UR5 + RG6 gripper, 2 RealSense D435 RGB cams (front + left, 90° apart), 10 Hz EEF velocity control; images 180x240; policy = ResNet-18 per view (from scratch), 3 stacked frames, proprio, MLP, MSE (velocity) + BCE (gripper), single-step. Training entirely in MuJoCo with scripted oracle demos (2000/task; 4000 for harder tasks) + visual domain randomization (DR); 7 tasks; 20 real trials per model/task. Real-data comparison: 150 real teleop demos for stacking (~4.5 h). Robustness scenarios: textured tablecloth, low room light, multicolor RGB flood lights, object color offset, camera pose noise.
Claim: well-chosen visual DR (high-quality textures, object color, camera jitter) chosen via a cube-localization proxy yields zero-shot sim-to-real policies (93% avg) that are MORE robust to real visual variations than policies trained on limited real data.
Evidence:
 - Real success, 7 tasks avg (Table III): 2D image aug only 0/20; +ambientCG textures 11.3/20; +light+object color 14.0/20; +camera variation 18.6/20.
 - Stacking robustness (Table IV; Default / tablecloth / low light / multicolor light / object colors / camera shift / avg): DR-sim 20/20/19/16/18/11 (17.3); Real-150 demos 20/1/17/14/19/8 (13.2); DR + real fine-tune 20/20/20/17/20/12 (18.2).
 - Sim design choices (Table I, avg over 7 tasks): single view+single frame 44.97% → +second viewpoint 65.43% (assembling +50.4) → +3-frame history 93.94% → +proprio 98.34% (sweeping +18).
 - Proxy localization error (Table II, 20k synthetic): 2D aug only 7.55 cm → +textures 2.52 → +light 2.66 (no gain) → +object color 1.62 → +camera 1.33 → +2D aug 0.95; 100k images 0.48; real-only 750 images 0.72 default but 3.08 under variations vs 1.39 for DR-sim; sim+real FT 0.14.
Ablations: listed above. Parameter sweeps (figure only): object HSV offset has sweet spot (~(0.05,0.1,0.1)); too-large hue shift confuses object identities; small camera randomization (±10 cm, ±0.05 rad, ±1° FOV) helps, larger hurts; lighting ranges insensitive.
Failure/limitations: scripted oracle demos (unimodal, clean), cubes/simple objects; single-step MLP head; camera shift remains the weakest case for all policies (11/20, 8/20, 12/20); physics gap failures (nut slipping). 20 trials per cell.
Conflicts: supports the Green-screen/RoboAgent/GenAug line that appearance diversity in training (texture/background) is what buys background robustness, while standard photometric 2D augmentation alone is insufficient (0/20 sim2real; 7.55 cm). Real-data policy was robust to object color change (19/20) but collapsed on tablecloth (1/20) — background texture is the most dangerous shift for a from-scratch CNN, consistent with CAGE (DP 0.00 on bg change). History of 3 frames helped strongly here (single-step policy) — contrasts with copycat/causal-confusion warnings, but those arise mostly with proprio history in real human data.
Relevance: Direct evidence for our failure modes (lighting, look of object, camera moved): a from-scratch ResNet trained on 150 real demos lost almost everything under a new tablecloth and ~60% under camera shift, but lighting changes were milder (17/20, 14/20). For us without a simulator, the analogue is background/texture randomization of real images (green screen / segmentation-based background replacement / generative aug) plus small camera-pose jitter augmentation; fine-tuning on real after diverse pretraining gave the best of both.
Decision impact:
 - Q05 augmentation: texture/background randomization is the key lever (2D aug only 0/20 → +textures 11.3/20 → full 18.6/20); photometric+shift aug alone insufficient — confidence M-H (real robot, 7 tasks, 20 trials; sim-to-real framing)
 - Q05 augmentation: small camera-pose randomization helps (14.0 → 18.6/20; 1.62 → 1.33 cm), large hurts — confidence M
 - Q14 robustness: from-scratch policy on 150 real demos collapses on new tablecloth (1/20) and camera shift (8/20) but tolerates lighting (17/20, 14/20) and object color (19/20) — confidence M
 - Q13 data diversity: appearance-diverse (synthetic) data > limited real data for robustness (avg 17.3 vs 13.2/20); diverse + real FT best (18.2) — confidence M
 - Q11 cameras: second viewpoint +20 pts avg (+50 on precise assembling) in sim — confidence M (sim, oracle data)
 - Q07 obs history: 3-frame history +28.5 pts for single-step policy (sim) — confidence L-M (sim, scripted demos; no copycat risk tested)
