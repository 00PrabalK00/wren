# W3_MimicPlayLongHorizonImitationLearningbyW — MimicPlay: Long-Horizon Imitation Learning by Watching Human Play (2023, arXiv 2302.12422, CoRL 2023)
Setup: real Franka, 6 environments / 14 long-horizon tasks (2000-4000 steps, 100-200 s at 20 Hz), two calibrated fixed cameras (left/right) + wrist camera; 20 or 40 teleop demos per task (RoboTurk phone) + 10 min human play video per environment (3D hand trajectory via stereo hand detection). Model: stage 1 goal-image-conditioned latent planner (ResNet-18 x2, MLP, 5-mode GMM over 3D hand trajectory) with KL feature-alignment human<->robot; stage 2 frozen planner + GPT-style transformer policy (history seq length 10, wrist + proprio tokens, GMM action head). Each ~12 h on one GPU; inference 17 Hz. Real trials: success fractions consistent with ~10 trials/task (inferred from granularity; not stated in main text).
Claim: a hierarchical policy whose high-level latent plan is learned from cheap human play (hand trajectories) and low-level from few robot demos beats end-to-end goal-conditioned IL on long-horizon multi-task manipulation.
Evidence (Kitchen, Table 1, "ALL" columns as extracted: subgoal@20 demos / subgoal@40 / long-horizon@20 / long-horizon@40): GC-BC(BC-RNN) 0.07/0.17/0.00/0.03; GC-BC(BC-trans) 0.07/0.53/0.00/0.03; C-BeT 0.37/0.47/0.00/0.00; LMP 0.20/0.37/0.07/0.13; R3M-BC 0.30/0.30/0.00/0.17; Ours (0% human) 0.60/0.67/0.23/0.40; Ours 0.83/0.90/0.47/0.70. Multi-task vs single (Table 3, as extracted): LMP 0.13 single -> 0.05 multi; R3M 0.25 -> 0.15; Ours 0.58 -> 0.55.
Ablations (Study Desk, 20 demos, Table 2; trained-tasks ALL / unseen-compositions ALL): GC-BC 0.00/0.00; LMP 0.00/0.00; Ours 0% human 0.20/0.10; 50% human 0.30/0.27; w/o KL 0.38/0.20; w/o GMM 0.28/0.07; full 0.55/0.47. GMM (multimodal plan head) is the single most important component; more human play helps generalization; KL feature alignment +17 pts.
Failure/limitations: authors: scene-specific play data; tabletop only; cross-embodiment representation imperfect. Critical read: small trial counts; baselines (GC-BC 0-7%) perhaps under-tuned; gains concentrated in long-horizon, goal-image-conditioned multi-task — not our single short task. Needs stereo calibration for hand tracking.
Conflicts: agrees with Play-LMP/LfP (hierarchy + latent plans help long-horizon) and with ACT/Diffusion Policy that multimodal heads (GMM/CVAE/diffusion) matter for human data (w/o GMM 0.28 vs 0.55). R3M-BC frozen pretrained features weak (0.00-0.30), consistent with Decomposing-gap result.
Relevance: low-moderate. Our pick-place is short; hierarchy unnecessary. Takeaways: multimodal action head matters with human teleop data; wrist camera + fixed scene cams + short history transformer at 17 Hz on one GPU is a feasible small-model design; human hand videos are a cheap extra data source only if we go multi-task.
Decision impact:
 - Q01 action head: supports multimodal (GMM) heads for human-demo data (w/o GMM 0.28/0.07 vs 0.55/0.47) — M (real robot, consistent across settings, small trials).
 - Q13 data: 10 min of human play video adds +23-30 pts long-horizon; 20->40 demos also large gain (LH 0.47 -> 0.70) — L/M.
 - Q03 encoder: frozen R3M baseline weak (LH 0.00/0.17) vs ResNet-18 trained end-to-end — L.
