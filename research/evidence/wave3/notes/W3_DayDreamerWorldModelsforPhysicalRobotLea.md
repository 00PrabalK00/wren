# W3_DayDreamerWorldModelsforPhysicalRobotLea — DayDreamer: World Models for Physical Robot Learning (2022, CoRL 2022)
Setup: ONLINE RL from scratch on 4 real robots (A1 quadruped, UR5 + XArm visual pick-place from sparse rewards with camera + proprio, Sphero navigation); DreamerV2 world model (RSSM) + actor-critic in imagination; same hyperparameters everywhere; learner and actor run in parallel for low-latency acting. No demonstrations.
Claim: world-model RL is sample-efficient enough to learn directly on hardware (quadruped walking in 1 h; UR5 multi-object pick-place 2.5 objects/min after 8 h, XArm 3.1 objects/min after ~10 h, approaching human teleop rate; Rainbow/PPO fail).
Not relevant to our decisions: it is online RL with hours of autonomous interaction, reward instrumentation and resets, not 50–100-demo imitation; the world model is the learning mechanism, not an auxiliary loss ablated for BC. Only tangential point: decoupled actor/learner threads for low-latency control (Q10), no numbers usable. No ledger entries.
