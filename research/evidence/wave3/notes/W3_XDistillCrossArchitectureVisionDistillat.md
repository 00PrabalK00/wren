# W3_XDistillCrossArchitectureVisionDistillat — X-Distill: Cross-Architecture Vision Distillation for Visuomotor Learning (2026, arXiv 2601.11269)
Setup: distill frozen DINOv2 ViT-L/14 CLS feature into ResNet-18 (11M) via MSE on ImageNet-1K (domain-agnostic), then fine-tune end-to-end with a Diffusion Policy head (obs history + proprio). Sim: 34 tasks (MetaWorld 29, Adroit 3, DexArt 2), 10 demos/task, 3 seeds, 20 eval episodes every 200 epochs, max SR reported. Real: xArm6, single web camera at 15 Hz, Meta-Quest VR teleop, 20–25 demos/task, 5 tasks with ID/OOD splits, 4–15 trials per condition; all DP variants 1500 epochs; pi0 SFT 30k steps (~20 h A100).
Claim: a CNN student distilled from DINOv2 combines ViT semantic priors with CNN inductive bias and beats scratch ResNet, fine-tuned DINOv2, DP3 and pi0 in the few-demo regime.
Evidence:
 - Sim average over 34 tasks: ResNet-scratch 64.1, DINOv2 ViT-S 66.2, Depth-Anything ViT-S 56.1, Theia 36.0, X-Distill 87.2, PointNet-DP3 (privileged cropped point cloud) 84.0. MW-easy(20): 76.6/78.5/68.2/50.9/93.9/90.4; MW-medium(7): 48.0/46.0/29.3/13.7/88.3/70.6; Adroit: 37.7/51.7/40.3/8.7/68.3/40.7; DexArt: 54.5/58.0/66.0/24.0/63.5/85.0 (same order). (Single-task hard/very-hard columns garbled in extraction; not used.)
 - Real (avg over 11 ID/OOD conditions): ResNet-scratch 41.9, DINOv2 31.4, pi0 SFT 26.7, X-Distill 75.6. Move Cube ID 66.7/26.7/0/93.3 (15 trials), OOD position 0/20/0/40 (5), unseen cube colours 50/20/0/70 (10); Writing "AGI" ID 40/0/0/100 (5), with paper dragged during writing 25/0/0/100 (4); Drawer OOD 13.3/13.3/33.3/53.3 (15); Door OOD 80/90/90/100 (10).
Ablations (MetaWorld avg of 29 tasks):
 - Student architecture at equal 11M: ResNet-18 90.7 vs ViT-S-Half 57.2 (-33.5) -> CNN inductive bias crucial at 10 demos.
 - Student size: ConvNeXt 89M 86.6 vs ResNet-18 90.7 (bigger slightly worse).
 - Teacher size: DINOv2-S 90.6 vs DINOv2-L 90.7 (no difference).
 - Qualitative: ResNet-scratch loops on the first letter (fails to perceive state change); DINOv2 and pi0 "tremble/hesitate" = underfitting.
Failure/limitations: tiny real trial counts (4–15), max-over-training evaluation in sim (optimistic), single camera, no lighting/camera-shift tests, no comparison to ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-18 (the most common default!) or frozen DINOv2 + small head, no R3M/VC-1 baseline. pi0 fine-tune on 20 demos may be under-tuned.
Conflicts: contradicts "use a big pretrained ViT (DINOv2/SigLIP) encoder" practice in the 20–25-demo regime: fine-tuned DINOv2 ViT-S was WORSE than scratch ResNet on real (31.4 vs 41.9). Agrees with ACT/DP defaults that ResNet-18-scale CNNs trained end-to-end work at ~50 demos, and with notes where VLAs underperform small policies at few demos. Missing ImageNet-ResNet baseline means the distillation benefit over standard ImageNet init is unproven.
Relevance: HIGH for our regime (50–100 demos, 8 GB GPU, DP/ACT-style small policy). A distilled ResNet-18 is 11M params, trivially fits 8 GB and gives low-latency inference. Unseen colour generalization (70 vs 50 scratch) is a hint for "different-looking pumpkin"; no lighting/camera evidence. Weights reportedly released (X-Distill.github.io) — cheap to try as a drop-in for ResNet-18 in ACT/DP.
Decision impact:
 - Q03 vision encoder: supports CNN (ResNet-18) with strong pretrained prior, fine-tuned end-to-end; weakens fine-tuning ViT DINOv2 at <=25 demos (real 31.4 vs 41.9 scratch vs 75.6 distilled) — confidence M (5 real tasks, few trials, missing ImageNet-ResNet baseline).
 - Q12 model size: supports small encoders (ResNet-18 11M > ConvNeXt 89M; > pi0 SFT 26.7 real) at 10–25 demos — confidence M.
 - Q14 robustness: object-colour OOD 70 vs 50 (scratch) vs 20 (DINOv2) vs 0 (pi0), 10 trials — confidence L.
