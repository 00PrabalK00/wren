# W3_ImmersiveDemonstrationsaretheKeytoImitat — Immersive Demonstrations are the Key to Imitation Learning (2023, arXiv 2301.09157)
Low relevance: simulation-only pick-and-place (rubber duck -> tray) teleoperated with a SenseGlove + Franka-rendered palm force; 10 participants x 5 demos x 3 grippers x 3 feedback conditions; policy is a 3-layer MLP on proprioceptive state only (no vision), MSE BC; no task success rates reported — only forces and execution times.
Finding: haptic feedback during demo collection reduces demonstrator (and cloned agent) grip/palm force, reduces force variance, and shortens execution time (Table I, demo times e.g. Franka ~20.5 s NFF vs ~17.7 s with fingertip+palm feedback; agent timings less consistent). Qualitatively supports "demo quality/consistency propagates to the policy" (Q13) but gives no evidence on success, vision, or our leader/follower SO-101 setup (which already gives some kinesthetic feel via the leader arm).
Decision impact: Q13 data quality: more consistent (lower-variance) demos -> lower-variance cloned behavior — L (sim, proprio-only, no success metric).
