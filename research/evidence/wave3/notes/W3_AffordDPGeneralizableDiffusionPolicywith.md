# W3_AffordDPGeneralizableDiffusionPolicywith — AffordDP: Generalizable Diffusion Policy with Transferable Affordance (2025, arXiv 2412.03142)
Setup: Franka + single side-mounted RGB-D camera (sim: IsaacGym/GAPartNet; real: teleop). Sim: PullDrawer, OpenDoor; object-specific setting 30 motion-planner demos/task at 3 variance levels; unified setting 5 objects x 20 demos. Real: OpenDoor, PullDrawer, Pick&Place, 4 objects x 25 = 100 teleop demos/task. Policy = DP3-style cropped/downsampled point-cloud MLP encoder + proprio MLP + affordance (3D contact point via SD-DINOv2 correspondence, post-contact trajectory transferred by PointSAM+ICP) -> DDIM diffusion; guidance pulls EE toward contact point when within threshold. Real trial counts not stated explicitly (percentages like 67.5% suggest ~40 trials/cell); sim Table 1 = 3 trials/object. Control rate, model size, compute: not reported.
Claim: conditioning a diffusion policy on a retrieved+transferred 3D affordance (contact point + trajectory) and guiding sampling toward the contact point generalizes to unseen instances/categories where DP and DP3 fail.
Evidence:
 - Real (Table 4, 100 demos/task, unified training), DP / DP3 / AffordDP:
   PullDrawer seen 0/10/67.5%, unseen inst 0/0/45%, unseen cat 0/10/40%;
   OpenDoor seen 0/20/80%, unseen inst 0/0/50%, unseen cat 0/30/50%;
   Pick&Place seen 0/40/52.5%, unseen inst 0/30/65%, unseen cat 0/15/50%.
   Image DP = 0% everywhere in real (authors attribute to needing >200 demos). DP3 "overfitted to specific positions", produces near-identical action sequences across placements.
 - Sim unified (Table 3) PullDrawer seen/unseen-inst/unseen-cat: DP 20.7/6.7/3.3, DP3 41.3/10.0/3.3, Ours 90.0/55.6/73.3; OpenDoor: DP 23.3/17.5/3.3, DP3 41.1/5.0/5.6, Ours 74.4/52.5/26.7.
 - Sim object-specific, 30 demos (Table 2) PullDrawer easy/med/hard: DP 80/16.7/13.3, DP3 80/13.3/16.7, Ours 80/33.3/26.7; OpenDoor: DP 76.7/26.7/30.0, DP3 66.7/36.7/20.0, Ours 90/53.3/50.0. At low placement variance all methods equal; gaps appear only as initial-pose variance grows, and all methods collapse at medium/hard variance with 30 demos.
Ablations (sim OpenDoor unified, seen/unseen-inst/unseen-cat): contact point only 72.2/42.5/17.8 -> +trajectory 71.6/50.0/22.2 -> +adaptive guidance 74.4/52.5/26.7. Small gains (few points), largest on unseen categories.
Failure/limitations: authors: foundation-model spatial understanding limits part-specific transfer; fails where affordances are hard to extract. Critical read: heavy pipeline (GroundedSAM, CLIP retrieval, SD-DINOv2, PointSAM, ICP) at inference - latency not reported; requires calibrated world-frame point clouds and forward kinematics; DP baseline at 0% in real with 100 demos suggests an under-tuned baseline (other papers get DP working with 50-100 demos); trial counts unclear; ablation gains are within a few trials.
Conflicts: DP=0% on 100 real demos conflicts with DP/ACT papers reporting high success with ~50-200 demos on single tasks; likely because unified multi-object training plus object placement randomization is harder, and baseline tuning. Agrees with iDP3/DP3 line that point clouds help spatial generalization, but shows DP3 still overfits placement with 100 demos.
Relevance: Pick&Place with novel-looking objects is close to our pumpkin -> tray. The key transferable idea for us is cheap: use an object-location prior (contact point from segmentation on RealSense depth) as an extra condition / guidance; full affordance pipeline is too heavy for 8 GB laptop latency budget. Evidence that plain DP3 on ~100 demos memorizes positions is a caution for Q04.
Decision impact:
 - Q04 3D/depth: weakens "plain point cloud (DP3) alone solves object/placement shift" (DP3 real 0-40%); supports adding explicit 3D object-location conditioning — L/M (unclear trial counts, possibly weak baselines).
 - Q14 robustness/novel objects: supports object-centric priors (foundation-model correspondence) for novel instances (real unseen-instance 45-65% vs DP3 0-30%) — L/M.
 - Q13 data: image DP 0% with 100 real demos across 4 objects, all methods collapse at high placement variance with 30 demos — more variance needs more demos — L.
 - Q01 action head: guided diffusion sampling (inference-time cost guidance) gives +2-9 pts on unseen categories — L (sim, small ablation).
