# W3_TheUnreasonableEffectivenessofDiscreteTi — The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Discrete-Time Gaussian Process Mixtures for Robot Policy Learning (MiDiGaP) (2025, arXiv id not in text)
Setup: Sim RLBench (5 mildly + 5 highly constrained unimodal tasks, 4 multimodal), 200 eval episodes/task; real Franka + wrist RealSense D435, 4 mildly + 4 highly constrained + 3 multimodal tasks, 5 demos (unimodal) / 10 demos (multimodal), 25 episodes each. Policy = per-timestep Gaussians over EE pose (20 Hz) in task-parameterized (object-frame) coordinates, mixture modes found by k-means/DBSCAN over whole demos; skills auto-segmented. Object frames come from ground-truth pose (sim) or DINO keypoints / FoundationPose (real). Trains <1 min on CPU; ~1 ms inference. Baselines DP (5 and 100 demos), LSTM, ARP, TAPAS-GMM — all given the same object-pose inputs.
Claim: a trajectory-level mixture of discrete-time Gaussians, conditioned on object poses, learns from ~5 demos, handles constrained/multimodal motions, and can be steered at inference time.
Evidence:
 - RLBench unimodal (Table III), avg mild/highly constrained: DP-100demos 0.67/0.48; DP-5 0.14/0.09; ARP-5 0.23/0.25; TAPAS-GMM-5 0.93/0.19; MiDiGaP-5 0.98/0.95. DP scaling to 400 demos still 0.79 ToiletSeatUp, 0.40 PlaceCups.
 - Multimodal (Table VI, avg): DP-100 0.46; ARP 0.40; MiDiGaP-15 0.86; +modal evidence 0.94.
 - Real (Table X-XII): LSTM/DP/ARP (5 demos) 0.00 everywhere; TAPAS-GMM 0.95 mild / 0.04 highly constrained; MiDiGaP 1.00 mild, 0.95 highly constrained, 0.99 multimodal, 1.00 on visual generalization (tablecloths, distractors, new instances) via DINO/FoundationPose task parameters.
 - Smoothness: 67% lower trajectory acceleration than TAPAS-GMM; DP much jerkier.
Ablations: partitioning strategies (DBSCAN fast but fails on OpenDrawer; k-means robust); no ablation of perception.
Failure/limitations: essentially open-loop trajectory replay per skill in object frames — no closed-loop visual feedback during a skill; relies on accurate object pose estimation (FoundationPose / DINO keypoint pipeline) and IK; residual real failures are self-collisions. Comparison vs DP at 5 demos is not informative for our 50-100 demo regime; DP at 100 demos in RLBench is a closer reference. Real DP 0.00 at 5 demos is expected. 25 trials per task.
Conflicts: Contradicts "deep generative heads are needed for multimodality" — explicit mode clustering over whole trajectories suffices when modes are discrete and few. Agrees with the broader finding that object-centric/structured representations (poses, keypoints) buy large robustness to background/distractor/instance shift (cf. KAI).
Relevance: A strong baseline/fallback for pumpkin->tray: segment into reach/grasp/transport/place, pose pumpkin & tray from RealSense RGB-D (DINO keypoints or FoundationPose), replay object-relative trajectories. Would sidestep lighting/pumpkin-appearance brittleness if pose estimator is robust, and trains on CPU. Weakness: SO-101 backlash/grasp slips need closed-loop correction which this lacks; IK on 5-DoF SO-101 arm restricts EE pose.
Decision impact:
 - Q01 action head: supports GMM-style explicit trajectory mixtures as a cheap, few-shot alternative when object pose is available (sim 0.95 vs DP-100 0.48 on constrained tasks) — M (sim+real, but different paradigm: pose-conditioned open-loop)
 - Q14 robustness: supports object-pose/keypoint intermediate (DINO/FoundationPose) for lighting/background/distractor/instance shift (real 1.00 under visual changes) — M (25 trials, pose estimator does the heavy lifting)
 - Q13 data: 5-15 demos suffice with structured object-frame representation; end-to-end DP needs >=100 — M
 - Q10 latency: CPU <1 ms per step inference, smooth trajectories — L (not comparable to closed-loop visuomotor)
