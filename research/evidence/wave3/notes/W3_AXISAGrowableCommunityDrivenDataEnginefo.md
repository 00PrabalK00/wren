# W3_AXISAGrowableCommunityDrivenDataEnginefo — AXIS: A Growable Community-Driven Data Engine for Scalable Robot Manipulation (2026, arXiv 2607.21588)
Setup: SIM data engine: browser MuJoCo-WASM teleop (keyboard/mouse/gamepad) of a Franka, 207 tasks, 50,129 episodes; cleaning (success re-check, static-segment removal thresh 5e-3, Savitzky-Golay window 15 order 3, cubic resample 6–8 Hz -> 20 Hz), state-replay rendering in IsaacSim with camera (±10 cm, ±8°), lighting, texture, scene randomization; 256x256 third-view + wrist RGB. Model: pi0.5 full fine-tune (Gemma-2B + 300M action expert), chunk 10, flow matching; 100k steps continual pretraining, then 30k steps LIBERO fine-tune; 8xA100. Eval: LIBERO-Plus 7 perturbation axes (rollout count K not stated). Real-world only qualitative.
Claim: diverse, cleaned, visually randomized community data as continual pretraining improves pi0.5 robustness, beyond a volume-matched sim corpus.
Evidence (Table 2/3, LIBERO-Plus success %): overall vanilla 83.9, +AXIS-25% 84.7, +50% 85.7, +100% 88.8, +RoboCasa-matched 57.5. Per axis (vanilla -> AXIS-100%): camera 72.5 -> 83.8 (+11.3), light 98.2 -> 96.5 (-1.7), sensor noise 82.5 -> 96.2 (+13.7), background 94.4 -> 98.1, layout 82.9 -> 85.5, language 89.6 -> 88.3, robot init pose 74.4 -> 78.2. RoboCasa-matched is far worse on every axis (camera 35.2, robot 39.4).
Ablations:
 - Data refinement (Table 1): raw 5 Hz -> smoothed -> smoothed+resampled 20 Hz: mean accel 1.354 -> 0.638 -> 0.489; mean jerk 11.59 -> 2.92 -> 2.22; BUT replay success 100% -> 91.4% -> 86.2% (smoothing breaks some trajectories). No policy-level ablation of smoothing.
 - Scaling: non-monotone on camera axis (25%: 77.5, 50%: 68.8, 100%: 83.8) — noisy evaluation.
Failure/limitations: sim-only quantitative; no ablation separating augmentation from task diversity; rollout counts unreported; the RoboCasa control collapsing to 57.5 (below vanilla 83.9) suggests catastrophic interference from that corpus rather than an AXIS-specific benefit — the comparison is not diagnostic. Light robustness was already saturated.
Conflicts: Consistent with domain-randomization/augmentation literature that rendered camera randomization improves camera-shift robustness; but gains are modest (+5 overall) for a 2B model.
Relevance: Low-moderate. We cannot pretrain pi0.5. Transferable pieces: (1) static-segment (idle pause) removal and light smoothing of teleop actions — but check replay success, because smoothing lost ~14% of trajectories' feasibility; (2) camera-pose randomization of ±10 cm/±8° gave the largest robustness gain on camera shift — supports camera/viewpoint augmentation for our "slightly moved camera" failure.
Decision impact:
 - Q14 robustness: camera-randomized pretraining data raised camera-shift success 72.5 -> 83.8 and sensor noise 82.5 -> 96.2 — confidence L-M (sim, VLA-scale).
 - Q05 augmentation: rendered viewpoint/lighting/texture randomization helps the matching shift axes; lighting already robust in pi0.5 — confidence L.
 - Q13 data quality: static-segment removal + smoothing reduce jerk 81% but reduce replay success to 86% — confidence L (no policy ablation).
