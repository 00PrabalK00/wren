# W3_FlowingWithPurposeLatentActionGuidedFlow — Flowing With Purpose: Latent Action Guided Flow Matching Policies for Robotic Manipulation (2025/26, arXiv id not in text)
Setup: Real Franka Panda, GELLO teleop, 4 multi-object long-ish tasks (plates->rack, drawer+screwdriver, cans->bin, stack bowls), 50 demos/task, 2 cams (external + wrist), 15 trials/task with randomized object positions. Sim: LIBERO-4 suites (1,693 eps, one unified model, 50 trials/task x 3 seeds) and LIBERO-90 (3,959 eps). Policy 0.11B: ImageNet ResNet18 image encoder, frozen ModernBERT text, 8-layer transformer encoder + 8-layer DiT decoder (cross-attn), chunk H=20 real (execute 15), H=10 LIBERO; 10 flow steps; trained from scratch, 200k steps, batch 448, ~20 h on one H100. LAM (latent action model, NSVQ codebook 512) pretrained on Fractal, 40 h H100, labels latent actions from external view with frames 1 s apart. Control rate not stated.
Claim: Replacing the single N(0,I) flow-matching source with a per-latent-action learned Gaussian prior (selected by predicting a discrete latent action from the observation) improves flow-matching BC.
Evidence:
 - Real (Table 1, SR avg / CS avg): pi0 3.3B fine-tuned 71.7 / 82.9; ACT 0.05B 40.0 / 51.7; FM 0.11B 63.3 / 75.0; LAFM 0.11B 86.7 / 93.9. Per task SR (plates/drawer/cans/bowls): pi0 86.7/60.0/66.7/73.3; ACT 40.0/13.3/60.0/46.7; FM 73.3/46.7/66.7/66.7; LAFM 100/80/86.7/80.
 - LIBERO-4 unified (avg): ACT 95.9±0.5, FM 95.4±0.3, LAFM 96.5±0.3, SmolVLA 88.8, pi0 86.0, DP 72.4 (per-suite experts).
 - LIBERO-90: DP 75.4, VQ-BeT 81.3, QueST 88.6, pi0 92.1, ACT 86.2, FM 82.6, FM+LAC 85.4, LAFM 93.0.
Ablations:
 - Structured prior vs auxiliary latent-action conditioning only (LIBERO-90): FM 82.6 -> FM+LAC (predict latent action, feed via cross-attn, standard Gaussian) 85.4 -> LAFM 93.0. Most gain is from the prior, not the aux label.
 - Codebook size 8..2048 (LIBERO-90, figure): inverted-U, LAFM > FM at all sizes (figure only; roughly 88-93%).
 - LAM training data (figure): Fractal alone best; OxE, OxE+Ego4D, Droid lower; Fractal+LIBERO worst (in-domain predictable data makes LAM latents uninformative). "Quality over quantity."
 - Denoising steps 10 -> 1: both FM and LAFM keep roughly equivalent success (figure, LIBERO-90) -> 1-step flow is viable, relevant to latency.
Failure/limitations: Authors: video LAMs capture camera motion / moving background, not only robot motion. Critical read: real eval 15 trials/task, no robustness (lighting/object/camera) tests; ACT in real was run with ACT defaults and chunk 20 executed 15 (no temporal ensembling stated) — ACT's weak real number (40%) may reflect tuning; in LIBERO ACT matches/beats plain FM (95.9 vs 95.4; 86.2 vs 82.6), so "FM > ACT" is not established in sim. Requires 40 h H100 LAM pretraining (they reuse a Fractal LAM, so could be reused). Compute-equal comparison with pi0 (same steps, much smaller batch for pi0) disadvantages pi0.
Conflicts: Supports that small from-scratch policies (0.11B) with ImageNet ResNet18 can beat fine-tuned 3B VLAs on 50-demo real tasks (agrees with several wave findings that small task-specific policies match VLAs in low-data single-embodiment settings). Contradicts the idea that flow matching strictly beats ACT/CVAE: on LIBERO plain FM <= ACT.
Relevance: Very close to our regime (50 demos, ext+wrist cam, LeRobot codebase, ResNet18). A ~0.1B flow/DiT policy trained from scratch is 8 GB feasible for inference; training at batch 448 on H100 is not — would need smaller batch. LAFM itself needs a pretrained LAM (released weights promised). 1-step flow result supports low-latency flow heads.
Decision impact:
 - Q01 action head: flow matching from scratch viable at 50 demos (FM 63.3 vs ACT 40.0 real), structured prior adds +23 pts real — supports flow/generative head, but ACT ~= FM in sim — confidence M (15 trials, ACT maybe untuned)
 - Q12 model size: 0.11B from scratch LAFM 86.7 > pi0 3.3B fine-tuned 71.7 real — supports small task-specific model — confidence M
 - Q10 latency: flow policy keeps performance with 1 denoising step (figure) — supports few-step flow for speed — confidence L (sim, figure only)
 - Q09 aux objectives: latent-action aux conditioning +2.8 pts (82.6->85.4) vs structured prior +10.4 — weak support for latent-action aux — confidence L (sim only)
 - Q03 vision encoder: ImageNet ResNet18 trained end-to-end sufficient to beat pi0 — confidence L (no encoder ablation)
