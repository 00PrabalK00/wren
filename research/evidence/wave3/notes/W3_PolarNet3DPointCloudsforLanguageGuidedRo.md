# W3_PolarNet3DPointCloudsforLanguageGuidedRo — PolarNet: 3D Point Clouds for Language-Guided Robotic Manipulation (2023, arXiv 2309.15596)
Setup: RLBench sim (Franka), 3 RGB-D cams (L/R shoulder + wrist, 128x128), keyframe ("macro step") actions executed by motion planner (<10 steps/episode); 10 / 74 / 18(249 variations) task setups, 100 demos/task; eval 500 episodes/task x 3 seeds (10-task), 25/task (18-task). PointNext-S encoder (ShapeNet init) + CLIP text + transformer (d=512, L=1-2), 2048 sampled points, 1 cm voxel downsampling; ~1 h training single-task on V100. Real: UR5 + RG6, 2x RealSense D435 (front + lateral), 7 tasks, 20 joystick demos/task finetuned from the RLBench multi-task model, 10 trials/task, MoveIt between keysteps.
Claim: a colored, workspace-cropped, multi-view-merged point cloud with a light PointNext encoder beats 2D (Hiveformer, Auto-λ) and voxel (PerAct) policies and is more robust to camera pose shifts.
Evidence (10-task single-task, sim): PolarNet 92.1±0.4 vs Hiveformer 88.4 vs Auto-λ 55.0. Multi-task 10 tasks: 89.8 vs 83.3 / 69.3. 74 tasks single: 69.0 vs 66.1 (Hiveformer re-impl); multi: 60.3 vs 49.2. 18 tasks/249 var: 46.4 vs PerAct 45.3, C2FARM-BC 42.7 (PolarNet has no history; Hiveformer uses history). Real: 60% avg over 7 tasks (Stack cup 8/10, Fruit in box 8/10, Plate 3/10, Open drawer 9/10, Item in drawer 4/10, Item in cabinet 4/10, Hang mug 6/10).
Ablations (10-task single-task, 100 demos, all else fixed):
 - Point features: XYZ only 72.1±4.4 → +RGB 91.3 → +RGB+normal 90.3 → +RGB+height 91.5 → all 92.1. Colour is the big one (Pick-Up Cup XYZ-only 26.2 vs ~96 with RGB: colour-disambiguation task).
 - Point removal: removing table+background 92.1 vs no removal 81.6 (Table 12 multi-task: 84.9 → 89.8). Same GT-mask removal on 2D Hiveformer HURT (82.8 → 73.9).
 - Coordinate origin: gripper-centred 92.1±0.4 vs point-cloud centre 89.9±2.8 (similar mean, gripper lower variance).
 - Cameras (point-cloud fusion): left 37.6, right 48.0, wrist 35.0, L+R 67.0, L+wrist 80.2, R+wrist 76.6, all three 92.1. Wrist weakest alone but most complementary (+>10 over two shoulder cams).
 - Capacity: single-task de=32 92.1 vs de=64 91.0 (small slightly better); multi-task de=32 77.4±8.6 vs de=64 L=1 89.3, L=2 89.8, L=4 86.3 → multi-task needs more capacity, but deeper hurts.
 - Camera pose perturbation (±f cm, ±5f deg on side cams, wrist fixed, no retraining): figure only, qualitative: PolarNet relative drop much smaller than Hiveformer at all f; <20% drop at f=30 on Push Button/Reach Target/Take Umbrella.
 - Data efficiency 10 vs 100 demos: figure only, qualitative: PolarNet degrades less than Hiveformer.
Failure/limitations: authors: multi-task < single-task; no generalization study to new scenes/objects; real failures = confusing semantically similar objects (strawberry vs apple — sparse points lack semantics) and imprecise grasp pose (partial views + multimodal action distribution, deterministic heatmap head). My read: keyframe + motion planner paradigm, not closed-loop continuous control — little to say about chunking/latency; camera perturbation is sim with calibrated extrinsics re-applied (point cloud in world frame requires known extrinsics — a moved uncalibrated camera would NOT be handled); real eval 10 trials/task, no 2D baseline on real robot.
Conflicts: agrees with DP3/iDP3-style claims that point clouds help viewpoint robustness, but disagrees with DP3's "XYZ only, no colour" choice — here colour is +19 points because tasks require colour disambiguation (DP3 tasks are mostly geometry-defined). Wrist-cam complementarity agrees with most wrist-camera ablations.
Relevance: we have one RealSense (depth available) + wrist cam; single task (pumpkin→tray) has no colour ambiguity but lighting shift would hit RGB features. Crop-to-workspace box is cheap and important (+10 pts). Only meaningful for camera shift if extrinsics are re-calibrated; point-cloud-in-world is otherwise not viewpoint-invariant. Small encoder (de=32) sufficient for single task → fits 8 GB easily.
Decision impact:
 - Q04 3D/depth: supports colored point cloud cropped to workspace over 2D multi-view — confidence M (sim, keyframe paradigm, large margins but RLBench).
 - Q11 cameras: supports adding wrist camera as complement to scene cam (single view −44 pts; wrist most complementary) — confidence M.
 - Q14 robustness (camera shift): supports 3D input for camera-pose robustness given calibrated extrinsics — confidence L (figure-only, sim).
 - Q12 model size: supports small encoder for single-task, larger for multi-task — confidence L (small differences).
