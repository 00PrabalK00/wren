# W3_MotionTracksAUnifiedRepresentationforHum — Motion Tracks: A Unified Representation for Human-Robot Transfer in Few-Shot Imitation Learning (2025, arXiv 2501.06994)
Setup: real robot arm (6-DoF EE control; 2 calibrated third-person cams at test). MT-pi = Diffusion Policy head on a pretrained ResNet image embedding + current 2D gripper keypoints (5 points). It predicts future 2D keypoint tracks + grasp per view, triangulates across 2 views and solves for SE(3) delta actions. Trained on 25 teleop robot demos + ~10 min human video per task (HaMeR hand keypoints, grasp heuristic from GroundingDINO+SAM2), with KL + domain-adversarial feature-alignment losses. Baselines DP and ACT get the same 25 robot demos with 6-DoF EE delta actions plus a wrist camera (which the authors say improved the baselines). 20 trials per task.
Claim: image-space motion tracks unify human and robot actions, so minutes of human video substitute for teleop demos.
Evidence (Table II, 25 robot demos): Fold Cloth DP 7/20, ACT 14/20, MT-pi 18/20; Fork on Plate 3/20, 5/20, 18/20; Serve Egg 7/20, 3/20, 17/20; Put Away Socks 10/20, 11/20, 16/20. Totals DP 27/80, ACT 33/80, MT-pi 69/80 (86%).
Ablations:
 - MT-pi with robot data only matches DP/ACT at equal robot demos (Fig. 4 heatmap, figure only). The gain comes from human video, not from the image-space action space alone.
 - MT-pi matches baselines with ~40% fewer robot-demo minutes when given ~10 min of human video. Teleop is ~5x slower than recording human video (100 robot demos ~65 min vs 70 human demos ~10 min).
 - Motions present only in human video (drawer closed leftward) transfer to the robot only with H+R training. DP/ACT get 0 on the unseen direction.
Failure/limitations: per-view predictions are not forced to agree, so triangulation errors appear, and demos had to be kept unimodal. Needs 2 calibrated cams at test and good hand tracking. Grasp labels from heuristics cause premature grasps. At 25 demos the DP/ACT baselines are weak and sensitive to start-state changes, but the authors say they rise with more robot data.
Conflicts: agrees with the "25 demos is too few for DP/ACT under start-state variation" picture. The fact that the image-space action space alone gives no gain means this is a data-source result, not an architecture result.
Relevance: medium. For the pumpkin task we could record ~10 minutes of hand videos with the RealSense (cheap diversity: pumpkin placements, other pumpkins, lighting) and co-train. That needs a second calibrated view for triangulation (we have only one fixed scene cam plus wrist, or the RealSense depth could replace the second view, which is untested here). High engineering cost. Worth it only if teleop time is the bottleneck.
Decision impact:
 - Q13 data: + ~10 min task-specific human video cuts needed robot demos by ~40% and teaches unseen motions (27–33/80 baselines vs 69/80) — confidence M (real, 4 tasks, 20 trials each)
 - Q11 cameras: ~ baselines used a wrist cam (stated to help); MT-pi needs two calibrated third-person views — confidence L
 - Q02 action space: ~ image-space 2D tracks + triangulation equal EE deltas with robot-only data (no gain alone) — confidence L (figure only)
