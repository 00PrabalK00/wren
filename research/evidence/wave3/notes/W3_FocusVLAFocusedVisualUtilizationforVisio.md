# W3_FocusVLAFocusedVisualUtilizationforVisio — FocusVLA: Focused Visual Utilization for Vision-Language-Action Models (2026, arXiv id not in text)
Setup: Builds on VLA-Adapter: Qwen2.5-0.5B Prismatic VLM (LoRA) + from-scratch 239M policy (343M trainable total), DINOv2+SigLIP visual tokens (512 raw -> 256 kept), chunk 8 (single arm) / 16 (dual). Sim: LIBERO 4 suites (500 trials/suite), RoboTwin 2.0 (100/300 trials). Trained on 4-8 A100, batch 64. Real: Realman 7-DoF arm, top + wrist cam, 3 tasks x 50 demos, 25 trials per variation (100/task) — results figure only.
Claim: VLA performance is limited by how visual tokens are used (policy-side attention), not by encoder quality; cascaded per-modality cross-attention + top-k patch pruning + element-wise gating fixes it.
Evidence: LIBERO multi-weight avg 98.7 (Spatial 99.6, Object 100, Goal 98.8, Long 96.2); single-weight 97.0 vs VLA-Adapter 95.6, EVO-1 94.8. RoboTwin avg Easy/Hard: DP 31/?, VLA-Adapter ~32/8, pi0 ~?; FocusVLA 58/15 (table columns partially garbled; FocusVLA best on both). Real: figure only, qualitative: FocusVLA > VLA-Adapter on all 3 tasks (fruit grasp w/ background variation, stack cups w/ spatial variation, place-left-block w/ target variation).
Ablations (LIBERO, multi-weight, avg SR):
 - Mixed -> cascaded attention (512 tokens, no gate): 93.6 -> 97.0.
 - Visual tokens kept 128/256/384/512: 96.3 / 98.0 / 97.8 / 97.0 — pruning to ~half helps, too aggressive hurts.
 - Gate none / 1-param / head-wise / element-wise: 98.0 / 97.3 / 97.1 / 98.2.
 - Visual representation (with full design): VLM feats 98.2, DINOv2+SigLIP 98.4, VGGT (3D) 96.8, VLM+DS 98.7. Fig. 4 (LIBERO-Long, figure only): all representations poor in naive mixed attention, all improved by constraints -> "utilization > representation".
 - Training convergence 1.5x faster than VLA-Adapter (5x on Spatial).
Failure/limitations: Authors: real success bounded by 50-demo data; when background color AND texture change simultaneously both FocusVLA and baseline drop substantially; non-standard initial arm pose breaks stack-cups; policy does not recover after drop (overfits to optimal trajectory). Critical: LIBERO near ceiling, differences of 1-2 pts on 500 trials; real numbers not tabulated; VGGT injected only at policy stage so 3D comparison not fair.
Conflicts: Agrees with the view that encoder choice matters less than fusion/conditioning when data is in-distribution (LIBERO); but LIBERO has no visual shift, so it says little about encoder robustness under lighting/object shift, where other studies find pretraining choice matters. 3D (VGGT) features did not help here, contrasting 3D-policy papers — due to late injection.
Relevance: Low-moderate. 0.5B VLA with LoRA is beyond comfortable 8GB training. Transferable idea: in a small policy, cross-attend from action queries to a pruned subset of visual tokens (top-k by attention) — reduces distraction from background; cheap. Their own failure: simultaneous background color+texture change breaks policy — i.e. architecture alone does not buy appearance robustness; augmentation still needed.
Decision impact:
 - Q03 vision encoder: DINOv2+SigLIP 98.4 ~ VLM 98.2 > VGGT 96.8 once fusion fixed; fusion design 93.6->97.0 larger than encoder deltas — weakens "encoder choice dominates" in-distribution — confidence L (LIBERO ceiling)
 - Q14 robustness: real background color+texture shift causes "substantial drop" even with focus attention — weakens reliance on attention/architecture for appearance robustness — confidence L (qualitative)
 - Q12 model size: 0.5B beats 7B OpenVLA-OFT on LIBERO — supports small models — confidence L
