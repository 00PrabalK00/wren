# W3_LIVLanguageImageRepresentationsandReward — LIV: Language-Image Representations and Rewards for Robotic Control (2023, arXiv 2306.00958, ICML 2023)
Setup: CLIP-architecture (ResNet50) vision-language representation pre-trained with a value-learning contrastive objective on EpicKitchens; evaluated as a FROZEN backbone + MLP language-conditioned BC policy on MetaWorld (1M random transitions), FrankaKitchen (5 tasks, 12.5K), and a real Franka toy kitchen (9 fruit→container tasks, 100 teleop demos/task, 6-DoF EEF displacement at 15 Hz, 3rd-person + wrist view, 10 rollouts/task, 1 seed). Baselines: CLIP, R3M+DistilBERT, VIP+DistilBERT.
Claim: a control-aware vision-language pretraining objective gives better frozen representations for multi-task language-conditioned BC than CLIP/R3M/VIP; LIV fine-tuning on in-domain data improves further (>40% relative, figure only).
Evidence: policy-learning results are figure only (Fig 4/5; numeric table in an appendix not present in the text): qualitative — LIV best in all 3 environments, largest gain on the real robot; baselines underfit on RealRobot (higher BC training loss) because they fail to ground which fruit/container the text refers to. Table 1 numbers concern reward-based planning, not policies.
Ablations: one-hot task encoding vs language embedding (appendix, not in text) — reported that LIV still helps even when language is unnecessary.
Failure/limitations: frozen ResNet50 + MLP policy (no chunking, weak head) — tests representation only; single seed, 10 rollouts per task on real robot; no robustness (lighting/camera) evaluation; ResNet-50 CLIP-era backbone.
Conflicts: consistent with "What do we learn from a large-scale study of pretrained visual representations" / R3M line that frozen PVR comparisons are sensitive to evaluation protocol; unlike CAGE/Theia it says nothing about OOD visual shift.
Relevance: Only relevant if we move to multi-object language conditioning (pumpkin vs second object): suggests a jointly vision-language aligned encoder grounds "which object" better than a vision-only encoder + separate text encoder. Our single-task case unaffected.
Decision impact:
 - Q08 language conditioning: jointly aligned vision-language encoder beats vision-only encoder + separate LM for multi-task language BC (figure only; RealRobot largest gap) — confidence L
 - Q03 vision encoder: frozen control-oriented VL pretraining > CLIP/R3M/VIP for frozen-backbone BC (figure only) — confidence L
