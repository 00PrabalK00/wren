# W3_P3POPrescriptivePointPriorsforVisuoSpati — P3-PO: Prescriptive Point Priors for Visuo-Spatial Generalization of Robot Policies (2024, arXiv 2412.06784)
Setup: xArm7 + xArm gripper, kitchen scene, ONE third-person RGB-D camera (no wrist cam), EE-pose + gripper actions, VR teleop at 30 Hz subsampled to 5 Hz, deployed at 5 Hz. 4 main tasks, 16-60 demos/task (8-15 per object instance; total 160). Human clicks semantic keypoints on 1 frame (<5 s); DIFT (diffusion-feature correspondence) transfers them to the first frame of every demo, Co-Tracker tracks them over time; points back-projected to 3D with depth -> flattened vector -> MLP -> BAKU causal transformer with observation history, deterministic head + action chunking with exponential temporal averaging. No proprioception for P3-PO. 10 trials per object per task (5 for distractor/depth tests).
Claim: replacing images with a handful of human-prescribed, automatically tracked 3D keypoints gives large gains in-domain (+43% abs), on novel object instances (+58%) and with background distractors (+80%).
Evidence (success counts; RGB = BAKU RGB, RGB-D = BAKU + depth token, GROOT = object-centric point cloud):
 - In-domain (Table I): Pick mug 13/40, 19/40, 7/40, P3-PO 39/40; Lift plate 22/30, 22/30, 7/30, 29/30; Mug on plate 8/40, 7/40, 0/40, 32/40; Take out bottle 5/20, 6/20, 0/20, 13/20.
 - Novel objects (Table II): Pick mug 6/30, 3/30, 4/30, 29/30; Lift plate 5/20, 10/20, 5/20, 18/20; remaining two tasks 0/20,1/20,0/20,16/20 and 2/20,4/20,0/20,10/20 (column assignment to mug-on-plate / bottle is ambiguous in the extracted text).
 - Background distractors (Table III, 5 trials): Pick mug in-domain 0/5, 1/5, 0/5, 5/5; novel 0/5, 0/5, 1/5, 5/5; Lift plate in-domain 4/5, 2/5, 1/5, 5/5; novel 0/5, 0/5, 1/5, 5/5.
 - Extra tasks: basket 80% (30 demos, 10 trials), open microwave 80% (22 demos).
Ablations:
 - Camera depth vs Depth Anything v2 monocular depth: 5/5 in all 4 conditions for both (Table IV) — predicted metric depth suffices for keypoint lifting.
 - Keypoints as flat vector vs fully connected distance graph: similar (Table V: 5/5 vs 5/5, 4/5 vs 5/5, 3/5 vs 4/5, 3/5 vs 3/5).
 - Adding a depth token to the RGB policy (RGB vs RGB-D baseline) gives little or inconsistent gain (e.g. pick mug 13→19/40, novel lift plate 5→10/20, but novel pick mug 6→3/30).
Failure/limitations: depends on correspondence/tracker quality (occlusion breaks tracking); loses scene context (clutter/obstacles); single task; failures attributed to depth noise. Critical read: baselines differ in more than representation (P3-PO uses history and no proprio; baselines use proprio and no history), baselines trained on only 16-60 demos with high object-instance diversity so they are in a data-starved regime; distractor/depth tests only 5 trials; no lighting or camera-shift test; heavy inference stack (DIFT diffusion model + Co-Tracker) — latency not reported.
Conflicts: Agrees with object-centric/keypoint works (e.g., ATM, GROOT claims) that abstracting away pixels improves instance/distractor robustness; contradicts the view that a depth channel alone gives robustness — naive RGB-D token barely helps; the gain comes from the object-centric abstraction. GROOT underperforming here conflicts with GROOT's own paper — authors attribute it to larger pose variation and centroid normalization.
Relevance: High conceptually for our "different-looking pumpkin" and background/lighting failures: a pumpkin + tray keypoint representation (DIFT/DINOv2 correspondence + tracker, lifted with RealSense depth) would be invariant to appearance. But on an 8 GB laptop, DIFT (Stable Diffusion features) + Co-Tracker adds latency; a cheaper variant (DINOv2 feature matching or a detector/segmenter centroid) is the practical adaptation. Monocular depth being as good as camera depth for keypoint lifting means RealSense depth noise is not a blocker.
Decision impact:
 - Q14 robustness: supports object-centric keypoint input for novel objects/distractors (novel pick mug 29/30 vs ≤6/30; distractors 5/5 vs ≤1/5) — M (real, strong effect, but confounded baselines and few trials)
 - Q04 3D: supports 3D keypoints lifted by depth over RGB-D image tokens; naive depth token adds little — M
 - Q03 vision encoder: frozen foundation features used for correspondence (not end-to-end) generalize across instances — L/M
 - Q13 data: 16-60 demos with 2-4 object instances suffice when input is abstracted — L
