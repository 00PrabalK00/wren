# W3_ActionControlNetALightweightDelayAwareAd — Action ControlNet: A Lightweight Delay-Aware Adapter for Smooth Asynchronous Control in VLA Models (2026, arXiv 2606.25985)
Setup: ACNet = small transformer encoder over the "delay action" (the d actions of the previous chunk executed while the new chunk was being inferred, padded with learnable tokens to H), pooled and added as a residual into the FINAL block of a frozen flow-matching action expert (Evo-1 lightweight VLA, 8-layer DiT expert); backbone features cached so many delays can be sampled cheaply; ~20% params trained. Sim: Kinetix (RTC protocol, d∈{0..4}), Meta-World MT50 (H=50, e=25, d∈{0,5,10,15}), RTX 4080 SUPER. Real: SO-ARM101 (our exact arm), 50 training rollouts, 10 epochs, 2 tasks (put cube in box, clean table with tabletop vacuum), 10 trials each, asynchronous execution. Baselines: Naïve Async (SmolVLA-style direct stitching), RTC (inference-time inpainting), Training-RTC (delay-conditioned full retraining) — the latter two on π0.
Claim: asynchronous chunk handoff error is a local boundary-conditioning problem; conditioning a frozen action head on the executed suffix matches full delay-conditioned retraining at a fraction of trainable params and gives smoother transitions.
Evidence:
 - Kinetix (Table I), success at d=0/1/2/3/4, avg d>0: Naïve 0.89/0.74/0.69/0.55/0.46 → 0.61; RTC 0.91/0.75/0.80/0.72/0.61 → 0.72; Training-RTC 0.89/0.88/0.83/0.79/0.70 → 0.80 (100% params); ACNet 0.90/0.87/0.84/0.76/0.68 → 0.79 (~20% params).
 - Meta-World MT50 (Table II), d=0/5/10/15, avg d>0: Naïve 0.80/0.71/0.70/0.70 → 0.70; RTC 0.79/0.72/0.72/0.71 → 0.71; Training-RTC 0.80/0.77/0.74/0.73 → 0.74; ACNet 0.81/0.76/0.74/0.73 → 0.74. Latency / control freq: Naïve 73 ms/13.6 Hz, RTC 159 ms/6.28 Hz, Training-RTC 134 ms/7.46 Hz, ACNet 91 ms/11.0 Hz (authors: latency gap due mainly to Evo-1 vs π0 backbone, not ACNet itself; RTC's inpainting guidance adds cost).
 - SO-ARM101 (Table III): Naïve Async 9/10 cube, 8/10 clean → 17/20; ACNet 10/10, 10/10 → 20/20. Qualitative: Naïve shows oscillations at chunk transitions that shift the contact point during cleaning.
 - Jerk (Fig. 4, H=50, d=10): flatter jerk profile around handoffs for ACNet (figure only).
Ablations (Meta-World, figure only, qualitative): injecting at final action-expert block best; early-layer (layer 0) injection degrades robustness. Learnable padding tokens > zero padding > noise padding at every delay.
Failure/limitations: my read: real-robot evidence is 20 trials with a 3-trial difference — suggestive only; no jerk numbers on real robot; backbones differ between ACNet (Evo-1) and baselines (π0) → confounded comparison; Meta-World gains over Naïve are small (0.70 → 0.74); delays only up to 15 steps.
Conflicts: consistent with RTC and Training-time RTC (Black et al.) that naive async stitching degrades with delay and that conditioning on the committed prefix fixes most of it; differs by being adapter-only. Naïve async degradation grows with d — directly the SmolVLA async failure mode we observe.
Relevance: HIGH. Same arm (SO-ARM101), 50 demos, lightweight VLA with flow-matching head, async inference — exactly our jerky-chunk-boundary problem. Actionable options for us: (a) condition the action head on the already-committed action prefix (ACNet/Training-RTC style) — cheap if we train our own flow/diffusion head; (b) inference-time RTC inpainting (no training, but ~2x latency here). Also shows Evo-1-class lightweight VLA at ~73–91 ms on a desktop GPU — far below our 2 s.
Decision impact:
 - Q10 latency/async: supports prefix/delay-conditioned action generation over naïve chunk stitching (Kinetix d>0 0.61 → 0.79; SO-101 17/20 → 20/20) — confidence M (sim numbers solid; real small-n; confounded backbone).
 - Q01 action head: flow-matching head compatible with cheap delay conditioning (residual on final block) — confidence L.
 - Q12 model size: lightweight VLA (Evo-1) runs at 91 ms/11 Hz vs π0-based 134–159 ms — confidence L (different hardware from ours, backbone-confounded).
 - Q06 chunking: H=50, execute e=25 with delay up to 15 still ≥0.73 success with conditioning — confidence L.
