# W3_DexterousImitationMadeEasyALearningBased — Dexterous Imitation Made Easy (DIME) (2022, arXiv 2203.13251)
Low relevance: teleop-system paper for a 16-DoF Allegro hand (MediaPipe single-RGB hand retargeting), 30 demos/task at 5 Hz; real-robot learning only compares nearest-neighbour INN/VINN (BYOL features) vs single-step state BC (BC fails all tasks from distribution mismatch; INN best; VINN solves 2 of 3; results figure-only), sim uses DAPG/BCRL RL fine-tuning. No chunking, no modern heads, no robustness/camera/latency evidence.
Only weak takeaway (Q13): dropping near-static transitions (<2 cm change) to remove operator pauses was used as data cleaning; and single-step BC from 30 demos fails — consistent with the need for chunking/history. No ledger-worthy decision evidence beyond that (L).
