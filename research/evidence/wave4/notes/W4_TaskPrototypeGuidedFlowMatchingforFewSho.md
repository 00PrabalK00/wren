# W4_TaskPrototypeGuidedFlowMatchingforFewSho — Task-Prototype Guided Flow Matching for Few-Shot Generalization in Vision-Language Robot Manipulation (2026, arXiv 2609.27780)
Setup: LeRobot SO-101 arm (our robot!), single RGB 224x224 + 10-D state + CLIP text; ResNet-18 encoder; flow-matching velocity net (6 gated-AdaLN blocks, d=512), chunk H=16, 8 ODE steps, 10 Hz execution, execute first action / short prefix. Trained episodically on "source" tasks (pick/place/push/close book/etc.), then frozen; target tasks get K in {1,2,4,6} support demos compressed into M=6 prototype tokens (no test-time gradient). Baselines: ResNet-T, Transformer BC, OpenVLA, ACT, Diffusion Policy, FlowPolicy, CFM, pooled-demo CFM, in-context flow. NOT reported: number of source demos, number of evaluation rollouts per cell, camera placement, GPU model, seeds, how ACT/DP/OpenVLA consume the K support demos.
Claim: Compressing few support demos into structured phase-level prototype tokens that shape both the flow prior and velocity field gives better few-shot adaptation, smoother chunks and 54 ms latency.
Evidence (as reported):
 - Few-shot SR@1/2/4/6 (Table 3): ACT 39.5/43.2/47.6/49.3; DP 45.1/49.7/53.8/55.6; FlowPolicy 48.7/53.5/57.9/59.8; OpenVLA 42.8/45.5/48.9/50.1; CFM (no support) 37.0/39.4/42.1/43.6; pooled 52.3/57.6/62.4/64.1; in-context 56.9/62.8/68.0/70.2; TP-Flow 66.8/73.4/79.6/82.1.
 - Efficiency (Table 6): TP-Flow 54.3 ms, 3.9 GB peak memory, context 6 tokens; in-context flow 91.4 ms, 6.8 GB, context 64; pooled 42.6 ms, 3.2 GB.
 - Action quality at 4-shot (Table 5): TP-Flow smoothness 0.052, replan error 0.031 (other rows not recoverable from text; OpenVLA smoothness 0.184).
Ablations (Table 8, SR@4 / noise-drop): full 79.6 / 6.2; w/o structured prototype 68.8 / 12.9; w/o reverse attention 72.5 / 10.7; w/o adaptive prior 73.5 / 9.7; w/o gated AdaLN 70.6 / 14.4; w/o contrastive 72.1 / 12.6; direct concat 69.8 / 15.1; in-context tokens 68.0 / 13.1. Prototype tokens M 1->6 improves then saturates; ODE steps saturate at ~8 (figure only).
Failure/limitations: authors: single embodiment; relies on support set consistent with query; contact-rich/deformable needs touch. Critical read: CREDIBILITY LOW — no trial counts, no demo counts, no seeds or error bars, suspiciously monotone smooth numbers across all tables, "task-completion signal" undefined for a real SO-101 (real photos shown in Fig. 8, but quantitative protocol unclear); baselines' protocol for using support demos unspecified, so ACT/DP numbers are not a fair comparison of heads. Treat as anecdotal.
Conflicts: Claims flow matching (FlowPolicy 59.8) > DP (55.6) > ACT (49.3) at 6-shot on SO-101 — direction consistent with some flow-vs-diffusion reports but unverifiable here; contrasts with Chronos where regression >= flow/diffusion at 50 demos.
Relevance: Same robot and a small ResNet-18 flow policy running at 10 Hz with 3.9 GB memory and 54 ms latency — shows feasibility of our 8 GB budget for a ResNet-18 + flow-matching head with 8 ODE steps. The few-shot/prototype mechanism is not needed for a 1-2 task setup with 50-100 demos.
Decision impact:
 - Q01 action head: reported flow (FlowPolicy/CFM variants) > DP > ACT on SO-101 few-shot — L (protocol/trials unreported)
 - Q10 latency: ResNet-18 + 6-block flow head, 8 ODE steps: 54 ms, 3.9 GB on SO-101 at 10 Hz — L/M (feasibility datum)
 - Q12 model size: small ResNet-18-based policy sufficient on SO-101; OpenVLA (7B) not better (50.1 @6-shot) — L
 - Q13 data: one-shot conditioning on 1-6 demos after multi-task source training (66.8 -> 82.1%) — L
