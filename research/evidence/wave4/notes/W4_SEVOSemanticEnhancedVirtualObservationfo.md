# W4_SEVOSemanticEnhancedVirtualObservationfo — SEVO: Semantic-Enhanced Virtual Observation for Robust VLA Manipulation via Active Illumination and Data-Centric Collection (2026, arXiv 2605.11114)
Setup: SO-101 arm on two mobile bases (Robot A: Jetson Orin NX, 2 body-fixed RGB cams front+side 640×480; Robot B: RPi 5, 3 body-fixed cams). Task: pick transparent water bottle, place aside/into basket. Leader-follower teleop: ACT 80 episodes (LeRobot default, ResNet-18 ImageNet init fully trainable, 51.6M, 400k steps, bs 24), SmolVLA 120 episodes (450M, frozen SigLIP+LM, 98M action expert trainable, 20k steps, bs 64); RTX 4090 training. SEVO = YOLOv8-seg yellow mask overlay (α=0.45) on RGB + 5 W red LED + diversified collection (backgrounds, lights on/off/dark, distractors, people walking through, null episodes with no bottle). 100 trials per condition.
Claim: observation design + diversified data collection (not model scale) gives cross-environment robustness; varied backgrounds is the #1 factor; body-fixed cameras beat wrist cameras.
Evidence:
 - Component ablation, body-fixed cams (Table II; parsed from extraction, consistent with text deltas; avg of train + similar-novel envs; ACT / SmolVLA): full (YOLO+red+variedBG) 90 / 79; red+BG 87 / 76; YOLO+BG 84 / 71; YOLO only 75 / 61; red only 66 / 53; none (plain RGB, baseline) 63 / 51.
 - Varied backgrounds in training data: +9 to +21 pts ACT, +10 to +23 SmolVLA; red light +3–6; YOLO overlay +3 (+ category filtering, no grasps of other reflective objects).
 - Cross-environment (Table III; train / novel-similar / novel-extreme white floor): ACT+SEVO 95/85/10; SmolVLA+SEVO 83/75/5; ACT no-SEVO 75/30/0; SmolVLA no-SEVO 70/35/0.
 - Data efficiency: ACT+SEVO ~80% at 40 episodes, 95% at 80; SmolVLA fails below ~80 episodes, 83% at 120.
 - Wrist cam added as 3rd input (Table IV): full SEVO ACT 90→86, SmolVLA 79→12; other rows ACT 20–76, SmolVLA 0–3 (row mapping ambiguous in extraction). Failure: ACT oscillates without converging; SmolVLA does repeated grasp-release in open air.
 - Latency: SmolVLA ~2 min per grasp cycle on Orin NX, ~15 min on RPi5; ACT real-time on Orin.
Ablations: listed above. Adding white-floor data (separate 80 episodes) fixed extreme environment (qualitative, no number).
Failure/limitations: static grasp only (grasping while base moves ≈10%); bounded by training visual diversity (white floor 0–10%); YOLO dependency; single simple task; object position varied only slightly. Critical: wrist-camera conclusion is confounded by a mobile base (visual flow during motion, arm on moving chassis), tiny wrist FOV at close range, and red LED saturation — not directly transferable to a fixed-base arm with a good wrist cam; ACT baseline at 400k steps/bs 24 may overfit; SmolVLA trained with frozen VLM only 20k steps.
Conflicts: Wrist cam hurting contradicts most fixed-base literature (ACT, DP, OFT, "What matters" studies where wrist views help precision and robustness to scene shift). ACT > SmolVLA consistent with ArmnetBench pick-place (ACT 63 vs SmolVLA 23) — both on SO-101. Environment diversity >> other factors agrees with data-scaling-law results (Lin et al.) and GreenAug-style background diversity.
Relevance: Very high — same arm, same community stack, same failure (breaks in new lighting/background), same models (ACT vs SmolVLA). Actionable: (1) collect demos under deliberately varied backgrounds, lighting (on/off/lamp directions) and distractors, keep motion consistent — biggest lever (+9–23 pts; novel env 30→85%); (2) a cheap object-highlight overlay from a detector (for us: pumpkin segmentation) gives appearance-invariant object cue and generalization to other object instances (claims different bottle brands work) — directly addresses "different-looking pumpkin"; (3) ACT with trainable ResNet-18 beats frozen-encoder SmolVLA at 80–120 demos and is real-time; (4) wrist cam: verify by ablation on our fixed-base setup rather than dropping it.
Decision impact:
 - Q13 data diversity: supports varying background/lighting/distractors during collection as #1 factor — confidence H-M (100 trials/condition, 2 robots, SO-101).
 - Q14 robustness: plain ACT/SmolVLA collapse to 30–35% in unseen rooms, 0% extreme; diversified collection + overlay → 85/75% — confidence M.
 - Q12 model size / Q03 encoder: ACT (51.6M, trainable ResNet-18) > SmolVLA (450M, frozen SigLIP) 95 vs 83; SmolVLA needs ≥80–120 demos — confidence M.
 - Q11 cameras: wrist camera added hurt (ACT 90→86, SmolVLA 79→12) on MOBILE base — weakens wrist cam only for mobile/close-range-saturated setups — confidence L for our fixed arm.
 - Q05 augmentation: semantic mask overlay (detector-based object highlighting) as observation augmentation +3 pts and category generalization — confidence L-M.
 - Q10 latency: SmolVLA impractical on edge (2 min/cycle Orin NX); ACT real-time — confidence M.
