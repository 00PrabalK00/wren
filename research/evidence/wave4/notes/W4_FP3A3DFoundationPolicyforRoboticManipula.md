# W4_FP3A3DFoundationPolicyforRoboticManipula — FP3: A 3D Foundation Policy for Robotic Manipulation (2025, arXiv 2503.08950)
Setup: 1.3B diffusion transformer (encoder-decoder, adaLN conditioning, causal action decoder), Uni3D ViT-L (300M, fine-tuned) per camera on colored point clouds (4000 pts FPS, 1 m crop), frozen CLIP text, proprio MLP. Obs history 2, predict 16 / execute 8 at 15 Hz, DDPM train / 16 DDIM steps. Pre-trained on 60k DROID trajectories (3rd-person + wrist cam point clouds; 8xA800, 48 h), LoRA fine-tune 80 demos/task (10 demos x 8 env-object pairs), ~2 h on 1 A800. Real Franka + Robotiq, ZED 2 third-person + ZED mini wrist, VR teleop, absolute Cartesian actions. 4 tasks (Fold Towel, Clean Table, Stand up Cup, Pour Water); eval 4 in-domain envs + 4 unseen envs/objects x 5 trials = 20 trials per cell. Inference on RTX 3090.
Claim: a large pre-trained point-cloud diffusion policy fine-tuned with 80 diverse demos generalizes zero-shot to new scenes/objects at ~80–95%.
Evidence (Table I, in-domain / in-the-wild avg over 4 tasks): FP3 95.0 / 82.5; FP3-Scratch 30.0 / 1.25; DP (2D) 36.25 / 1.25; DP3 22.5 / 2.5; OpenVLA 7.5 / 3.75. Per task FP3 in-domain/wild: Fold Towel 90/85, Clean Table 100/95, Stand up Cup 95/75, Pour Water 95/75. DP best on Clean Table 75 in-domain but 5 wild.
Ablations (Table II, Clean Table, in-domain / wild, 20 trials each):
 - FP3 1.3B 100/95; FP3-Base 365M 95/90 (model size: small effect).
 - FP3-Base-30k pretrain 95/90 (half pretraining data: no change).
 - FP3-Base-Image (DINOv2 on RGB images instead of point cloud, same pretraining) 90/55 -> point cloud mainly buys out-of-domain generalization (+35 wild), not in-domain.
 - FP3-Scratch 35/0 -> pretraining is the dominant factor.
 - Lighting/object color/background/~30 deg camera view/distractor (Clean Table): figure only, qualitative: DP drops especially on color & background and fails completely on 30 deg camera shift; DP3 stable under lighting/color (no color channels) but capped by low in-domain; FP3 high everywhere since calibrated point cloud is in world frame. Distractors: all methods sometimes grasp distractors.
Failure/limitations: authors: base model weak zero-shot; CLIP language too simple; doesn't use 2D foundation encoders. Critical: 20 trials per cell; baselines (DP, DP3) trained from scratch on only 80 demos spread across 8 environments (10 per env) — a hard regime for scratch models, so the gap is mostly pretraining; camera-shift robustness relies on re-calibration (extrinsics known), which is not the same as an uncalibrated bumped camera. DROID-style Franka pretraining doesn't transfer to SO-101 embodiment directly.
Conflicts: DP3 vs DP here: DP3 is not better than DP in-domain (22.5 vs 36.25) — contradicts DP3 paper's large gains, likely because demos are spread over 8 diverse scenes and DP3's colorless points lose object identity. Agrees with "Data scaling laws" (Lin et al.) that environment/object diversity matters. Point cloud vs image gap appears only OOD, consistent with iDP3/GeoVLA-type claims.
Relevance: Directly addresses our failure modes (lighting, new pumpkin, moved camera) and uses our demo count (80) + wrist + scene cam. Takeaways: (1) with a scratch small model and 80 demos, expect ~0 OOD success regardless of 2D/3D — pretraining is decisive; (2) 3D point cloud in world frame, with calibration, is what gives camera-shift robustness; (3) 365M model nearly as good as 1.3B. 1.3B + Uni3D x2 is too heavy for 8 GB training; FP3-Base (365M) might be feasible with LoRA. No SO-101-scale pretrained 3D policy exists; DROID pretraining is embodiment-specific.
Decision impact:
 - Q04 3D/depth: supports colored point cloud (world frame) over RGB for OOD — Base-Image 55 vs Base 90 wild — confidence M (real, 1 task, 20 trials; both pretrained)
 - Q03 vision encoder / pretraining: supports large-scale pretraining over scratch — scratch 30/1.25 vs 95/82.5 — confidence H (4 real tasks)
 - Q12 model size: 365M ≈ 1.3B (95/90 vs 100/95); weak evidence that bigger helps marginally — confidence L
 - Q13 data diversity: 80 demos over 8 env/object pairs gives in-the-wild generalization only with pretraining; scratch policies at 10 demos/env fail OOD — confidence M
 - Q14 robustness: 2D DP fails at ~30 deg camera shift and color/background change; calibrated point-cloud policy robust — confidence M (figure only)
 - Q07 history: uses 2-frame history (no ablation) — confidence L
 - Q06 chunking: predict 16 / execute 8 at 15 Hz with diffusion (no ablation); OpenVLA failures attributed to no chunking — confidence L
