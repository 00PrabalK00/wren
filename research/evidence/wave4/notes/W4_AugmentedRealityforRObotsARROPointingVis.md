# W4_AugmentedRealityforRObotsARROPointingVis — Augmented Reality for RObots (ARRO): Pointing Visuomotor Policies Towards Visual Robustness (2025, arXiv 2505.08627)
Setup: Franka FR3 (custom fingers), third-person side camera (plus wrist cam for pi0 dataset); 4 real tasks (pick-v1 cuboid, push-v1 to cross, doll-v1 plush next to doll, box-v1 cube in box + close lid), 90 teleop demos each; Diffusion Policy trained 1000 epochs, 10 real trials per condition; Octo/OpenVLA/pi0 fine-tuned; sim (MuJoCo replica) real-to-sim 100 eps, cross-embodiment FR3->UR5e 100 eps. Pipeline: GroundingDINO boxes + SAM (unprompted) regions labelled and picked by GPT-4o (gripper fingers) at frame 0, SAM2 tracking afterwards; keep gripper+object pixels, paste onto fixed colored grid background (ARRO) or black (Masked). Same transform at train and test.
Claim: Segmenting only gripper + task objects and compositing onto a fixed virtual background makes policies robust to background/table/robot-appearance/distractor shifts without extra data.
Evidence:
 - Fig. 1 (DP, 10 trials, shifted scene: new background, table texture, robot appearance, distractors): figure-only mapping ambiguous; qualitatively Vanilla collapses (values 0-0.3), Masked partial, ARRO best (0.75-1.0 range).
 - Distractor spatial-reasoning push (Fig. 4): left/right: Vanilla 0.30 vs ARRO 1.00; closer/farther: 0.20 vs 0.90 (my reading of figure labels).
 - Generalist policies under altered scene (Fig. 6): Vanilla Octo/OpenVLA/pi0(cluttered) drop to 0.00; Masked/ARRO recover to 0.30-0.70 (per-bar mapping uncertain). pi0 with same-shape red distractor cube: Vanilla 30%, Masked 70%, ARRO 90%.
 - Table I real-to-sim (fraction of real success retained; DP/Octo/OpenVLA/pi0): Vanilla 0/0/0/79%; Masked 0/3/53/94%; ARRO 0/5/55/77%. Real-to-real baselines 90/50/40/100%.
 - Cross-embodiment FR3->UR5e sim (DP/Octo/OpenVLA): Vanilla 0/71/29%; Masked 80/87/72%; ARRO 99/87/80% (column mapping inferred from text).
Ablations:
 - Background: structured grid (ARRO) vs black (Masked): ARRO better in real DP shifts and pi0 distractor (90 vs 70%); roughly equal in real-to-sim (OpenVLA 55 vs 53; pi0 77 vs 94 — black better).
 - pi0 with side camera only "decreases substantially" (no number) -> they add wrist cam.
Failure/limitations: authors: depends on segmentation/tracking quality, esp. wrist camera where objects enter/leave; cannot compensate lighting changes or reflections (pixels inside mask keep their appearance). Critical read: 10 trials per cell, most numbers only in bar charts; GPT-4o + GroundingDINO + SAM2 at inference is heavy (no latency reported), SAM2 tracking alone is ~tens of ms on big GPU; removing context also removes information (tray/container must be prompted as object); in-domain cost of masking not clearly reported.
Conflicts: Agrees with object-centric / segmentation-mask input lines (e.g. green-screen/background replacement augmentations) that background shift is the main visual failure of image BC. Differs from augmentation-based approaches (generate diverse backgrounds at train time) — here the test input is canonicalized instead. Doesn't address lighting, which is one of our failures; photometric augmentation or depth needed for that.
Relevance: For pumpkin->tray, prompts "pumpkin", "tray", gripper would be easy for GroundingDINO/SAM2. Directly targets our background/distractor shift and partially our "different-looking pumpkin" (shape silhouette retained but texture still visible). Does NOT help lighting (our failure) nor camera shift (mask positions move). On 8 GB laptop, SAM2-tiny tracking + policy is feasible but adds latency; our latency budget is already bad. A cheaper variant: use SAM2 masks offline only to do background-replacement augmentation at train time.
Decision impact:
 - Q14 robustness: supports object-centric masking/canonical background for background & distractor shift; not lighting — confidence L/M (10 trials/cell, figure-only numbers, but consistent across 4 policy families)
 - Q05 augmentation: supports segmentation-based background handling (test-time canonicalization) over raw RGB; structured background > black in real tests — L
 - Q10 latency: weakens (adds foundation-model segmentation/tracking in the loop; no timing given) — L
