# W4_BFAHierarchicalBestFeatureAwareTokenPrun — BFA++: Hierarchical Best-Feature-Aware Token Pruning for Multi-View VLA (2026, arXiv 2602.20566)
Setup: pi0 (full fine-tune 5000 steps, bs 256) and RDT-1B (1200 steps, bs128) on RoboTwin sim (8 tasks, 50-100 demos/task, 100 eval trials/task) + RoboTwin2 OOD (clutter, unseen lighting/background/table height; 50 demos, 4 tasks) + 5 real dual-arm tasks (200 demos each, 20 trials each). 3 cams (head + 2 wrist). Train 8xA100, inference RTX 3090. Prune ratios [α1..3, β]=[0.3,0.2,0.2,0.5]: 768 → 483 → 241 tokens for pi0.
Claim: supervised (Grounding-SAM box masks + phase-based view labels) token pruning of visual tokens before the LLM speeds VLAs 1.5-1.8x AND raises success ~10 points by removing background/distractor tokens.
Evidence:
 - RoboTwin mean (8 tasks): pi0 0.496 @6.5 Hz; +DART 0.456 @9.8; +BFA 0.524 @11.1; +BFA++ 0.583 @10.3. RDT 0.470 @1.0 Hz; +DART 0.403; +BFA 0.489; +BFA++ 0.565 @1.5 Hz.
 - Real (5 tasks, 20 trials each): pi0 0.27 @5.8 Hz; +DART 0.19; +BFA 0.38 @10.1; +BFA++ 0.48 @9.4 Hz. RDT 0.23 @1.2 Hz → +BFA++ 0.46 @1.7 Hz. (e.g. Grasp Banana Hard w/ distractors pi0 0.35 → 0.55; Insert Cable 0.10 → 0.30.)
 - RoboTwin2 OOD (lighting/background/height/clutter): pi0 0.328 → BFA++ 0.405; DART 0.208 (unsupervised pruning hurts OOD).
 - View-occlusion probe (Fig 3, qualitative): occluding wrist cam during approach/post-manip doesn't matter; during manipulation it causes failure. Head view suffices for approach.
Ablations (pi0, 4 sim tasks: Hammer, Bottle Adjust, Shoe Place, Cup Place): BFA++ 0.84/0.58/0.65/0.48; w/o Inter-IP 0.76/0.41/0.56/0.50; w/o Intra-IP 0.74/0.51/0.56/0.35; w/o hierarchical (end-to-end rank drops wrist tokens) 0.73/0.49/0.58/0.46; w/o spatial smoothing 0.84/0.53/0.60/0.46; random drop 0.70/0.51/0.52/0.41; adaptive pooling 0.75/0.48/0.55/0.46; online annotation drop 0.66/0.45/0.50/0.39 (and slow). Prune-ratio sweep (Fig 10, figure only): success rises then falls sharply past a threshold; FPS monotonically up.
Failure/limitations: authors: importance predictors fit to training distribution, may fail with unseen objects/camera configs. Critical: 20 real trials per task, 200 demos/task (2-4x our budget); needs Grounding-SAM annotation pipeline; baselines are big VLAs (pi0 3B) running ~6 Hz on 3090; gains may be largely "less background = less overfitting" which cheaper crops/masks could also give.
Conflicts: Agrees with attention-focusing / object-centric papers (e.g. TinyVLA "where to look", masking/crop results) that suppressing background tokens improves robustness in low data. Contradicts "more pixels/views always better": unsupervised DART pruning hurts, supervised pruning helps → selection quality matters.
Relevance: Medium. For SmolVLA on 8 GB laptop, token count drives latency; dropping ~70% of visual tokens with a learned (box-supervised) mask gave speedup + robustness to clutter/lighting. Wrist cam matters mainly during grasp phase — consistent with keeping wrist cam. Cheap variant for us: object-box-supervised auxiliary saliency / crop around pumpkin+gripper.
Decision impact:
 - Q10 latency: supervised visual-token pruning 768→241 tokens gives 1.5-1.8x speed (pi0 5.8→9.4 Hz real) with no accuracy loss — confidence M.
 - Q14 robustness: removing background/distractor tokens raised OOD (lighting/background/clutter) 0.328→0.405 and real distractor task 0.35→0.55 — confidence M (sim OOD 4 tasks, 20 real trials).
 - Q09 aux objectives: auxiliary BCE on object/gripper masks (λ=0.1) is a useful aux loss — supports object-grounding aux — confidence L-M.
 - Q11 cameras: wrist view critical only during manipulation phase; end-to-end pruning that drops wrist tokens hurts (0.84→0.73 Hammer) — supports wrist cam — confidence M.
