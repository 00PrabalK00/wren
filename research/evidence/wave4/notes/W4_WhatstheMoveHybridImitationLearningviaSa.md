# W4_WhatstheMoveHybridImitationLearningviaSa — What's the Move? Hybrid Imitation Learning via Salient Points (SPHINX) (2024, arXiv 2412.05426)
Setup: Franka Panda, 2 calibrated third-person RGB-D cams (fused colored point cloud, FPS to 1024 pts) + wrist RGB; data at 10 Hz with ≤1 cm max delta. Real tasks: Drawer (20 demos), Cup Stack (30), Train Track (30), Coffee Making (60); sim Robomimic Can (20), Square (50). Waypoint policy = GPT-2-style transformer over point tokens (salient-point classification + per-point offset regression + rotation/gripper/mode tokens); dense policy = Diffusion Policy (ResNet-18, wrist image + proprio only). Custom GUI demos (click salient point + drag waypoint; SpaceMouse for dense). 440 real trials total; OOD tests 10 trials each.
Claim: point-cloud waypoints anchored on predicted task-relevant points for long-range motion + wrist-image dense diffusion for precise phases gives better spatial/visual/viewpoint generalization than image-only DP, HYDRA, fine-tuned OpenVLA.
Evidence: 86.7% average over 4 real + 2 sim tasks, +41.1% over next best baseline (per-task bars figure-only). OOD (Table 1, 10 trials each, DP vs SPHINX): Drawer unseen height 4/10 vs 9/10; Drawer novel viewpoint 1/10 vs 9/10; Cup Stack visual distraction 1/10 vs 8/10; Cup Stack novel viewpoint 0/10 vs 9/10. Speed: 2x-faster waypoint controller → Cup Stack 9.2 s vs DP 15.6 s (10 trials). Fine-tuned OpenVLA (single third-person view, no wrist) was worst — "lacks precision".
Ablations:
 - Waypoint head (Table 2, sim, without/with temporal augmentation): Can — Vanilla regression 68/70, +aux salient 70/78, SPHINX offset-from-salient 77.5/93.5; Square — Vanilla 8/13, +aux salient 68/65.5, SPHINX 75.5/86.5. Salient-point classification as an AUXILIARY loss alone lifts Square 13 → 65.5.
 - Temporal augmentation (train on interpolated steps, α=0.2, ~6x data) helps 5/6 settings, SPHINX +13.5 avg.
 - Point-cloud translation aug ±5 cm used.
 - Appendix: DP3 (scene point cloud only, no wrist) worse than image DP with wrist on Robomimic and complex real tasks; "close-up wrist information is crucial".
Failure/limitations: most failures from dense policy's last-mile imprecision; linear waypoint controller → quasi-static only; needs calibrated multi-camera RGB-D and a special data-collection GUI (salient point annotation), not leader-follower teleop. Viewpoint robustness assumes re-calibrated extrinsics. OOD evaluated with only 10 trials each vs DP only.
Conflicts: agrees with iDP3/DP3 line that 3D input improves viewpoint/spatial generalization, but also shows DP3 without wrist cam underperforms image DP+wrist — consistent with papers stressing wrist cam for precision. Contrasts with OpenVLA fine-tuning claims; consistent with other reports that OpenVLA fine-tunes poorly on single-task Franka.
Relevance: our leader/follower SO-101 data is dense-only; the full hybrid pipeline would need new teleop tooling. Transferable parts: (1) wrist-camera-only dense policy for the grasp phase is immune to scene/camera changes — directly addresses our camera-shift and lighting failures near the object; (2) RealSense depth + calibration gives viewpoint-invariant inputs for the reach phase; (3) an auxiliary "where is the target" (salient point/keypoint) loss is cheap and strongly helps. Speed-up via controller-executed waypoints relevant to latency.
Decision impact:
 - Q04 3D/depth: calibrated point-cloud waypoint policy generalizes to new viewpoints (DP 0–1/10 vs 9/10) — confidence M (real but 10 trials, needs calibration).
 - Q11 cameras: wrist-only dense policy keeps performance under distractors/view change; DP3 without wrist is worse — supports keeping wrist cam central — confidence M.
 - Q09 auxiliary objectives: salient-point classification aux loss: Square 13 → 65.5 — confidence M (sim, 50 demos).
 - Q02 action space: waypoint + dense hybrid, waypoints relative to object points beat absolute waypoint regression (Can 70 → 93.5) — confidence L-M (requires waypoint-annotated data).
 - Q14 robustness: distractors 1/10 → 8/10 with salient attention + wrist-only dense — confidence L-M.
 - Q12 model size: fine-tuned OpenVLA 7B worst on precise tasks vs small DP/SPHINX — confidence L.
