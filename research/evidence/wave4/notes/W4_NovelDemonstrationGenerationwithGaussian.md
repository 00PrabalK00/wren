# W4_NovelDemonstrationGenerationwithGaussian — Novel Demonstration Generation with Gaussian Splatting Enables Robust One-Shot Manipulation (RoboSplat) (2025, arXiv 2504.13175)
Setup: Franka FR3, two fixed eye-on-base RealSense D435i RGB cams (no wrist cam), SpaceMouse teleop at 10 Hz; 5 real tasks (Pick Object, Close Drawer, Pick-Place-Close, Dual Pick-Place, Sweep). ONE real demo + multi-view static scene images → 3DGS reconstruction (COLMAP + Depth Anything prior), URDF alignment via differentiable rendering, Grounded-SAM segmentation; generates demos by equivariant object-pose transforms + motion planning between keyframes, relighting (per-scene color scale U(0.3,1.8), offset U(−0.3,0.3), per-Gaussian noise N(0,0.1²)), COCO/3D-scene background replacement, novel-view rendering (camera target ±10 cm, radius ±0.2 m, angles ±π/6), AI-generated objects (~50 types) + AnyGrasp grasps, embodiment swap. Policy: ResNet-18 + GPT-2-style transformer, 128x128, history 1, chunk 10 with temporal ensembling, L2 BC on relative EE pose actions; RTX 4090, 0.1 s latency. 30 trials per setting.
Claim: 3DGS editing of a single demo yields data that beats hundreds of real demos + 2D augmentation across object pose, lighting, appearance, camera view, object type and embodiment (avg 87.8% vs 57.2%).
Evidence:
 - Object pose (Fig 7, avg of 5 tasks): 200 manual demos 87.3% ≈ 800 generated; 1800 generated 94.7%. Generation 0.64 s/demo vs 19.1 s manual (Table I).
 - Camera view (Table II; Collected 200 real / Pose-only 1800 gen / VISTA 3200 / Ours 3200): Pick Object novel view 6.7/0/33.3/90.0, moving view 0/0/33.3/86.7; Close Drawer novel 16.7/26.7/56.7/100, moving 13.3/30/70/96.7; Pick-Place-Close novel 0/0/33.3/53.3, moving 0/0/16.7/56.7; average 6.1 / 9.5 / 40.6 / 80.6.
 - Lighting (5 conditions incl. flashing red/blue, dark, bright, green, yellow; 6 trials each): Ours >80% avg over 3 tasks, ~+70 pts over un-augmented real data; beats color jitter (per-bar mapping figure-only).
 - Appearance (6 backgrounds): >70 pts over no-appearance-aug; Pick Object 100%; beats 2D inpainting aug.
 - Novel object types (5 unseen real objects): Collected (400 demos, 5 objects) 23.3, ROSIE inpainting 60.0, Ours 76.7 (order as printed; text says >40 pt gain).
 - Embodiment (UR5e from Franka demo): ~100% vs RoVi-Aug lower (figure).
Ablations: Pose-only generated data gives no robustness to camera/lighting (camera avg 9.5 vs 6.1 real) → robustness comes from the matching augmentation axis, not data volume. Scaling generated demos 100→1800 monotonically improves.
Failure/limitations: rigid objects only, no physics (quasi-static pick/place, no contact-rich); needs multi-view capture, URDF, calibration; policy has no wrist cam (wrist views would need robot-attached rendering). Real 200-demo baseline trained without any augmentation in most comparisons (color-jitter baseline only for lighting). Heavily engineered pipeline.
Conflicts: consistent with "What Matters" (camera pose is the critical DV) and with view-augmentation papers (VISTA) but shows 3D-consistent rendering beats 2D novel-view synthesis (40.6 vs 80.6). Contrasts with claims that plain color jitter suffices for lighting — here colour jitter underperforms physically-consistent relighting (magnitude figure-only).
Relevance: directly targets our three failures (lighting, different-looking pumpkin, moved camera). SO-101 has a URDF; RealSense gives depth for reconstruction. Our 50–100 real demos could be multiplied with camera-view and lighting edits of the scene camera stream; wrist-cam frames are harder (not addressed). Also shows a small ResNet-18 transformer with chunk 10 + TE at 10 Hz is sufficient when data is diverse — model size is not the bottleneck.
Decision impact:
 - Q05 augmentation: 3D-consistent generative augmentation (view, lighting, background, object) >> 2D aug; camera-view avg 6.1 → 80.6 — confidence M-H (real, 30 trials, 3 tasks; costly pipeline).
 - Q14 robustness: without targeted augmentation, 200 real demos collapse under novel view (6.1%) and lighting/appearance (~10–17%) — confidence M.
 - Q11 cameras: fixed scene cams + no wrist: policy nearly 0 under camera shift unless view-augmented — confidence M.
 - Q13 data: 800 generated ≈ 200 real; diversity along the shifted axis matters more than count — confidence M.
 - Q12 model size: ResNet-18 + small transformer suffices for all six generalization axes given diverse data — confidence L-M.
