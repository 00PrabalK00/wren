# W4_XSimCrossEmbodimentLearningviaRealtoSimt — X-Sim: Cross-Embodiment Learning via Real-to-Sim-to-Real (CoRL 2025, arXiv 2505.07096)
Setup: Franka 7-DoF, 5 tasks in 2 envs (Mustard Place, Corn in Basket, Shoe on Rack, Letter Arrange, Mug Insert). One RGB-D human video per task (ZED 2) → FoundationPose object tracking + 2DGS scene reconstruction → ManiSkill; PPO state policy with object-centric reward; 500 successful sim rollouts rendered with object pose noise (±2.5 cm, ±π/8), camera ±3 cm, 4 lighting presets; distilled to image-only Diffusion Policy (ResNet-18, 96x96, delta EE actions, To?=single image, action horizon 2 / prediction horizon 8, 10 DDIM-like inference steps). Optional online calibration: 10 real rollouts replayed in sim → InfoNCE real/sim encoder alignment (weight 0.1). 10 real trials per task, metric = average task progress (partial credit).
Claim: object-motion rewards from human video + photorealistic sim + randomized rendering produce image policies that beat hand-retargeting baselines without any teleop data.
Evidence: >30% avg task-progress gain over Hand-Mask and Object-Aware IK baselines (per-task bars figure-only). vs state-based Human2Sim2Robot on Letter Arrange: 43.3% vs 83.3% progress. Calibration +8% avg (+13% Mug Insert). Data efficiency: 1 min human video → 90% vs 10 min teleop BC → 70% on widened Mustard Place.
Ablations: viewpoint (Shoe on Rack, Fig 9; train→test): Side-only: Side 83.3 / Frontal 23.3 / Novel 33.3; Frontal-only: 23.3 / 76.7 / 30.0; Side+Frontal: 96.7 / 80.0 / 53.5. → training on 2 viewpoints improves even the seen views and novel view.
Failure/limitations: 10 trials per task, task-progress metric (partial credit inflates); needs object meshes, rigid objects, env scan, default physics; approximate test camera known; BC baseline (teleop) small.
Conflicts: consistent with "What Matters" and RoboSplat on viewpoint diversity; image > object-pose-state policies for sim-to-real contrasts with 3D/pose-centric methods (because pose tracking noise at test time).
Relevance: we have real teleop so the RL/human-video part is less relevant. Key transferable finding: single-view-trained policy drops from ~80% to ~23–33% under a different/novel camera view; multi-view training data raises novel view to 53.5% — matches our "slightly moved camera" failure. Small 96x96 ResNet-18 DP works.
Decision impact:
 - Q11 cameras / viewpoint: single-view training → 23–33% on other views; 2-view training → 80–97% seen, 53.5% novel — confidence L-M (10 trials, sim-rendered data).
 - Q05 augmentation: rendering with camera/lighting randomization enables zero-teleop transfer; real/sim contrastive encoder alignment +8% — confidence L.
 - Q13 data: human-video-derived sim data (1 min) 90% vs 10 min teleop BC 70% on wide initial-state distribution — confidence L (single task).
