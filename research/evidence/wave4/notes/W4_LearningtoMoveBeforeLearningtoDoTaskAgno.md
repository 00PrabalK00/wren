# W4_LearningtoMoveBeforeLearningtoDoTaskAgno — Learning to Move Before Learning to Do: Task-Agnostic Pretraining for VLAs (2026, arXiv id not in text)
Setup: Qwen2.5-VL-3B + SigLIP-400M (224px), 2-layer MLP head, 7-D delta-EE actions; Stage 1 inverse-dynamics (o_t, o_t+1 → a_t, data downsampled to 5 Hz) on task-agnostic data (off-task Bridge trajectories in sim; 30 h autonomous scripted random play in real, resets every 30 min); Stage 2 BC fine-tune on expert demos. SIMPLER WidowX (4 tasks, 50 episodes/ckpt, 5k expert trajs); real WidowX 250 (third-person cam), 2 tasks (carrot→plate, push pumpkin left), 200 expert demos, 20 trials per scenario × 5 scenarios. 8×H100.
Claim: Inverse-dynamics pretraining on cheap unlabeled play data teaches "how to move", raising BC success and robustness (esp. background/viewpoint shift).
Evidence: SIMPLER Avg-All: TAP-20k 33.32 vs Standard BC (same arch/Stage-2 data) 23.15, OpenVLA 7.75, RT-1-X 3.03; Avg-Partial (grasp) TAP 45.82 vs Octo 42.30, pi0 53.10. Scaling Stage-1 episodes 8k/14k/20k → 24.47/30.2/33.32. Real (Table 3; scratch / TAP / NORA): Carrot — standard 20/40/65, init-state pert 20/30/65, distractors 5/30/40, background texture 0/25/10, viewpoint 0/15/0, avg 9/28/36. Push pumpkin — standard 55/75/85, init 45/75/80, distractors 5/65/60, background 0/65/55, viewpoint 0/25/0, avg 21/61/56.
Ablations: Stage-1 × Stage-2 step sweep (Table 5): with 20k Stage-1 steps Avg-All stalls ~18–24% regardless of Stage-2 length; 100k Stage-1 reaches 33.3%. Fig 3: BC from scratch plateaus ~23% while pretrained keeps climbing (raises ceiling, not speed). Grad-CAM: ID pretraining concentrates attention on gripper + objects, suppresses background (qualitative).
Failure/limitations: failures ~75% semantic (grasping distractor, freezing/looping), ~25% execution (slip, mm misalignment, depth ambiguity from single view). My read: big 3B model; real baseline "from scratch" trains a 3B VLM on 200 demos (weak baseline); 20 trials per cell; SIMPLER absolute numbers low. Note camera shift ("minor extrinsic shifts") → 0% for both scratch and NORA (1M-traj pretrained) — viewpoint robustness very hard even for large pretrained VLAs.
Conflicts: Agrees with inverse-dynamics/latent-action pretraining literature (e.g., LAPA, Seer) and with AR-WAM/FOCA that dynamics-oriented objectives help. Contradicts the common belief that large robot-data pretraining (NORA) gives viewpoint robustness: NORA 0% under small camera shift on both tasks.
Relevance: High on robustness diagnosis: small camera extrinsic shifts collapsed even a 1M-trajectory VLA to 0% — matches our SmolVLA camera-move failure; cheap self-play + inverse-dynamics objective on our own SO-101 (scripted random motions around the table with objects) is a feasible data source and gave the only non-zero viewpoint numbers (15–25%). Background texture shift: ID pretraining 25–65% vs scratch 0%. Also the "downsample to 5 Hz for ID" detail matters for our 30 fps recording.
Decision impact:
 - Q09 aux objectives: supports inverse-dynamics pretraining/aux on unlabeled play data (+10 abs SIMPLER; real avg 9→28, 21→61) — confidence M (real, 20 trials/cell)
 - Q14 robustness: viewpoint shift → 0% for scratch and NORA, 15–25% with ID pretraining; background shift 0→25/65 — confidence M
 - Q13 data: 30 h autonomous random play is a useful cheap supplement to 200 demos — confidence M
 - Q11 cameras: single third-person view gives depth ambiguity failures; camera extrinsics shift is catastrophic — confidence L-M
 - Q02 action space: delta EE chosen for transfer of play priors (no ablation) — confidence L
