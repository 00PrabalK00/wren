# W4_GeneralizableHierarchicalSkillLearningvi — Generalizable Hierarchical Skill Learning via Object-Centric Representation (GSL) (2025, arXiv 2510.21121)
Setup: Sim GemBench (CoppeliaSim, Franka, 2 RGB-D cams): 10 training tasks, 22 zero-shot test tasks, 20 rollouts each; GSL uses 3 demos/task vs baselines 100. Real: UR5 + 3 RealSense D455 RGB-D (calibrated), 3 source tasks, 6 test tasks, 10 trials; GSL 3 demos vs 3D-LOTUS++ 30. Pipeline: VLM segments demo into (skill label, object) segments → DINOv2+SAM2 masks → lift to object point cloud via depth + extrinsics → re-center point cloud and EE keypose trajectory at a random on-object anchor ("mask-as-frame") → EquiDiff (equivariant diffusion) low-level policy on (canonical point cloud, skill-label embedding). Only close-range interaction segment is learned; free-space approach done by motion planner.
Claim: object-frame canonicalized skills + hierarchical VLM interface give huge sample efficiency and compositional/appearance generalization.
Evidence:
 - GemBench training tasks mean: GSL (3 demos) 98.0 vs BridgeVLA 96.8, RVT-2 97.6, 3D-LOTUS++ 88.3 (100 demos).
 - GemBench zero-shot test tasks mean: GSL 82.3 vs 3D-LOTUS++ 66.8, BridgeVLA 58.9, RVT-2 46.5, 3D Diffuser Actor 32.4.
 - Real (10 trials): Toy in cup 9 vs 7; Toy in box 9 vs 4; Hammer from drawer 6 vs 5; Teabag in cup (novel obj) 9 vs 6; Teabag + distractors 9 vs 4; Prepare Tea (long composition) 6 vs 3 (/10, GSL vs 3D-LOTUS++).
Ablations (Table IV, mean SR over 5 tasks, 20 trials): complete 0.83; include approach phase + full robot state ("regular skill") 0.37; no canonicalization 0.12; keypose action as interface 0.10; heatmap as interface 0.17. Composition task Push Btns4+3: 0.90 vs 0.10–0.25 for ablations (skill boundary gives +65 pts).
Failure/limitations: SAM2 part-level segmentation failures (drawer handle); GT masks raise Prepare Tea +20% and Hammer +30%. Single-object skills only (no multi-object relations like "place into tray" relative to a second object — although place skills exist with target-object masks). Requires calibrated RGB-D, keyframe-based open-loop trajectories + motion planner, not closed-loop reactive control. Real trials 10 each.
Conflicts: supports object-centric/canonicalization papers (e.g., ControlVLA, language-guided object-centric DP) — relative/object-frame action representation beats world-frame. Very different paradigm from end-to-end closed-loop BC; low demo numbers (3) are only possible because free-space motion is delegated to a planner.
Relevance: for pumpkin→tray, the core idea usable for us is "express actions relative to the segmented object's 3D centroid" (from RealSense depth + SAM2 mask) to get position invariance and appearance invariance; full hierarchical VLM stack is overkill and the keypose/planner paradigm conflicts with our joint-space teleop BC. Moderate relevance.
Decision impact:
 - Q04 3D/depth: supports lifting segmented object to point cloud for spatial/appearance generalization (zero-shot mean 82.3 vs 46.5 RVT-2 w/ 3 vs 100 demos) — confidence M (sim-heavy, 10-trial real).
 - Q02 action space: object-frame (canonicalized) actions >> world-frame (0.83 vs 0.12 ablation) — M.
 - Q14 robustness: object-centric point cloud input robust to novel instance/color and distractors (real 9/10 vs 4/10) — L/M.
 - Q13 data: 3 demos/task suffice when skills are object-canonicalized and approach is planned — L (not transferable to end-to-end BC).
