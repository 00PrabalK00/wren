# W4_SensingWhichModalityMattersEvidenceGated — Sensing Which Modality Matters: Evidence-Gated Regularization for Robust VLA Policies (2026, arXiv id not in text)
Setup: pi0.5 (PaliGemma-2B LoRA + 300M action expert LoRA, horizon 32) fine-tuned with EGR: per-frame, per-camera evidence = visible area of focal/interaction objects (sim: GT segmentation; real: Grounding DINO + SAM2 offline, training only); loss adds (a) invariance of flow velocity when low-evidence camera is randomly erased, (b) sufficiency (same output when only high-evidence camera kept). Zero inference overhead. Sim: BEHAVIOR-1K 47 skill segments (11 nav, 36 manipulation), 20 rollouts each. Real: bimanual Kinova with 3 RGB cams (head + 2 wrist), 4 tasks × 10 trials per condition; single-arm MELFA + 1 RGB + 2 GelSight, 2 tasks × 10 trials. Baselines: vanilla pi0.5, ModDrop (random modality dropout).
Claim: Multi-camera VLAs learn spurious dependence on uninformative cameras ("modality entanglement"); gating consistency regularizers by object-visibility evidence fixes distractor/occlusion brittleness.
Evidence: Real bimanual pooled (baseline → EGR, N=40): Clean 28/40 → 32/40 (n.s.); NoUseless (corrupt irrelevant cam) 21/40 → 36/40; UsefulOnly 10/40 → 29/40; physical RealDistractor 12/40 → 34/40 (p<0.001). Tactile: Clean 18/20 → 17/20; SingleUseful 8/20 → 18/20; RealDistractor 11/20 → 14/20 (n.s.). Sim MAN SR: Full 12.5 (vanilla) / 7.5 (ModDrop) / 16.4 (EGR); NoUseless 9.4 / 6.8 / 16.5; UsefulOnly 2.8 / 2.8 / 6.1. Action-MSE increase under UsefulOnly: +158.5% vanilla, +137.7% ModDrop, +121.8% EGR.
Ablations: ModDrop (uniform random camera dropout) does NOT help and hurts Full SR in sim (12.5 → 7.5); evidence gating is what matters. No threshold sweeps reported in main text.
Failure/limitations: My read: needs object masks for training data (GDINO+SAM2 pipeline); sim absolute SR tiny (≤16%); real trials 10 per task-condition; real distractor placement protocol unspecified. Evidence heuristic (visible pixel area) assumes the task object is known.
Conflicts: Contradicts the common practice of plain random camera/modality dropout as a robustness recipe (ModDrop ≈ or < vanilla here). Consistent with "wrist cam can cause spurious correlations" findings, and with AR-WAM's observation of shortcut features.
Relevance: Directly relevant: our setup has scene + wrist cams and fails with new-looking pumpkin/distractors. EGR-style training (random-erase the camera that does not see the pumpkin/tray; enforce action invariance; enforce sufficiency of the camera that does) needs only offline masks (GDINO+SAM2 on our 50–100 demos — feasible) and adds no inference cost. Demonstrated on pi0.5, but the loss is architecture-agnostic.
Decision impact:
 - Q14 robustness: supports evidence-gated camera invariance/sufficiency regularization: real distractors 30% → 85% — confidence M (real, Fisher p<0.001, N=40)
 - Q05 augmentation: weakens naive uniform modality/camera dropout (sim 12.5 → 7.5 Full) — confidence M
 - Q11 cameras: multi-camera policies over-rely on uninformative views; single-view fallback 25% → 73% with EGR — confidence M
 - Q09 aux objectives: consistency-style auxiliary losses (velocity invariance under erasure) effective, zero inference cost — confidence M
