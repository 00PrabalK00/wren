# W4_RoBridgeAHierarchicalArchitectureBridgin — RoBridge: A Hierarchical Architecture Bridging Cognition and Execution for General Robotic Manipulation (2025, arXiv n/a in text)
Setup: GPT-4o planner decomposes into primitives (reach/grasp/place/press/...); GroundingDINO+SAM+Track-Anything produce an "invariant operable representation" (IOR) = 3rd-view object/gripper MASKS + 1st-view (wrist) MASKED DEPTH + action one-hot + direction constraint; low-level "guided embodied agent" (DrQ-v2-sized CNN, 168x168) trained by imitation from per-task RL experts in sim (MetaWorld MT50, Robosuite) with heavy DR (arm pose, object shape, camera offset, depth warping/blur/holes, mask shift/crop/add/delete) + adaptive offline DAgger. Real: Kinova Gen3, wrist + third-person RealSense D435i; sim-trained agent fine-tuned with 5 real demos/task (2k steps); 6 GB GPU inference excluding GPT-4o; per-frame GEA + tracking stated "6080ms" (likely 60–80 ms, extraction garbled); VLM 0.3 s + detection 1 s per primitive.
Claim: an object-mask + masked-depth intermediate representation makes a small low-level policy invariant to background/light/color/camera changes.
Evidence: MetaWorld (MT50 / unseen background / light / color / camera pose / mean): RoBridge 85.4/84.6/82.6/83.2/74.8/82.1; ManipGen 73.2/75.4/70.4/72.4/62.8/70.8; DrQ-v2 (RGB) 24.0/24.6/17.4/22.2/19.8. Real (Table 2, success %): RoBridge Pick seen/unseen 85/85, Sweep 70/65, Press 95, Drawer 100, mean 83.3; π0 (5-demo fine-tune) mean 18.3; RDT 7.5; ReKep 49.2. Long-horizon avg len 3.0 vs ReKep 1.7, π0 0.5. Zero-shot unseen sim tasks 75% mean.
Ablations (Table 5, MetaWorld; MT50 / camera pose / mean): full 85.4/74.8/82.1; raw depth instead of masked depth 87.2/60.8/79.7 (better ID, worse camera shift); DINOv2 features instead of masks 62.2/30.6/50.4; keypoints 80.2/43.2/59.9; language-only 84.8/20.2/39.3 (unseen bg 34.4); smaller VLM 65.4/64.4/63.8; w/o DAgger 68.4/50.2/65.1; w/o online learning 75.4/66.6/73.7; w/o domain randomization 62.8/30.2/56.1.
Failure/limitations: mask loss under occlusion/overlap, position deviation; hierarchical → each module can fail; depends on GPT-4o and several detectors (latency ~1.3 s at each primitive start); simple shapes only; real baselines (π0/RDT) given only 5 demos — unfair to end-to-end methods; real trial counts likely 20/task (not stated).
Conflicts: DINOv2 features doing poorly (attention ignores gripper/small objects) contradicts works recommending DINOv2 as frozen encoder — but here it's frozen features into an RL-distilled small policy under DR, not fine-tuned. Masked-object representations robust to background/light matches object-centric papers (SPHINX salient points).
Relevance: object masks (e.g., SAM2/tracked pumpkin + tray) plus wrist masked depth as policy inputs are a concrete recipe for lighting/background robustness on 8 GB (GEA itself small). Tracking adds per-frame cost; mask quality becomes the failure point. Raw depth improves ID but hurts camera shift — for us, depth should be masked/object-centric or canonicalized.
Decision impact:
 - Q14 robustness: object-mask + masked-depth inputs keep 83–85% under unseen bg/light/color vs RGB RL ~20% — confidence M (sim 50 tasks; real small).
 - Q03 vision encoder: frozen DINOv2 features 50.4 vs masks 82.1 mean; camera shift 30.6 — weakens frozen DINOv2 for small policies — confidence L-M.
 - Q04 depth: masked wrist depth > raw depth under camera shift (74.8 vs 60.8) — confidence L-M.
 - Q05 augmentation: w/o DR mean 82.1 → 56.1, camera 74.8 → 30.2 — confidence M (sim).
 - Q13 data quality: w/o DAgger 82.1 → 65.1 — on-policy correction data matters — confidence M (sim).
 - Q12 model size: π0 fine-tuned with 5 demos only 18.3% real — confidence L (unfair demo budget).
