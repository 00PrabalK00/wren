# W4_0ResourceAwareRobustManipulationviaTamin — χ0: Resource-Aware Robust Manipulation via Taming Distributional Inconsistencies (2026, arXiv 2602.09021)
Setup: two dual-arm systems (Agilex Piper, ARX X5; 2×6DoF+gripper), 3 RealSense D435i (1 fixed head, 2 wrist) 640×480 @30 Hz, joint controllers 100–200 Hz; 3 long-horizon garment tasks (flatten+fold; conditional retrieve/sort; hang shirt); ~20 h demos/task (2668 / 3519 / 2988 episodes incl. DAgger); base π0.5 (full fine-tune, flow matching), chunk K=50, 80k steps, bs 128, lr 2.5e-5, 8×A100 train, RTX 4090 inference; eval 10 trials × 3 garments per setting.
Claim: robustness bottleneck is train/model/deploy distribution mismatch, not scale; weight-merging subset policies (Model Arithmetic), stage-aware advantage-weighted BC, and train-deploy alignment (heuristic DAgger, spatio-temporal aug, temporal chunk-wise smoothing) give ~250% relative SR over π0.5.
Evidence: nearly ALL quantitative results are in bar figures (Fig. 7–19) — figure only, qualitative:
 - System breakdown (Task A): performance monotonically increases as MA, SA, TDA are added; SA dominates throughput, TDA dominates success rate but raises retry cost.
 - MA (Tasks A/B/C): every merging variant (avg, inverse-loss, gradient descent, greedy) beats both the single-best subset model AND the model trained on the full union of data; OOD (DAgger) validation set for choosing merge weights gives lower variance than in-domain; greedy best. Correlation weaker on A/B (OOD data quality).
 - Heuristic DAgger (start from hand-set failure states, record recovery) ≈ standard DAgger in recovery quality, both clearly > base; retry cost increases (base "low retry" = never recovers).
 - Control (Fig. 12, 17–19): temporal chunk-wise smoothing (drop stale actions by latency, linear cross-fade old buffer→new chunk over overlap) > sync, naive async, temporal ensembling, RTC "in most cases"; smoothing+RTC best. Spatio-temporal aug (flip+arm swap, frame skipping) gave no significant gain on Task A; helps only when paired with control optimization.
 - Absolute vs delta joint (appendix Figs 17–19): delta joint UNDERPERFORMS absolute joint on Task B; Task C (precise hanger insertion) most sensitive to parameterization; smoothing helps under both.
 - Baseline screen: XVLA, GO-1, UniVLA, OpenVLA, DexVLA → negligible SR after full post-training on 20 h (30 trials); only π0/π0.5 viable.
 - Data quality statement: "success rate fluctuates 20%–60% under identical settings" depending on data quality; algorithmic gains vanish on high-quality data ("masking effect"). Proposed test: replay-ability (open-loop replay of demo from similar initial state succeeds).
Ablations: listed above; no numbers in text.
Failure/limitations: authors: no check of pretrained-prior retention; garment-only; data valuation is costly. Critical: 10 trials/garment, all numbers figure-only, massive data (20 h/task, ~3k episodes) and 8×A100 — far from our regime; MA result (merging beats joint training) is for a 3B VLA and may not transfer to small from-scratch policies.
Conflicts: Chunk-wise linear cross-fade beating temporal ensembling and RTC is consistent with SmolVLA-async jerkiness complaints; explicitly says SmolVLA's naive chunk switching causes "severe prediction-execution misalignment and control instability" — matches our observed failure. Absolute > delta joint agrees with ACT's un-quantified claim; disagrees with action-space studies favoring delta.
Relevance: Very relevant for Q10: our jerky chunk boundaries with async SmolVLA — Algorithm 1 is a cheap drop-in (drop d=min(k,dmax) stale steps, linearly blend overlap). Same camera layout (fixed RealSense + wrist, 640×480, 30 Hz). Heuristic DAgger (set up failure states — pumpkin half-grasped, knocked over — and record recoveries) is cheap for 50–100 demo regime. Replay-ability check is a cheap data QA on SO-101 (backlash will show up). Record at 30 Hz like them.
Decision impact:
 - Q10 latency/smoothness: supports async + stale-action drop + linear cross-fade at chunk boundaries over naive switching/TE/RTC-alone — confidence M (real robot, multiple tasks, figure-only, big VLA).
 - Q02 action space: supports absolute joint over delta joint — confidence M (real, 3 tasks, figure only).
 - Q13 data: supports recovery demos from staged failure states (heuristic DAgger) and data-quality filtering by replay — confidence M.
 - Q05 augmentation: flip/frame-skip spatio-temporal aug no significant gain alone — confidence L.
 - Q06 chunking: chunk 50 @30 Hz (~1.7 s) worked with smoothing — confidence L (not ablated).
 - Q12 model size: many VLAs (OpenVLA, GO-1, XVLA, UniVLA) failed to fine-tune on hard tasks; only π0/π0.5 viable — confidence L (different regime).
