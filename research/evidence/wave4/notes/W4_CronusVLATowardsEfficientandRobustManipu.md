# W4_CronusVLATowardsEfficientandRobustManipu — CronusVLA: Towards Efficient and Robust Manipulation via Multi-Frame Vision-Language-Action Modeling (2025, arXiv id not in text)
Setup: VLA (7B OpenVLA-based, and 0.5B) pretrained single-frame on OXE (discrete tokens), post-trained multi-frame on Fractal+Bridge; per-frame VLM "learnable feature" cached in a FIFO queue, DiT cross-frame decoder with diffusion loss predicting action chunk. Sim: SimplerEnv (Google/WidowX), LIBERO (third-person + wrist), new SimplerEnv-OR (24 disturbance types, 200–400 trials/setting). Real: Franka Research 3, single third-person RealSense D435i, 50 demos/task, 25 trials/task, baselines DP3 and OpenVLA fine-tuned.
Claim: Cached per-frame VLM features + cross-frame decoder give cheap multi-frame history, improving success and robustness to transient occlusion/noise.
Evidence: SimplerEnv avg 70.9 (7B) vs +M.F.-naive 32.4; LIBERO avg 97.0 (wrist view, no state) vs OpenVLA-OFT 97.1, pi0 94.2. SimplerEnv-OR (Table 11, 7B): total avg SR 52.4 / R-Score 86.9 vs CogACT 39.8/72.1, lerobot-pi0 24.7/49.4, RoboVLMs 28.8/67.4. Full occlusion Cyclic 1:1: Cronus avg 35.4 vs CogACT 4.2, SpatialVLA/RoboVLMs 0. Real: "72.6% under interference/occlusion" — per-task real numbers figure only, qualitative: Cronus > OpenVLA and DP3 on all tasks; DP3 sensitive to distractors/human interference; OpenVLA re-presses same button (state aliasing).
Ablations (Table 4, SimplerEnv avg / Hz, 7 frames):
 - Single-frame baseline 31.0 @5.18 Hz → naive multi-frame images into VLM 32.4 @3.09 Hz (+1.4 abs, −40% speed).
 - + continuous decoder on cached features (decoder only trained) 48.2 @8.73 Hz; + train VL backbone 67.2; + multi-frame regularization 70.9.
 - Decoder (Table 5): no cross-attn 68.3; no modulator (current and past frames treated equally) 63.5; MLP decoder 51.8; SiT flow-matching decoder 67.6 vs DiT diffusion 70.9.
 - Frame count (Fig 5, figure only, qualitative): inverted-U; 7B best at 7 frames, 0.5B best at 4; too many frames degrade.
 - From-scratch multi-frame VLM (no single-frame pretraining) at 50k steps: 10.4 / 18.1 vs 70.9.
Failure/limitations: My read: all ablations in SimplerEnv/Bridge-scale data, large 7B models; real-world results figure-only, 25 trials, single third-person camera; the "robustness" is to transient corruptions (occlusion frames, noise), not to persistent lighting/object/camera shift. Modulator result shows past frames can dominate current cues (copycat-type risk) unless down-weighted.
Conflicts: Agrees with "history helps only if current frame is privileged" views; contrasts with Diffusion Policy/ACT findings that history often hurts in low-data BC — here history helps because features are pretrained and history is compressed to one token per frame with explicit current-frame emphasis. Naive image stacking barely helps (+1.4) — consistent with common finding.
Relevance: For our setup, a 7B VLA is out of budget. Transferable idea: cache per-frame encoder features and feed a small history (≈2–4 frames) to the action head with current-frame emphasis; cheap at inference. Useful mainly for occlusion (wrist cam occluded during grasp) and phase ambiguity (e.g., has pumpkin already been placed). No evidence for lighting/camera shift.
Decision impact:
 - Q07 history: supports short compressed feature history (cached per-frame tokens, current frame weighted) over naive frame stacking — confidence M (large sim ablation, real qualitative)
 - Q14 robustness: history improves robustness to transient occlusion/noise (R-Score 86.9 vs 72.1 CogACT) — confidence M (sim disturbance benchmark)
 - Q01 action head: DiT diffusion decoder 70.9 > SiT flow 67.6 > MLP 51.8 at large data — confidence L (large-data regime)
 - Q10 latency: caching past-frame features keeps 8.7 Hz vs 3.1 Hz naive multi-frame — confidence M
