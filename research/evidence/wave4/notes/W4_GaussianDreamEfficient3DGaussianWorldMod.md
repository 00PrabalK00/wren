# W4_GaussianDreamEfficient3DGaussianWorldMod — GaussianDream++: Efficient 3D Gaussian World Modeling for VLA (2026, arXiv ~2608.xxxxx, Aug 2026)
Setup: π0.5 (PaliGemma + flow-matching action expert) + 16 World State tokens + 4 World Prediction tokens in the prefix. Training-only head decodes tokens into current 3D Gaussians (metric depth, unprojected with calibrated cameras, rendered RGB/depth/alpha losses) and future Gaussians (residual center motion, 3D flow, static-consistency). Head removed at inference. LIBERO (4 suites) + LIBERO-Plus zero-shot shifts (Camera, Robot, Language, Light, Background, Noise, Layout). Real: dual-arm robot, global camera + wrist cam, 2 tasks × 3 conditions (Standard / Layout / Camera relocated) × 20 trials. Latency per chunk 286 ms (π0.5) → 330 ms (hardware not stated).
Claim: training-time 3D Gaussian current/future supervision on a few extra tokens improves geometric robustness (camera/layout) at ~15% latency cost.
Evidence (matched protocol, π0.5 reproduced → GaussianDream++): LIBERO avg 96.9 → 98.6. LIBERO-Plus Overall 85.5 → 87.8; Camera 73.2 → 80.1; Layout 87.7 → 90.0; Noise 89.9 → 94.2; Light 96.3 → 99.1; Background 95.5 → 98.8; Robot 77.9 → 73.0 (worse). Real (Table 2, 20 trials each): pooled 35/120 (29.2%) → 63/120 (52.5%); Standard 40.0 → 62.5; Layout 25.0 → 50.0; Camera relocation 22.5 → 42.5. Bowl-Proximity 25.0 → 46.7; Eggplant 33.3 → 58.3.
Ablations (LIBERO-Plus Overall): extra tokens without Gaussian supervision 86.3 (+0.8, capacity alone); + current-world recon 86.9 (Camera 77.0); + future prediction (uncoupled) 87.2; coupled 87.5; + static consistency 87.8. Remove metric-depth loss: 87.8 → 86.9, Camera 80.1 → 76.5, Layout 90.0 → 87.7. Remove 3D flow: 87.2.
Failure/limitations: gains in LIBERO-Plus are a few points; Robot-shift gets worse; real baseline π0.5 surprisingly weak (29%); needs calibrated cameras + depth targets during training; base model ~3B, 330 ms/chunk on unspecified GPU — too big for 8 GB laptop.
Conflicts: consistent with DIAL/FLARE family that future-prediction auxiliaries help, and with 3D-aware papers that depth supervision specifically helps camera shift. Gains are much smaller than data/augmentation approaches (RoboSplat camera 6→81%) — auxiliary geometry supervision helps but doesn't solve camera shift alone (real camera shift still only 42.5%).
Relevance: we have RealSense depth → an auxiliary metric-depth prediction loss from the scene camera features during training (dropped at inference) is a cheap way to add geometric grounding without latency cost. Real camera relocation still halves success even with this → combine with view augmentation/multi-view data.
Decision impact:
 - Q04 depth: depth used as TRAINING-ONLY supervision (not input) improves camera-shift robustness (LIBERO-Plus Camera 76.5 → 80.1 from depth loss; real camera shift 22.5 → 42.5) — confidence M (20 real trials/condition).
 - Q09 auxiliary objectives: current-3D reconstruction + future prediction aux: +2.3 Overall; tokens alone +0.8 — confidence M.
 - Q14 robustness: real camera relocation drops success ~40% relative even for best model — confidence M.
 - Q10 latency: aux-only heads removed at deploy keep overhead small (286 → 330 ms) — confidence L.
