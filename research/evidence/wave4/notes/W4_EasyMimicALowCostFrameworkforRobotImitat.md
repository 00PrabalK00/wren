# W4_EasyMimicALowCostFrameworkforRobotImitat — EasyMimic: A Low-Cost Framework for Robot Imitation Learning from Human Videos (2026, arXiv 2602.11464)
Setup: SO100-Plus (SO-100 with added joint, 6-DoF) + Joy-Con teleop; 2 RGB cams (fixed top-down + wrist); 4 tasks (Pick duck→bowl, drawer pull/push, 2-level stack, language-conditioned pick-place with 4 object/goal combos); 100 human hand videos (HaMeR 3D hand → EE pose via retargeting) + 20 robot teleop demos per task; GR00T N1.5-3B fine-tuned (frozen VL encoder, shared DiT flow head, per-embodiment state/action enc/dec), absolute EE pose + gripper actions; 5k steps, RTX 4090, bs 32; 10 trials/task, staged scoring (0/0.5/1).
Claim: Co-training a pretrained VLA on retargeted, hand-color-randomized human videos + ~10–20 robot demos greatly beats robot-only fine-tuning on a low-cost arm.
Evidence (Table II avg score): Robot-only 10 traj 0.26; Robot-only 20 traj 0.51; Pretrain(human)→finetune 0.75; EasyMimic co-train 0.88. LC task: 20 robot traj 0.40 → 0.90. Unseen objects (trained on pink duck): green duck 0.50→0.80, pink cube 0.20→0.50 (robot-only→EasyMimic).
Ablations (3 tasks avg): no action alignment 0.60 vs 0.87; no visual alignment (hand recolor) 0.40; partial hand masking 0.27; shared action head 0.47 vs separate 0.87. Init (Table VI): no pretrain robot-only 0.15, no pretrain + human 0.53, pretrain robot-only 0.25, pretrain + human 0.87. Scaling (Fig 4, figure only, qualitative): human data gains saturate beyond ~50 videos; robot data gains saturate after ~10 traj (with 50 human videos).
Failure/limitations: failures = premature release, imprecise handle grasp, knocking objects when stacking, unstable edge placement. My read: 10 trials/task with 0.5-point partial credit → each cell ±0.15 noise; robot-only baseline trained only 5k steps with 10–20 demos, so "robot-only" is weak and far below our 50–100 demo regime; 3B model does not fit our inference budget easily; no lighting/camera shift test.
Conflicts: Agrees with human-video co-training literature (e.g., EgoMimic, Phantom) that appearance gap must be handled (hand recolor, full mask essential). The weak 20-demo robot-only (0.51) vs our expectation from ACT at 50 demos — different regime (few demos, big VLA, short training).
Relevance: Moderate. Same arm family and camera layout (top + wrist). If demo collection is the bottleneck, cheap human videos (8–15 min per 100) with HaMeR retargeting can substitute for many robot demos; requires EE-space actions and hand-eye calibration. Wrist cam must be zero-padded for human data. Novel-object gain (different pumpkin) is relevant to Q14.
Decision impact:
 - Q13 data: supports augmenting 10–20 robot demos with ~50–100 human videos (0.51→0.88) — confidence M (real, same arm family, but 10 trials)
 - Q05 augmentation: supports full-hand visual randomization for human data; partial masking fails (0.27) — confidence L
 - Q14 robustness: human-video co-training improves unseen object color/shape (0.35→0.65 avg) — confidence L (10 trials)
 - Q02 action space: absolute EE pose used; separate per-embodiment action heads needed (0.87 vs 0.47) — confidence L
 - Q08 language: LC task with 4 combos reached 0.90 with co-training vs 0.40 robot-only — confidence L
