# W4_FOCAFutureOrientedConditioningforDataEff — FOCA: Future-Oriented Conditioning for Data-Efficient Vision-Language-Action Adaptation (2026, ICML; arXiv id not in text)
Setup: Aux objectives added to VLA fine-tuning (mainly pi0, also GR00T-N1.5): explicit prediction of future object-centric latent interaction embeddings (via a few "representative tokens") + implicit contrastive alignment of current context token with a future (t+10) goal/interaction embedding; optional action-free pretraining on DreamGen synthetic videos. Sim: LIBERO at 10/40/100% data (~5/20/43 demos per task), RoboCasa 30/100 demos. Real: bimanual ALOHA (Open Bag, Set Table, Tie Shoelaces; pi0 backbone) + "simulated humanoid" Place Parts (GR00T-N1.5, 50 trials). Large VLAs (3B-class), multi-GPU training (~20 h).
Claim: Future-oriented latent aux losses raise the per-demo learning signal and close much of the few-shot VLA gap.
Evidence (LIBERO avg, Table 1, identical splits/budgets): 100%: pi0 94.6, GR00T-N1.5 94.6, EO-1 94.1, SmolVLA 92.5, FOCA 96.6. 40%: pi0 89.9, GR00T 91.4, EO-1 91.0, SmolVLA 90.3, FOCA 94.0. 10%: pi0 77.6, GR00T 78.2, EO-1 82.2, SmolVLA 77.3, FOCA 85.3. LIBERO-10 (long) at 10%: pi0 59.0, SmolVLA 51.3, FOCA 69.4. PEFT at 10%: ControlVLA 78.4, LoRA 78.2, DoRA 78.6. Future baselines (40%): DreamVLA-style 92.1, FLARE 91.0, FOCA 94.0; RoboCasa GR00T: FLARE 28.4 vs FOCA 34.4. RoboCasa gains +4.4–12% (pi0), +7.2–10.4% (GR00T). Real ALOHA Set Table (Table 8, 40%=120 / 100%=300 demos; layout ambiguous in extraction): pi0 ≈10%/10%, FOCA 20%/45%, FOCA+DreamGen 55%/60%. Open Bag 20→40% (limited), 45% full; Tie Shoelaces FOCA 95%; humanoid Place Parts 58→84% (sim). Other real per-task numbers figure only.
Ablations: explicit only (40%) 93.3; explicit on full-image pixels 90.9; explicit object-centric pixels 91.4 → latent object-centric best; token count moderate (8) best vs 2 or 18 (figure only). Implicit: single future frame t+10 ≈ multi-frame; full-image embedding and small-model contrastive (R3M/LIV-style) objectives worse (figure only). Pseudo-action (IDM on synthetic video) gives no consistent gain, degrades at low data; implicit FOCA on synthetic video + real at 40% → 95.7 > pi0 at 100%. Dynamic (visual-grounded) token init > static.
Failure/limitations: My read: huge VLAs only; real-robot trial counts not clearly reported in main text (except 50 trials for humanoid), and one "real" platform is simulated; gains at 100% data are small (+2); per-task real numbers mostly figure-only. Needs interaction-region masks (object-centric) — extra annotation pipeline.
Conflicts: Agrees with AR-WAM (this wave): latent/compressed future targets > pixel reconstruction. Agrees with FLARE / Fast-WAM line. Gains concentrate in low-data (10–40%) regime — matches the intuition that aux objectives act as regularizers when demos are scarce.
Relevance: Our 50–100 demos single-task regime is "low data" per task. SmolVLA row directly relevant: SmolVLA ≈ pi0 at every data scale on LIBERO (92.5/90.3/77.3) — so our SmolVLA failures are not due to the model being weak in-distribution; they are robustness failures. Future-latent contrastive aux (current vs t+10 frame embedding) is cheap to add to a small policy with a frozen encoder.
Decision impact:
 - Q09 aux objectives: supports latent object-centric future prediction + future-goal contrastive alignment, biggest at low data (+7.7 at 10%, +4.1 at 40%) — confidence M (sim ablations, large VLAs)
 - Q09 aux objectives: weakens pixel-level future prediction and IDM pseudo-actions from synthetic video — confidence M
 - Q13 data: SmolVLA/pi0 drop 92–95 → 77–78 going from ~43 to ~5 demos/task on LIBERO — confidence M
 - Q05 synthetic data: DreamGen synthetic videos help only via representation-level (implicit) loss, not pseudo-actions — confidence L
 - Q12 model size: SmolVLA (small) ≈ pi0/GR00T at all data scales on LIBERO — confidence M
