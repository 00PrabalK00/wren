# W4_UniVLALearningtoActAnywherewithTaskcentr — UniVLA: Learning to Act Anywhere with Task-centric Latent Actions (2025, RSS; arXiv id not in text)
Setup: Latent action model (VQ-VAE inverse dynamics on DINOv2 features, language-conditioned to split task-centric vs task-irrelevant dynamics, ~1 s horizon) pretrained on OpenX + GNM + Ego4D videos (no action labels); Prismatic-7B VLM predicts latent action tokens; 10.8M action decoder (visual-query attention) maps latents to action chunks (size 12 real); history latent actions as prompt. LIBERO (50 demos/task, 500 trials/suite), R2R nav, CALVIN, SimplerEnv. Real: AgileX Piper, single third-view RGB (Orbbec), 4 tasks, 20–80 demos/task, success rates consistent with ~15 trials/task; 10 Hz closed loop on RTX 4090.
Claim: Task-centric latent actions learned from action-free video make VLA pretraining cheaper and give better downstream success, data efficiency and real-world generalization.
Evidence: LIBERO avg: UniVLA (full pretrain) 95.2, Bridge-only 92.5, human-video-only pretrain beats OpenVLA by 12.2; OpenVLA 76.5, LAPA 65.7 (derived from stated margins). Real avg SR: UniVLA beats LAPA by 36.7 pts; OpenVLA 38.3% (stuttering from 0.18 s/step, 0.68 s per 4-chunk latency). Real generalization (Table II): lighting variation UniVLA 66.7% vs DP 20.0, OpenVLA 13.3, LAPA 26.7; overall generalization avg 68.9% / score 2.49 vs LAPA 28.9 / 1.36, OpenVLA 20.0 / 0.98; novel object (screwdriver → marker) −6.6 pts; distractors cause "notable drop" for semantic policies (numbers in table not extractable). Per-task: DP beats UniVLA on towel folding (53.3 vs 46.7); DP 6.7% vs UniVLA 86.7% on Tower of Hanoi (semantic).
Ablations: Task-centric vs task-irrelevant latents (Ego4D pretraining) LIBERO avg 88.7 vs 56.5 (Long 79.4 vs 0.2). Decoder: visual-query decoder 95.2 vs w/o visual 92.5 (Long 92.0 vs 86.0); vs autoregressive discrete decoding +42.1 on LIBERO-Long. History latent actions: Goal 95.0→95.6, Long 88.1→92.0, R2R 30.6→47.1. Data efficiency: 10% LIBERO-Goal data 86.3% > OpenVLA full 79.2%. Pretraining data scale: real score 2.05 (Bridge) → 2.35 (+OpenX) → 2.64 (+human) (figure values).
Failure/limitations / my read: 7B backbone (10 Hz on 4090, not an 8 GB laptop); real trials small (~15/task) and single camera; "lighting" robustness may come from DINOv2 features + big pretraining; DP is the only small baseline and was single-task. Per-condition distractor numbers not recoverable.
Conflicts: Supports latent-action/inverse-dynamics pretraining (agrees with TAP, AR-WAM gist). Chunked continuous decoding >> autoregressive discrete tokens (agrees with OpenVLA-OFT, weakens VQ/discrete tokens as action head). Latency-induced stutter for OpenVLA mirrors our SmolVLA chunk-boundary jerk issue.
Relevance: Moderate. Key transferable items: (1) DINOv2-feature-space inverse dynamics as a robust pretraining/aux objective; (2) compact history as past latent-action tokens (cheap, helps long horizon); (3) lighting robustness of DINO-feature models vs DP trained from scratch (66.7 vs 20.0). Full UniVLA is too big for our budget.
