# W4_GlobalPriorMeetsLocalConsistencyDualMemo — Global Prior Meets Local Consistency: Dual-Memory VLA (OptimusVLA) (2026, arXiv n/a in text)
Setup: π0.5-initialized VLA (3.6B total) + Global Prior Memory (GPM: retrieve k=8 similar trajectories from a memory bank via InfoNCE-trained retrieval token; form N(µ, Var) over action chunks; start flow matching from that prior with similarity-adaptive noise and adaptive NFE) + Local Consistency Memory (LCM: self-attention over previous action chunk + Mamba state → additive bias to the initial sample, trained to predict A*−µ). 8× A800, batch 512, 30k steps. Sim: LIBERO (500 rollouts/suite), CALVIN ABC→D, RoboTwin 2.0 Hard (16 tasks × 100 rollouts). Real: Galaxea R1 Lite bimanual 14-DoF, wrist + third-person cams 224x224; generalization tasks 100–150 demos, 50 rollouts each (25 lighting changes, 25 scene variations); long-horizon 200–300 demos.
Claim: starting flow sampling from a retrieved task prior cuts NFE ~3x and conditioning on the previous chunk improves temporal consistency and progress awareness.
Evidence: LIBERO avg 98.6 (π0.5 96.9), NFE 3.2 vs 10. CALVIN avg len 4.45 (π0.5 reproduced lower; π0 +13.5% relative). RoboTwin 2.0 Hard avg (8 shown tasks): DP 20%, DP3 2%, ACT 1%, others 11/25/29%, OptimusVLA 38%. Real generalization suite SR: OptimusVLA 85.0 vs π0.5 75.0 vs π0 59.5 (Fig 1); NFE 3.4 vs 10; stated 2.9x inference speedup over π0.5 (per-task bars figure-only).
Ablations (Table 4; LIBERO-Long / CALVIN len / real generalization): full 96.4 / 4.45 / 85.0; single-module removals 93.2 / 4.28 / 77.0 and 94.8 / 4.38 / 79.5 (checkmark mapping lost in extraction — one is w/o GPM, one w/o LCM); both removed (= π0.5) 92.4 / 4.26 / 75.0. GPM memory size (LIBERO-Long): 6500 traj k=8 96.4, k=16 94.8, k=1 92.6; 1300 traj k=8 95.2, k=1 92.4; 130 traj k=8 93.6.
Failure/limitations: memory bank = training trajectories (retrieval is essentially nearest-neighbor action prior → risk of copying in novel layouts; no OOD spatial test isolated); huge compute; real per-task trial counts moderate (50) but details figure-only; ablation mapping ambiguous.
Conflicts: RoboTwin Hard numbers again show small from-scratch policies (DP 20%, DP3 2%, ACT 1%) collapse under randomized lighting/texture/clutter — agrees with PriorVLA (DP 0%). Previous-chunk conditioning for smoothness parallels "bidirectional decoding"/RTC-style inpainting approaches that condition on the committed prefix; here it is a learned additive bias rather than inference-time guidance.
Relevance: two cheap ideas for our flow/diffusion head: (1) warm-start sampling from a data-driven prior (e.g., mean of nearest-neighbour demo chunks, or previous chunk shifted) to cut NFE — directly helps latency; (2) condition next chunk on previously executed chunk to reduce chunk-boundary jerk. Also reinforces that robustness to lighting/clutter is absent in small scratch policies without augmentation.
Decision impact:
 - Q10 latency/smoothness: retrieved-prior initialization reduces NFE 10 → 3.2–3.4 with equal/better SR; previous-chunk consistency bias improves smoothness/SR (ablation −1.6 to −3.3 pts LIBERO-Long) — confidence L-M (big model, mapping ambiguity).
 - Q07 history: conditioning on executed ACTION history (not images) helps progress awareness without copycat issues — confidence L.
 - Q01 action head: flow matching with informed prior rather than Gaussian noise — confidence L.
 - Q14 robustness: RoboTwin Hard DP 20%, DP3 2%, ACT 1% — from-scratch small policies fail under DR shifts — confidence M (100 rollouts × 8 tasks).
