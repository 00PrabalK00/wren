# W4_DreamGenUnlockingGeneralizationinRobotLe — DreamGen: Unlocking Generalization in Robot Learning through Video World Models (2025, arXiv 2505.12705)
Setup: 4-step pipeline: LoRA-finetune a video world model (WAN2.1 default; also Cosmos, CogVideoX, Hunyuan) on teleop videos → roll out from new initial frames + prompts → label pseudo-actions with IDM (SigLIP-2 + flow-matching DiT, 2 frames → action chunk) or LAPA latent actions → train policy (DP, pi0, GR00T N1) co-training 1:1 with real (state zeroed for neural trajectories). Sim: RoboCasa 24 tasks (GR00T N1), 240k neural trajs. Real: GR1 humanoid (4 tasks), Franka (3), LeRobot SO-100 (2: pick 3 strawberries, tic-tac-toe), 10-13 real trajectories per task ("low data"), 10 trials per SO-100 task. Compute: 240k RoboCasa videos = 54 h on 1500 L40 GPUs.
Claim: synthetic "neural trajectories" from a fine-tuned video model improve low-data policies and enable new behaviors/environments from one-task one-environment teleop data.
Evidence:
 - Real low-data co-training: GR1 avg 37→46.4%, Franka 23→37%, SO-100 (GR00T N1) 21→45.5%. SO-100 per task (Table 5 parse): pick&place 17→26, tic-tac-toe 25→65. For reference "High Data" (all 40/50 SO-100 demos) GR00T: 36 / 40 — neural-traj low-data beat real high-data on tic-tac-toe (flattened table; verify column alignment).
 - RoboCasa: log-linear gain with #neural trajectories in low (720), mid (2.4k), high (7.2k) GT regimes, IDM and LAPA similar (figure only). Neural-only training: 20.6% avg over 24 tasks.
 - Behavior generalization (GR1, 14 new verbs, 50 neural trajs each, neural only): 11.2 → 43.2%. Novel environments (13 tasks): 0.0 → 28.5%.
 - DreamGen Bench: fine-tuned models far above zero-shot (e.g. RoboCasa IF GPT: WAN-zero 0 → WAN-sft 77.1; Cosmos-sft 79.2); bench score correlates positively with RoboCasa neural-only success (figure).
Ablations: IDM vs latent actions — similar gains (figure). Zero state for neural trajectories "does not harm" (preliminary, no numbers). No ablation on number of real demos beyond low/high.
Failure/limitations: authors: huge compute, manual initial frames, simple tasks, VLM-based evaluator can hallucinate. Critical: 10 trials per SO-100 task with partial credit; baseline low-data (10 demos) is weak so large relative gains; zeroed proprio in co-training confounds (it acts like proprio dropout — authors themselves note policy "overfits less to proprioceptive states" and "gets stuck at home position" less).
Conflicts: Complements DCODA/DMD (generative augmentation helps low data). The proprio-overfitting observation agrees with works reporting copycat/proprio shortcut in low-data BC (e.g. proprio dropout helps). Unlike simple image augmentation, adds new motions, not just appearance.
Relevance: Medium-low for direct use (video-model fine-tuning infeasible on 8 GB laptop; could use cloud). Very relevant side-finding on SO-100: baseline GR00T fine-tunes on ~10 demos get stuck at home pose and over-rely on proprio → proprio dropout / zero-state augmentation is a cheap trick to test.
Decision impact:
 - Q05 augmentation: generative video synthetic demos double SO-100 low-data success (21→45.5% avg, 10-13 real demos) — confidence L-M (10 trials, partial credit, heavy compute).
 - Q07 history/proprio: mixing zero-state trajectories reduced over-reliance on proprio and stuck-at-home failures on SO-100 — supports proprio dropout — confidence L (qualitative).
 - Q13 data: 10-13 real demos + synthetic beats 40-50 real demos on one SO-100 task (65 vs 40) — diversity of states matters more than count — confidence L.
 - Q09 aux: IDM trained on robot data is a usable action labeller; LAPA latent actions work similarly — confidence L for our use.
