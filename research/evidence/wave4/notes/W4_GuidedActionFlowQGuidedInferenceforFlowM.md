# W4_GuidedActionFlowQGuidedInferenceforFlowM — Guided Action Flow: Q-Guided Inference for Flow-Matching Vision-Language-Action Policies (2026, arXiv 2607.02092)
Setup: Undisclosed real 7-joint arm + gripper; body cam + wrist cam; frozen SmolVLA (task-fine-tuned, dual camera) outputs 50-step normalized joint chunks at 15 Hz (≈3.33 s/chunk); critic = 3-layer transformer (d=256) on state + frozen SmolVLA visual tokens (128×960) + chunk, trained by offline IQL on 100 real rollouts of the frozen policy (47 success / 53 fail; 8,917 chunks), sparse terminal reward. Guidance: v ← v − clip(∇_â Q,1)/β, β=2. Task "put the water bottle into the box", roughly fixed placement; 40 episodes per condition, 180 s timeout.
Claim: A small offline critic trained on ~100 deployment rollouts can steer a frozen flow VLA at inference time, raising success without fine-tuning.
Evidence: frozen SmolVLA 19/40 (47.5%) vs +QGF 34/40 (85.0%); timeouts 13/40 → 3/40. Distractor (yellow tape measure): SmolVLA 0/11 vs QGF 6/12.
Ablations: β chosen from {0.5, 2, 4} in preliminary trials (no numbers). No other ablations.
Failure/limitations: My read: baseline and QGF episodes are sequential blocks (77–116 vs 118–157) — not interleaved, so drift/operator effects confounded; β tuned on the robot; single task, near-fixed object pose; distractor test tiny (n≈12) and uneven. Critic trained on rollouts from the same pose distribution — likely overfit to scene. Latency overhead of critic gradients per denoising step not reported.
Conflicts: Corroborates our own observation: frozen SmolVLA fails catastrophically under a simple visual distractor (0/11) — SmolVLA's in-distribution success is mediocre (47.5%) with many timeouts (hesitation). Consistent with "small offline RL / value guidance" line (IDQL, Q-guided flow).
Relevance: Direct SmolVLA evidence on a real arm with body + wrist cam: many failures are timeouts/hesitation rather than wrong motion. Offers an alternative to re-training: log our deployment rollouts with success labels and train a small critic to guide sampling. But it doesn't fix our lighting/camera-shift issue systematically and adds inference cost to an already 2 s latency.
Decision impact:
 - Q14 robustness: frozen SmolVLA 0/11 with one distractor object; critic guidance recovers 6/12 — confidence L (tiny n, non-interleaved)
 - Q13 data: 100 autonomous rollouts (success+failure) are a usable extra data source via offline RL critic (+37.5 pts) — confidence L-M (40 eps/condition, single task)
 - Q01 action head: flow-matching head enables inference-time value guidance (a plus for generative heads) — confidence L
