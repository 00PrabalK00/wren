# W4_ManiVID3DGeneralizableViewInvariantReinf — ManiVID-3D: Generalizable View-Invariant Reinforcement Learning for Robotic Manipulation (RA-L 2026)
Setup: 3D visual RL (point clouds in CAMERA frame, no extrinsics). ViewNet (supervised in sim) canonicalizes point clouds from arbitrary views into a unified frame; plus contrastive view-invariant / view-dependent disentanglement losses with β curriculum; 30% of samples get block occlusion augmentation. GPU batch rendering (5128 FPS on RTX 4090, 512 envs). Sim: 10 tasks (AIRBOT Play, UR5, Franka, Allegro), eval 50 episodes × 5 seeds under yaw ±60°, pitch ±7.5°, distance 0.9–1.1×. Real: AIRBOT Play + RealSense L515 depth, zero-shot sim-to-real, 5 tasks × 4 deviated viewpoints × 5 trials + appearance/instance/lighting/clutter tests (10 trials each).
Claim: calibration-free canonicalization of camera-frame point clouds gives strong viewpoint invariance with a small, fast network.
Evidence: sim avg SR under random views: ManiVID-3D 94.5 vs Maniwhere (RGB-D contrastive) 88.9, Maniwhere+3D 82.2, DexPoint (point cloud with extrinsics) 78.4, ReViWo 57.6, MV-MWM 55.9, SRM (aug) 12.1, MoVie 8.3. Viewpoint sweep: Maniwhere drop 3.9% at ±30° → 31.5% at ±75°, ManiVID-3D <6.7% variation. Real total 103/140 vs Maniwhere 79/140 (viewpoint: Reach 18 vs 16/20, Cube Lift 15 vs 11, Pick&Place 11 vs 8, Laptop 16 vs 13, Button 18 vs 14; appearance/instance/lighting/clutter each ~+1–3/10). Efficiency: −52.6% inference latency, −84.4% params, −69.8% FLOPs vs Maniwhere.
Ablations (Table IV avg of 3 tasks): full 92.9; w/o ViewNet 57.1; w/o invariance loss 87.9; w/o disentangle 89.5; fixed β 88.0–91.9; ViewNet replaced by self-supervised 3D-STN 78.1. IL comparison (Cube Lift, 100 multi-view demos): DP3 18.0 vs ManiVID-3D encoder trained with IL 78.4 (RL 92.0).
Failure/limitations: RL in sim with privileged ViewNet supervision; real trials small (5 per viewpoint); tasks simple (reach, lift, press); noise clusters in depth remain an issue.
Conflicts: supports 3D-input viewpoint robustness (with SPHINX, iDP3) but shows naive point clouds (DP3, even DexPoint with extrinsics) are NOT automatically view-invariant — canonicalization is what matters. DP3 at 18% under view changes contradicts claims that point-cloud policies are inherently robust to camera moves.
Relevance: our RealSense gives depth. If we use a scene point cloud, express it in the ROBOT BASE frame via extrinsics (or canonicalize) — a camera-frame point cloud policy would inherit camera-shift fragility. Re-calibrating extrinsics after a camera bump is our cheapest fix; learned canonicalization is heavy. Occlusion augmentation for point clouds is a cheap trick.
Decision impact:
 - Q04 3D/depth: point clouds help viewpoint robustness only when canonicalized (w/o ViewNet 92.9→57.1; DP3 18% under view change with 100 demos) — confidence M (sim-heavy, real 140 trials).
 - Q11 cameras: calibration-free view alignment lets one policy survive ±60° yaw; RGB-D contrastive baseline degrades 31.5% at ±75° — confidence L-M.
 - Q05 augmentation: plain data augmentation baseline (SRM) 12.1% under view randomization — image aug alone insufficient for large view shifts — confidence L (RL setting).
