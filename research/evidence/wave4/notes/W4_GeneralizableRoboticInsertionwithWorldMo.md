# W4_GeneralizableRoboticInsertionwithWorldMo — Generalizable Robotic Insertion with World Models (2025/26, arXiv id not in text)
Setup/Claim: Sim-only (Isaac/AutoMate assemblies) online model-based RL (TD-MPC2, 5M params, wrist depth 96x96 + proprio, delta EE actions); specialists 79.78% vs AutoMate 70.31% over 100 assemblies; 90-object generalist 55.0% seen / 56% zero-shot unseen vs AutoMate generalist 15.6%. Ablations (figure only, qualitative): blind (no vision) much worse; alternative wrist-camera placement with better plug view but more socket occlusion worse; curriculum helps; 100 expert demos/assembly help only marginally.
Why low relevance: online RL in simulation with millions of steps, no imitation from human demos, no real robot. Only transferable points: wrist-camera placement must avoid the grasped object occluding the target (Q11, L confidence), and vision is critical for precise placement. No ledger entries beyond Q11.
