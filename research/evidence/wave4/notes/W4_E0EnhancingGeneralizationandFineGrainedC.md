# W4_E0EnhancingGeneralizationandFineGrainedC — E0: Enhancing Generalization and Fine-Grained Control in VLA Models via Tweedie Discrete Diffusion (2025/26, arXiv 2511.21542)
Setup: π0-style backbone (PaliGemma/Gemma 2B VLM + 300M action expert). Actions quantile-discretized (1st–Mth percentile) into 2048 bins per dim; Gaussian noise added to (label-smoothed α=0.1) one-hot vectors, network predicts categorical logits (CE loss), 10 refinement steps with KV cache; chunk H=50. Spherical viewpoint augmentation: unproject to fixed depth, random yaw/pitch rotation, reproject + learnable relative camera-offset embedding. Fine-tune 30k steps, batch 32, 24 h on one RTX PRO 6000; inference on RTX 3090 server. Benchmarks: LIBERO, VLABench, ManiSkill (5 tasks). Real: Franka FR3, GELLO teleop, 8 tasks, 50 demos/short task, 80/long task, multi-task mixture, 20 trials/task.
Claim: discrete (Tweedie) diffusion over fine-grained action bins beats continuous flow (π0) and AR tokens (π0-FAST) in generalization/precision; spherical warping augmentation fixes camera-shift sensitivity.
Evidence:
 - LIBERO (Table 1): E0 97.2/99.4/95.0/92.2 (Spatial/Object/Goal/Long); π0 (retrained same setup) 96.8/98.8/95.8/85.2; π0.5 95.4/98.4/97.2/89.6; π0-FAST 96.4/96.8/88.6/60.2; DP 78.3/92.5/68.3/50.5.
 - Overall avg across LIBERO+VLABench+ManiSkill: E0 60.8 vs π0.5 52.2, π0 51.4, π0-FAST 50.1 (per table).
 - Real (Table 2, 20 trials; Pick/Press/Stack/Pull/Close/PickTwice/Open&Put/Put&Close): π0 60/60/40/30/45/45/55/10 avg 43.1; π0-FAST 20/20/5/10/15/10/0/0 avg 10.0; E0 75/70/50/35/35/60/40/20 avg reported 45.6 (the 8 listed values average 48.1 — table/extraction inconsistency). E0 is WORSE than π0 on Close (35 vs 45) and Open&Put (40 vs 55).
 - Camera perturbation on LIBERO (Table 3, avg): π0 19.7 → +view aug 50.8; E0 66.5 → +view aug 83.9 (Long: π0 2.6 → 16.2; E0 28.8 → 63.8).
Ablations (Fig. 6, figure only, qualitative + some legible numbers): bins — precision improves up to ~2048 then saturates/degrades; action horizon — 10–20 steps best, longer horizons lose reactivity; smoothing factor 0.1 +1.4%; quantile normalization > mean–std (stated).
Failure/limitations: real gain over π0 is small (+2.5 pts avg reported) and mixed per task; baseline π0-FAST suspiciously weak in real (10%); camera-perturbation eval is sim-only; 3B model, not laptop-friendly; VLABench Select Painting weak (semantic grounding).
Conflicts: Supports multimodality argument (vs L1 regression) but real evidence that discrete diffusion > continuous flow is weak. Camera augmentation result agrees with other viewpoint-robustness works (novel-view synthesis / random perspective aug) that fixed-camera-trained policies collapse under camera shift and that geometric view augmentation recovers much of it.
Relevance: Our "slightly moved camera" failure: spherical warp augmentation (homography-like rotation of the image about the camera center using intrinsics) is cheap, needs no extra data, and gave +22–31 pts avg under camera perturbations in sim for two different models. Easy to add to any policy using RealSense intrinsics. Action head recipe itself is VLA-scale, not directly applicable.
Decision impact:
 - Q14 robustness (camera shift): supports geometric view (spherical warp) augmentation: π0 19.7→50.8, E0 66.5→83.9 under camera perturbation — M (sim LIBERO only, but consistent across 2 models)
 - Q05 augmentation: add rotation-about-camera-center warp augmentation for scene cam — M
 - Q01 action head: discrete diffusion over 2048 bins ≥ continuous flow in sim (LIBERO-Long 92.2 vs 85.2), ~equal in real (45.6 vs 43.1) — L
 - Q06 chunking: horizon 10–20 steps best on LIBERO; longer reduces reactivity — L (figure only)
