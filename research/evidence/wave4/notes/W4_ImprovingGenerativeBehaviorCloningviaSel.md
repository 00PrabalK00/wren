# W4_ImprovingGenerativeBehaviorCloningviaSel — Improving Generative Behavior Cloning via Self-Guidance and Adaptive Chunking (2025, arXiv 2510.12392, NeurIPS 2025)
Setup: Inference-time only, no retraining. Diffusion Policy (obs history 2, H=16, DDIM-30 in sim; DDIM-10 real). Sim: Push-T, Robomimic Lift/Can/Square/Transport, Kitchen; 100 episodes x 3 seeds; "Stochastic" = temporally correlated disturbance (object moved at velocity P) with closed-loop h=1; "Static" = clean, open-loop h=8. Real: SO-100 arm via LeRobot DP, 3 webcams (bird's-eye, front, wrist) downsampled to 224x224, 30 Hz, ResNet-50 ImageNet backbone, 300 demos, pen-holder pick-and-place into cup, 20 trials per condition; trained 240K steps (27 h on RTX 6000 Ada). Also pi0 / OpenVLA-OFT on LIBERO-Spatial with moving targets.
Claim: (1) Self-Guidance (SG): CFG-style extrapolation using the model's own prediction conditioned on the PREVIOUS observation as the negative, eps = (1+w)eps(s_t) − w eps(s_{t-1}), sharpens the action distribution and anticipates future state; (2) Adaptive Chunking (AC): re-query every step but keep executing the old chunk while cos-sim(first queued action, first new action) ≥ τ, otherwise replace the chunk — closed-loop during precise phases, open-loop during transport.
Evidence:
 - Push-T under disturbance P=0/1/2/3 (Table 1): Vanilla 0.496/0.322/0.231/0.204; EMA (ACT temporal ensembling, λ=0.5) 0.456/0.306/0.229/0.204; BID 0.652/0.454/0.236/0.200; Ours 0.819/0.718/0.413/0.261.
 - 6 sim tasks average: Ours +23.25% over Vanilla DP, +12.27% over BID (DDIM-30); DDPM-100: +19.63% / +7.58% stochastic, only +1.90% / +1.23% in static. -> gains are mostly under disturbance/closed-loop; in clean open-loop setting it's ~2 pts.
 - BID costs 16x FLOPs and 2x latency; SG costs one batched pass with [s_t, s_{t-1}].
 - Guidance comparison on Push-T (Fig. 14 bar values): Vanilla 0.496, CFG 0.070, Noised-obs guidance 0.375, TSG 0.516, AutoGuidance 0.735, SG 0.873. CFG (unconditional negative) badly HURTS diffusion policies.
 - AC similarity metric (Table 4, Push-T/Kitchen/Lift/Square/Can/Transport): Vanilla 0.496/0.298/0.157/0.633/0.787/0.387; L1 0.621/0.309/0.227/0.583/0.687/0.25; L2 0.639/0.323/0.53/0.533/0.7/0.25; Cosine 0.72/0.398/0.587/0.753/0.91/0.553 (read from flattened table; column order as in header).
 - Real SO-100: main static/stochastic bars figure only, qualitative: Ours > Vanilla, especially when cup is moved. Additional real (cup moving on circular path, 20 episodes): Ours 70%; EMA lower than Vanilla; BID > Vanilla but < Ours (exact bars figure only). BID ran at 16 Hz -> halting motion; Ours at ~29 Hz, smooth.
 - VLA extension Table 2 (LIBERO-Spatial with moving target): extracted numbers appear duplicated between pi0 and OpenVLA rows (82.0/73.4/83.8 at P=1; 12.2/14.4/19.9 at P=5 for open-loop/closed-loop/ours) — treat as: ours best, closed-loop hurts at low noise; do not rely on per-model values.
Ablations: SG alone and AC alone each beat vanilla, combined best (Fig. 10, figure only). Optimal SG w increases with disturbance level (Fig. 3). EMA's optimal λ differs strongly per task (sensitive); AC's τ curve is flat (robust) — Fig. 6/12, figure only.
Failure/limitations: AC needs a model call every control step (same compute as closed-loop) — directly conflicts with our 2 s latency; would only be feasible with a fast small policy. Real-robot evidence is 20 trials, one task, bars not numerically reported. Disturbances are synthetic object motions, not lighting/camera shifts. Per-task tuned w in sim.
Conflicts: Temporal ensembling/EMA (ACT) often HURTS diffusion policies here (and on real SO-100), contradicting the ACT ablation where TE gave +3.3% for a CVAE policy — difference: stochastic sampler; averaging two different modes produces invalid in-between actions. Supports BID/RTC-style view that naive CL with diffusion jitters; supports hybrid "commit unless disagreement" execution.
Relevance: High for Q10/Q06 on our exact hardware class (SO-100 + LeRobot DP + wrist cam, 30 Hz). Practical recipes: (a) don't use temporal ensembling with a diffusion/flow head; (b) SG needs no retraining — just a 2-batch forward pass with previous observation — cheap to try in LeRobot DP/SmolVLA flow heads; (c) AC gives a principled chunk-switching rule to reduce boundary jerks, but requires per-step inference (needs <33 ms model at 30 Hz or ~100 ms at 10 Hz). Note 300 demos, not 50–100.
Decision impact:
 - Q10 latency/smoothness: supports similarity-gated adaptive chunk replacement + self-guidance; BID-style search too slow (16 Hz, halting) — confidence M (sim 6 tasks x 3 seeds + small real SO-100)
 - Q06 chunking/ensembling: weakens temporal ensembling/EMA for diffusion heads (Push-T 0.456 vs 0.496 vanilla; lower on real SO-100) — confidence M
 - Q01 action head: with diffusion heads, CFG-style unconditional guidance collapses (0.070 vs 0.496), but past-obs guidance helps (0.873) — confidence M (sim Push-T)
 - Q07 history: using previous obs as a negative condition gives implicit future-awareness (obs history 2 used) — confidence L
