# W4_TeachingTinyVLAModelsWheretoLookandHowto — Teaching Tiny VLA Models Where to Look and How to Move (XS-VLA) (2026, arXiv 2607.04171)
Setup: SmolVLA-0.25B (SmolVLM2-0.25B, 16 layers) + SmolVLA-style flow action expert. Stage 1 CSD: Qwen3-VL-4B predicts target-object image center (conf ≥0.5; 72% agreement with human judgement), quantized to 3x3 grid label, distilled into the VLM (vision encoder frozen) as constrained text prediction. Stage 2 LFM: CVAE latent z (from proprio + GT chunk, KL warm-up) conditioning the flow-matching decoder (Huber loss); z=0 at inference. LIBERO (SmolVLA LeRobot data, 160k steps, 10 episodes/task fixed seed). Real: Mobile ALOHA, 3 tasks, 100 demos each from 3 operators (multi-style), 20 trials/task, train+infer on one RTX 3090.
Claim: tiny VLAs lose spatial grounding and multimodal action organization; coarse spatial distillation + CVAE-latent flow matching restore them at no deployment cost.
Evidence:
 - LIBERO avg: SmolVLA 0.25B 82.8 (Long 63.0), 0.5B 87.3, 2.25B 88.8; XS-VLA 0.25B 90.3 (Spatial 93, Object 91, Goal 88, Long 89).
 - Real (20 trials each, 256M both): Cup placing 45% → 90%; Block stacking full 0% → 30% (score 3/40 → 20/40); Sequential coordination 20% → 75%; avg 21.7 → 65.0.
Ablations (LIBERO avg SR / steps): vanilla 82.8 / 174.5; +LFM only 87.4 / 173.9; image-only VLM pretrain + LFM 87.0 / 177.9; spatial pretrain w/o LFM 88.8; full 90.3 / 172.5. → CVAE latent on flow +4.6; spatial distillation +6.0 w/o LFM; spatial > generic image pretrain (+3.3 with LFM).
Failure/limitations: 3x3 grid only coarse 2D; depends on teacher VLM label quality in clutter; latent not interpretable. Critical: LIBERO evaluated with only 10 episodes/task fixed seed (low statistical power); real 20 trials; no robustness (lighting/camera) tests; multi-operator data is where CVAE latent is expected to help.
Conflicts: CVAE latent helping a flow head (+4.6) is notable — flow matching is already multimodal in principle; suggests at small scale the latent regularizes/"style-selects" (z=0 prior mean) similar to ACT's CVAE finding (human data needs latent). Contrasts with works that report flow alone suffices (pi0) — scale/data difference. Spatial grounding aux aligns with BFA++ (mask supervision) and OBEYED (explicit grounding).
Relevance: VERY HIGH — same model family as our current SmolVLA fine-tune. Cheap wins for us: (1) add a CVAE latent (ACT-style) to SmolVLA's flow expert, infer with z=0 — deterministic, smoother chunks under multi-style teleop; (2) pre-finetune the VLM backbone on auto-labelled coarse pumpkin/tray location (teacher VLM or even our own color/detector labels) before action training. Real gains were large on a 3090-class GPU (≈ our 8 GB budget, 256M params).
Decision impact:
 - Q01 action head: flow + CVAE latent (z=0 at test) +4.6 LIBERO avg; real multi-operator 21.7→65.0 (combined) — supports latent-conditioned flow for multimodal human demos — confidence M.
 - Q09 aux: coarse spatial (3x3 target location) distillation into VLM +6.0 over vanilla; better than image-only adaptation (88.8 vs ~87) — supports target-localization aux — confidence M.
 - Q12 model size: 0.25B XS-VLA (90.3) > SmolVLA 2.25B (88.8); tiny model fine with targeted structure — confidence M.
 - Q03 encoder/backbone: task-specific grounding pretraining of the VLM before action training helps more than generic in-domain image pretraining — confidence L-M.
