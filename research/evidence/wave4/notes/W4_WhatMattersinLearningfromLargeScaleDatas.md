# W4_WhatMattersinLearningfromLargeScaleDatas — What Matters in Learning from Large-Scale Datasets for Robot Manipulation (ICLR 2025, arXiv 2506.13536)
Setup: MimicLabs = procedurally generated sim (LIBERO/robosuite + MimicGen from ~500 human source demos → ~1M trajectories, 3K task instances, 8 scenes). Sim policies: BC-RNN / BC-Transformer / Diffusion Policy with ResNet18 @128x128, FiLM language; co-training = 10 target demos + 1000 co-training demos, 50/50 batch sampling (ω=0.5), 30 rollouts, peak checkpoint. Real: Franka + Robotiq, DROID-like mobile rig with external cams; Diffusion Policy (ResNet18 + spatial softmax, random crop, 128x128, To=2, Ta=8, Tp=16, DDIM), EE pose low-dim; 20 rollouts; 7 tasks; 20–50 target demos per task.
Claim: when co-training with other data, camera pose and spatial arrangement are the dimensions of variation (DVs) that must be diverse/aligned; object texture barely matters; retrieving aligned subsets beats using a whole large dataset (all-DROID co-training → 0%).
Evidence:
 - Sim collector, clear-table task (Table 1, rows = DV misaligned between target and co-train; target-only / low-var baseline cotrain / cotrain diverse in camPose): camPose misaligned 16.67 / 43.33 / 90; objTex misaligned 30 / 93.33 / 96.67; tableTex 43.33 / 66.67 / 80; objSpat misaligned 10 / 26.67 / 46.67 (diverse objSpat cotrain: 56.67 best). Diverse camera poses also rescue texture misalignment.
 - Sim retriever (Table 2, 10 target + 1000 cotrain): microwave mug target-only 6.67 → full-variation 60 → aligned camPose 70 → misaligned camPose 46.67/53.33. Object texture aligned vs misaligned ≈ same.
 - MimicLabs retrieval (Table 3): bin carrot target-only 50 → retrieved obj/skill 70 → +camPose 96.67; retrieving skill-relevant ~10k demos beats unfiltered ~100k.
 - Real DROID retrieval (Fig 5; target-only / all-DROID / obj-skill / +camPose / +objTex / +objSpat): serve snack 5/0/65/70/35/85; bin can 60/0/15/85/65/85; pour 50/0/35/75/60/65; wipe board 55/0/45/55/55/65; baking 40/0/40/55/40/35; put marker (50 demos) 30/0/20/35/15/20. All-DROID co-training = 0% on every task ("erratic movement, not precise").
 - Real collector (Table 13 text): aligned camera pose in co-train +25–50%; misaligned-texture co-training still +40–65%; bin can 30% (small spatial reset distribution) → 60% (large).
Ablations: DV-by-DV diversity vs alignment (above); motion-primitive diversity (Table 10): single misaligned primitive can hurt (−20% easy clear-table), full-coverage mixture best (83.33 vs 63.33 target-only on easy variant); sampling ratio ω 0.3/0.5/0.7 in Fig 13 (figure only).
Failure/limitations: mostly sim with textures only (no lighting/material variation) — "object texture doesn't matter" is under unchanged lighting and fixed geometry, and is about CO-TRAINING data, not about test-time appearance shift. Peak-checkpoint reporting in sim. Real: 20 rollouts, single lab. Co-training regime (10 target demos + big corpus) differs from our single-task 50–100 demos.
Conflicts: agrees with Gao et al./"Data Scaling Laws" line that spatial/viewpoint coverage dominates; conflicts with the common assumption that "more diverse pretraining data always helps" (all-DROID = 0%). Texture-insensitivity agrees with papers that find color jitter matters less than viewpoint/spatial coverage, but contrasts with our observed failure on a different-looking pumpkin — their target demos always include the test texture.
Relevance: our fixed RealSense scene cam: the camera-shift failure we see matches their finding that camera pose misalignment is the most damaging visual DV; if we can't hold the camera fixed, collecting demos with a few camera poses (or view augmentation) is the high-value diversity. Spatial coverage of pumpkin/tray placements in our 50–100 demos directly drives success (bin can 30→60%). If we co-train with public SO-100/101 community data, retrieve only camera-pose/skill-aligned episodes rather than dump everything in.
Decision impact:
 - Q13 data diversity: spatial-arrangement coverage > count; aligned/retrieved subsets > whole large dataset (all-DROID 0%) — confidence M (sim large-scale + real 7 tasks × 20 rollouts).
 - Q11 cameras/viewpoint: camera pose is the most harmful misaligned DV; camera-pose diversity in training improves transfer (sim 43→90) — confidence M.
 - Q14 robustness: texture variation in co-training data barely matters (sim, fixed lighting) while viewpoint/spatial do — confidence L-M (no lighting tested).
 - Q05 augmentation: implies viewpoint diversity is worth more than color/texture diversity — confidence L (indirect, from data composition not augmentation).
