# W4_HumanEgoZeroShotRobotLearningfromMinutes — HumanEgo: Zero-Shot Robot Learning from Minutes of Human Egocentric Videos (2026, arXiv id not in text; Univ. Maryland)
Setup: Human demos with Aria glasses at 30 Hz (~40 s each), 30 min per task (≈45 demos); arm inpainted (SAM2+LaMa), virtual gripper + object keypoints rendered; Interaction-Centric Tokens (ICT, 29-D per entity: entity pose in fixed ref frame + both hands' poses in entity frame + grasp); flow-matching transformer decoder (Euler) + 3 aux losses (object future 6-DoF motion, 2D traces, latent ICT K steps ahead). Deploy: bimanual Trossen WidowX, top RealSense D405, object detection/segmentation/orientation once per episode, grasp latching, depth back-projection; ~10 Hz synchronous on RTX 4090. 4 real tasks, 40 trials each.
Claim: explicit entity-level hand–object relative-pose representation + dense aux objectives lets a small flow policy learn from minutes of human video and transfer zero-shot, beating teleop-trained ACT.
Evidence:
 - Avg success 4 tasks: HumanEgo 30 min 92.5%, 15 min 75.0%; ACT on 30 min robot teleop 51.2%; 5 human-video baselines 1.9–45.0%. Downstack Cups 87.5% (others ≤45), Water Flowers 95% (best baseline 45).
 - Data scaling (Serve Bread): ~7 min → 50%, 30 min → 95%. ACT 30 min teleop 52.5%. SAME HumanEgo architecture on 30 min robot teleop → 65% vs 95% on human video (+30 pp) — authors attribute to diversity: human data higher SNR, smoother, less idle, wider spread of positions/trajectories per minute.
 - Zero-shot OOD (background, lighting, viewpoint, distractors, novel object instances, other robots Franka/UR10): 85–91.25%, "no measurable degradation in most cases" (figure).
Ablations:
 - Representation (Water Flowers, 30 min): raw human RGB only 7.5%; + inpaint/keypoint render 20%; robot RGB (no visual gap) 32.5%; raw RGB + ICT 85%; ICT only (no image) 75%; full 95%. → visual-only policies plateau ≈30% regardless of image fidelity; explicit 3D relative object pose is the key.
 - Aux (15 min): none baseline; +object motion +17.5 pp; +2D trace +5; +latent consistency +12.5; all three +25 pp. Across data (Serve Bread): aux helps 2–12 min (8 min: 57.5 vs 37.5), no difference ≥18 min (both 95% at 30).
Failure/limitations: needs Aria stereo hand tracking (monocular substitutes drop sharply); objects posed once per episode + rigid latching (no slip/re-grasp); parallel-jaw only; perception failures cascade; precision plateau ~1 cm. Critical: comparison vs ACT uses matched collection TIME not demo count (teleop slower: 60 s/demo); ACT baseline is RGB-only, while HumanEgo gets explicit object poses from a heavy perception frontend — so part of the gap is "state-based with object pose vs pixels".
Conflicts: Supports object-centric/3D-structured inputs (agrees with OBEYED-VLA, 3D policies). Aux-loss gains vanishing with more data mirror TRI co-training study (latent action benefits vanish with more robot data). "Diversity > count" consistent with data-diversity studies.
Relevance: Medium-high conceptually. For a single pick-place with RealSense depth, an explicit pumpkin/tray pose (detected + back-projected via depth) fed as tokens alongside images could give strong robustness to lighting/appearance/camera shift (entity-relative gripper pose is viewpoint-invariant). Aux object-motion prediction is cheap and helps most in our 50-demo regime. Also: human video as a cheap data source for pumpkin pick-place is plausible but requires Aria-level hand tracking.
Decision impact:
 - Q04 3D: explicit 3D entity-relative pose tokens (from depth) raise success 7.5→85% over RGB alone; RGB-only plateaus ~32.5% even with no domain gap — confidence M-H (40 trials, one task ablation).
 - Q09 aux: future object motion (+17.5), latent future state (+12.5), 2D traces (+5), all +25 pp at low data; no gain at ≥18 min data — confidence M.
 - Q14 robustness: 85–91% under lighting/background/viewpoint/distractor/new instance shifts with object-centric representation — confidence M.
 - Q13 data: data diversity per minute (positions, trajectories) matters more than count; same policy 65% (teleop) vs 95% (diverse human) — confidence M.
 - Q01 action head: flow matching small transformer works from ~45 demos — confidence L.
