# W4_VisualSimtoRealLearningforRoboticInserti — Visual Sim-to-Real Learning for Robotic Insertion under Geometric Variations: Application to Rebar Installation (RebarSim) (2026, arXiv 2609.20477)
Setup: Isaac Lab; PPO privileged state teacher (MLP) over procedurally generated rebar geometries (9 nominal designs × 20 tolerance variants), reset-state bank; distilled by DAgger/BC mixture (β=0.5) into RGB student: 8 views (6 close-ups + 2 wide, 150x150 crops/homography-warped from 2 ZED Mini cams), one ImageNet ResNet-18 shared + per-view embedding, 4-step proprio history, MLP head; action = Cartesian delta pose @10 Hz → impedance controller. Franka FR3. 1.4 mm clearance insertion. 4× L40 GPUs. Real: 150 rollouts on factory rebars (2 designs), 30 rollouts background test. No real demos at all.
Claim: an end-to-end visual student trained only in sim with appearance randomization + DAgger transfers zero-shot to a real tight-clearance insertion across geometric variation.
Evidence: sim Dtrain 96.1, unseen-interp 96.6, extrap 77.6; real 91.3% (137/150; D1 68/75, D2 69/75) vs 95.4/96.8 in sim. Background change (retextured, cluttered, marble): 25/30 vs 6/6 clean lab.
Ablations (Table III, sim, Dtrain): full 96.1; w/o appearance randomization 2.8 (while 91.1 on its own fixed appearance); w/o viewpoint randomization (±1 cm, ±3°) 90.2; pure BC instead of DAgger 68.9 (−27.2). Geometry diversity (student on D1): neither 79.4 → ε only 89.8 → design d only 95.9 → both 96.6; teacher zero-shot on extrapolated designs 55.4 (single design) vs 92.6 (9 designs) vs 0 from scratch; pretrained student needs 4.5–6.2× fewer distillation samples.
Failure/limitations: grasp assumed done; fixed crops/viewpoints designed per slot group; ~20% insertions need emergent back-out-and-retry; some failed recoveries due to sim contact mismatch; gravity load compensated manually. Needs a simulator + asset generator + teacher RL — heavy infrastructure. Viewpoint randomization tiny (±1 cm).
Conflicts: appearance randomization's importance (2.8 vs 96.1 under randomized eval) matches RoboSplat/other augmentation papers; DAgger > BC matches covariate-shift theory and supports recovery data in IL. Object-diversity-helps-single-object mirrors "What Matters"/grasping results.
Relevance: not our pipeline (no teleop demos, no SO-101 sim), but lessons transfer: (1) photometric/appearance randomization is what makes pixel policies survive lighting/background change; (2) on-policy correction data (DAgger/intervention) is worth ~27 pts over pure BC — for us, collecting recovery/intervention demos; (3) small ResNet-18 multi-view student with 4-step proprio history works at 10 Hz; (4) training on object-shape diversity improves even the original object (relevant to "different-looking pumpkin" → collect with several pumpkins).
Decision impact:
 - Q05 augmentation: appearance randomization indispensable (2.8 → 96.1 under randomized appearance); small viewpoint randomization +5.9 — confidence M (sim ablation, real transfer validated).
 - Q13 data quality: DAgger/on-policy corrections vs pure BC +27.2 pts; object-geometry diversity 79.4 → 96.6 even on seen object — confidence M.
 - Q14 robustness: real background/clutter change 25/30 (only 30 trials) with heavy appearance DR — confidence L.
 - Q12 model size: ImageNet ResNet-18 + MLP suffices for 1.4 mm insertion from 8 views — confidence L.
