# A2C2 — Leave No Observation Behind: Real-time Correction for VLA Action Chunks (Sendai et al., U. Tokyo, Sep 2025, arXiv 2509.23224)
Setup: base chunk policy FROZEN; a small residual "correction head" runs EVERY control step with the latest observation, the base action a_{t+k}, a sinusoidal time-in-chunk feature τ_k, base-policy latent, and outputs Δa added to the base action. Kinetix: 12 tasks, MLP head 0.31M params, 2048 rollouts/point, vs naive async and RTC. LIBERO-Spatial: SmolVLA-450M base (fine-tuned), correction head = 6-layer transformer + ImageNet ResNet-18 (32M params), 10 rollouts × 10 tasks (Table 7) / 50 per task (Table 1). Sim only.
Evidence:
 - Kinetix (Table 4, Naive / RTC / A2C2): d=0,e=1: 90.8/90.9/90.3; d=0,e=8: 86.0/86.0/87.8; d=1,e=1: 78.1/88.2/89.5; d=1,e=5: 78.5/83.1/87.9. Text: at d=4 A2C2 ~35 pts above naive; remains >85% at execution horizon 7.
 - LIBERO-Spatial (Table 7, naive → A2C2): e=40, d=10: 0.67→0.84; d=5: 0.66→0.86; d=3: 0.65→0.86; d=1: 0.74→0.83. e=10, d=10: 0.75→0.88; d=1: 0.83→0.92. d=0 horizon sweep e=50/40/30/10/5/1: naive 0.71/0.79/0.79/0.85/0.83/0.77 → A2C2 0.84/0.89/0.89/0.87/0.85/0.84.
 - Latency (RTX 5080 LAPTOP GPU, 16 GB): SmolVLA 101 ms per inference; correction head 4.7 ms per step (~20×).
 - Training: Kinetix head ~20 min/task on A6000; LIBERO head 200k steps ≈ 4 h on H200 (dataset built by running SmolVLA on the demos).
Ablations: none on head components reported in main text.
Failure/limitations: sim only; LIBERO only vs naive (no RTC on LIBERO); head must be trained per setup from base-policy rollouts on demo data; residual can inject noise at low delay (AsyncInferenceStudy found IT-RTC slightly better at d≤2). Critical read: 10 rollouts/task per LIBERO cell → ±~3–5 pts noise.
Conflicts: AsyncInferenceStudy independently confirms A2C2 leads at high delay on both Kinetix and SmolVLA-LIBERO, but costs 1.75× FLOPs per chunk when s=50 residual calls. Shows even at d=0, executing long chunks (e=50) hurts SmolVLA (0.71 vs 0.85 at e=10) — consistent with SmolVLA's own n_exec ablation.
Relevance: high. Key data point: SmolVLA runs at ~101 ms on a laptop RTX 5080 — our ~2 s on an RTX 5060 laptop is almost certainly a deployment/power-mode problem, not model cost. A small per-step residual head (camera + state + base action + chunk index) restores closed-loop reactivity; it runs at 200 Hz-class rates on a laptop GPU.
Decision impact:
 - Q10 async: per-step residual correction over a frozen chunk policy, +17 pts at d=10 (LIBERO-Spatial) — M (sim, one base model, independently replicated).
 - Q10 latency: SmolVLA ≈ 101 ms/inference on a laptop RTX 5080 — H (measurement).
 - Q06 execution horizon: long open-loop execution (e=50 of 50) costs ~14 pts at d=0 for SmolVLA — M.
